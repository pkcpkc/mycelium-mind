## Definition
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a subfield of Artificial Intelligence and Linguistics focused on the interaction between computers and human language. It encompasses the development of technologies that allow machines to understand, interpret, generate, and manipulate human language in a way that is both meaningful and contextually relevant.

## Core Components & Related Concepts
- [[Deep Learning]]: The foundational architecture enabling modern NLP through neural networks.
- [[LLMs]]: Large Language Models represent the current state-of-the-art in generative NLP.
- [[Deep Neural Networks]]: The structural backbone used for complex pattern recognition in text and speech.
- [[Reinforcement Learning]]: Often used in RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) to align model outputs.
- [[Synthetic Data Generation]]: Increasingly used to train and fine-tune models when organic data is scarce.

## Industry Applications & Research
Modern NLP research, as highlighted by figures like [[Andrej Karpathy]], involves the full stack of AI development:
- **Training & Midtraining**: The process of refining models on specific datasets to improve performance.
- **Autograd Engines**: Fundamental tools (e.g., `micrograd`) used to build the backpropagation systems that power NLP training.
- **Software 2.0**: The paradigm shift where models are trained as programs, a core tenet in modern NLP deployment.

## Key Figures & Influence
- **Andrej Karpathy**: A leading educator and engineer who has simplified complex NLP concepts through "Zero to Hero" content and practical implementations of building GPT models from scratch.
- **Academic Foundations**: Significant contributions have been made by researchers such as [[Fei-Fei Li]], [[Daphne Koller]], and [[Andrew Ng]].

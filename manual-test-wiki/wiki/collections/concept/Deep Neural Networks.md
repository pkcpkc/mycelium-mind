# Deep Neural Networks

Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) are the foundational architecture of modern artificial intelligence, enabling the modeling of complex, non-linear relationships within large datasets. They are characterized by multiple layers of interconnected "neurons" that automatically learn hierarchical representations of data.

## Key Architectures & Sub-fields
- [[Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)]]: Essential for spatial data processing and [[Computer Vision]].
- [[Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)]]: Designed for sequential data and time-series analysis.
- [[Large Language Models (LLMs)]]: Massive-scale neural networks trained on vast corpora of text.
- [[Reinforcement Learning]]: Training agents to make decisions through environmental feedback.

## Industry Applications & Research
- **Autonomous Systems:** Utilized extensively in [[Tesla]]'s Autopilot and robotics for real-time perception and path planning.
- **Synthetic Data Generation:** Creating artificial datasets to train models where real-world data is scarce or biased.
- **Software 2.0:** The paradigm shift where neural networks represent programs defined by data rather than explicit code.

## Notable Contributors & Influence
The advancement of Deep Neural Networks has been driven by key figures and institutions:
- **Andrej Karpathy:** A leading researcher and educator who has bridged the gap between theory and practice through projects like `micrograd` and large-scale deployment at OpenAI and Tesla.
- **Academic Foundations:** Significant research led by figures such as [[Fei-Fei Li]], [[Daphne Koller]], and [[Andrew Ng]] at institutions like Stanford University.
- **Corporate Innovation:** Major breakthroughs facilitated by teams at [[OpenAI]], Google Brain, and Tesla.

## Educational Frameworks
Deep Neural Networks are often taught through "Zero to Hero" pedagogical approaches, focusing on:
- Building autograd engines from scratch.
- Understanding visual-semantic alignments.
- Implementing foundational papers on image captioning and video classification.

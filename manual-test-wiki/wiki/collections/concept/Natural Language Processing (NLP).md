## Overview
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a subfield of artificial intelligence focused on the interaction between computers and human language. It encompasses the development of technologies that allow machines to understand, interpret, generate, and manipulate human language in a way that is both meaningful and contextually relevant.

## Key Concepts & Technologies
- [[Large Language Models (LLMs)]]: Modern state-of-the-art models used for complex linguistic tasks.
- [[Deep Neural Networks]]: The foundational architecture for modern NLP breakthroughs.
- [[Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)]]: Historically significant architectures for sequential data processing.
- [[Synthetic Data Generation]]: Increasingly used to train and refine language models.
- [[Autodiff]]: The underlying mechanism for training neural networks used in NLP.

## Research & Industry Connections
- **Key Figures**: 
  - [[Andrej Karpathy]]: A prominent researcher who has contributed significantly to the bridge between Computer Vision and NLP, specifically in connecting images and natural language.
  - **Collaborators**: Research in this space often involves collaborations with figures like [[Andrew Ng]] and various researchers at [[OpenAI]] and [[DeepMind]].
- **Industrial Applications**: 
  - Significant advancements in NLP are driven by large-scale industrial applications, such as those explored during Karpathy's tenure at [[Tesla]] and his work with [[OpenAI]] on midtraining and synthetic data.
- **Education**: 
  - Foundational NLP concepts are widely disseminated through academic courses (e.g., Stanford's CS 231n) and technical educational series like "Let's build GPT."

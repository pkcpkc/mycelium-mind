# Deep Learning

## Overview
[[Deep Learning]] is a subfield of machine learning characterized by the use of multi-layered artificial neural networks to model complex patterns in data. It serves as the foundational technology for modern advancements in [[Computer Vision]], natural language processing, and autonomous systems.

## Core Technical Components
- **Neural Architectures:** Includes [[Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)]], which are pivotal for image recognition, and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs).
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Modern deep learning applications that leverage massive datasets and transformer architectures to generate and understand human-like text.
- **Synthetic Data Generation:** A growing methodology in deep learning where artificial data is created to train models, overcoming real-world data scarcity or privacy constraints.
- **Software 2.0:** A paradigm shift where software is expressed as optimized weights in a neural network rather than explicit human-written code.

## Key Figures and Influence
The advancement of Deep Learning has been significantly influenced by researchers and practitioners such as:
- **Andrej Karpathy:** A prominent researcher known for work on CNNs, LLMs, and the practical application of deep learning at scale (Tesla, OpenAI).
- **Fei-Fei Li:** A pioneer in computer vision and academic mentorship at the Stanford Vision Lab.
- **Andrew Ng:** A foundational figure in deep learning education and research.

## Applications
- **Autonomous Vehicles:** Utilizing deep learning for real-time perception and path planning (e.g., Tesla Autopilot).
- **Generative AI:** Powering the creation of text, images, and video through large-scale model training.
- **Robotics:** Integrating reinforcement learning with deep neural networks for complex physical interactions.

# Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)

## Definition
Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are a class of deep neural networks most commonly applied to analyzing visual imagery. They are characterized by the use of "convolution" layers, which allow the network to automatically and adaptively learn spatial hierarchies of features, from low-level edges and textures to high-level objects and scenes.

## Context & Synthesis
CNNs represent a foundational pillar of [[Computer Vision]] and [[Deep Learning]]. They are the primary architecture that enabled significant breakthroughs in image recognition, object detection, and video analysis.

## Key Associations & Research
- **Academic Foundations:** Significant research into CNNs and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) was conducted at the Stanford Vision Lab.
- **Key Figures:** 
    - [[Andrej Karpathy]] (PhD research focused on CNNs/RNNs and connecting images to natural language).
    - [[Fei-Fei Li]] (Academic mentor and Stanford Vision Lab leader).
- **Applications:**

    - **Education:** Core component of the [[Stanford CS 231n]] curriculum.

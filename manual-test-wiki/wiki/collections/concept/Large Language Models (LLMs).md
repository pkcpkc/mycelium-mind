## Overview
Large Language Models (LLMs) are advanced artificial intelligence systems trained on massive datasets to understand, generate, and manipulate human language. They represent a core component of modern generative AI and are foundational to the work of researchers like [[Andrej Karpathy]].

## Key Technical Foundations
- **Deep Learning:** LLMs are built upon deep neural network architectures, specifically leveraging the advancements in [[Deep Neural Networks]].
- **Architectures:** Historically influenced by [[Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)]], modern LLMs primarily utilize the Transformer architecture to handle long-range dependencies in text.
- **Training Paradigms:** The development of LLMs involves complex training cycles, often incorporating [[Reinforcement Learning]] for alignment and fine-tuning.

## Industry Context & Research
- **Software 2.0:** LLMs are a primary driver of the transition toward "Software 2.0," where models are trained on data rather than explicitly programmed with logic.
- **Synthetic Data Generation:** As high-quality human-generated data becomes scarce, the use of LLMs to generate [[Synthetic Data Generation]] for training other models is becoming a critical research frontier.
- **Multimodal Integration:** Research continues into the intersection of LLMs and [[Computer Vision]], enabling models to process both visual and textual inputs simultaneously.

## Notable Figures & Contributions
- **Andrej Karpathy:** A leading figure in the field who has contributed to the pedagogy and practical implementation of LLMs, notably through his "Zero to Hero" series and work on foundational deep learning concepts.
- **Research Origins:** Influenced by the academic foundations of researchers like [[Fei-Fei Li]], [[Andrew Ng]], and the engineering excellence at organizations like [[OpenAI]] and [[Tesla]].

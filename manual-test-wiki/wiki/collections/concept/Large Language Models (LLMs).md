description: >
  Large Language Models (LLMs) are a class of deep learning models trained on massive datasets to understand, generate, and manipulate human language. They serve as the foundation for modern generative AI, enabling tasks ranging from creative writing and coding to complex reasoning and autonomous agent behavior.
key_details:
  - **Foundational Architecture:** Built primarily on the Transformer architecture, leveraging self-attention mechanisms to process long-range dependencies in text.
  - **Training Paradigms:** Often utilize pre-training on vast corpora followed by instruction fine-tuning and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF).
  - **Synthetic Data:** Increasingly utilized in training loops to improve model reasoning and diversity where human-generated data is scarce.
  - **Software 2.0:** LLMs represent a shift toward "Software 2.0," where models are trained on data rather than explicitly programmed with logic.
  - **Key Figures & Contributors:** Significant contributions and educational leadership provided by [[Andrej Karpathy]], who has popularized the technical breakdown of LLM training and "Zero to Hero" educational tracks.
  - **Applications:** Powering chatbots, automated coding assistants, synthetic data generation for other modalities (like Computer Vision), and complex reasoning tasks.

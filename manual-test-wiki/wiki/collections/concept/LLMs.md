## Overview
Large Language Models (LLMs) represent a class of artificial intelligence models trained on massive datasets to understand, generate, and manipulate human language. They are a cornerstone of modern [[Natural Language Processing]] and are central to the shift toward "Software 2.0," where neural networks replace traditional hand-coded logic.

## Technical Context and Evolution
LLMs are built upon foundational architectures such as [[Deep Neural Networks]], specifically leveraging advancements in:
- **Transformer Architectures:** The backbone for modern LLM scaling.
- **Autograd Engines:** Essential for backpropagation, exemplified by educational projects like `micrograd`.
- **Synthetic Data Generation:** Used to augment training sets and refine model capabilities.

## Key Contributors and Influence
The development and popularization of LLMs have been significantly influenced by key figures and institutions:
- **Andrej Karpathy:** A prominent educator and researcher who has provided extensive technical guidance on LLM training recipes, "Zero to Hero" technical tracks, and practical implementation guides.
- **OpenAI:** A founding organization that has spearheaded large-scale LLM research and deployment.
- **Academic Foundations:** Research at institutions like Stanford University has bridged the gap between Computer Vision and NLP, providing the multimodal foundations for modern models.

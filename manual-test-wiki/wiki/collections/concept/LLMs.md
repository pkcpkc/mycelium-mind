## Overview
Large Language Models (LLMs) represent a frontier in [[Natural Language Processing]] and [[Deep Learning]], characterized by their ability to understand, generate, and manipulate human-like text at scale. They are a core component of the "Software 2.0" paradigm, where models are trained on data rather than explicitly programmed with rules.

## Technical Foundations
- **Architecture**: Built upon [[Deep Neural Networks]], specifically utilizing Transformer architectures to process sequential data.
- **Training Paradigms**: Involves large-scale pre-training, [[Midtraining]], and fine-tuning techniques.
- **Data Engineering**: Modern LLM development relies heavily on [[Synthetic Data Generation]] to augment training sets and improve model reasoning.
- **Core Technologies**: Development often involves custom [[Autograd Engines]] (e.g., `micrograd`) and high-performance computing infrastructures.

## Key Figures and Influence
The advancement of LLMs is closely tied to the work of prominent researchers and educators:
- **Andrej Karpathy**: A leading figure in the field who has contributed to the education and deployment of LLMs, notably through his "Zero to Hero" series and work at [[OpenAI]].
- **Research Institutions**: Significant contributions from the [[Stanford Vision Lab]] and research hubs at [[Tesla]] and [[Google Brain]].

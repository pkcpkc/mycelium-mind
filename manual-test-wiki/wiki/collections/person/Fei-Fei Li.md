# Fei-Fei Li

## Affiliations & Roles

- Professor at [[Stanford University]]
- Director of the Stanford Vision Lab

## Biography & Context

Fei-Fei Li is a prominent figure in the field of artificial intelligence, particularly recognized for her contributions to computer vision and deep learning. She served as a primary advisor for several high-profile researchers in the field, including [[Andrej Karpathy]], who completed his PhD under her supervision at the Stanford Vision Lab. Her work has been instrumental in shaping the academic and industrial landscape of modern AI, bridging the gap between theoretical research and practical applications in computer vision.

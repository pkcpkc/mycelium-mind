# Andrej Karpathy

## Affiliations & Roles

- Director of AI at [[Tesla]]
- Founding Member at [[OpenAI]]
- Research Scientist at [[Google Brain]]
- Instructor/Course Designer at [[Stanford University]]

## Biography & Context

Andrej Karpathy is a leading figure in the field of artificial intelligence, recognized for his work in "Software 2.0" and the development of deep neural networks. He established a strong academic foundation with a BSc from the University of Toronto, an MSc from the University of British Columbia, and a PhD from Stanford University where he focused on Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs).

During his career, Karpathy has bridged the gap between academic research and industrial application. At Tesla, he served as the Director of AI, leading the Autopilot computer vision team where he oversaw data labeling, network training, and custom chip deployment. He was also a founding member of OpenAI in 2015 and has recently returned to the organization to focus on midtraining and synthetic data generation.

As an educator, Karpathy is highly influential; he designed Stanford's flagship CS 231n course and maintains a popular public presence through his "Zero to Hero" educational content. His technical contributions include projects like `micrograd`, `char-rnn`, and `arxiv-sanity`, as well as influential thought leadership on the effectiveness of neural networks.

# Andrej Karpathy

## Affiliations & Roles

- Director of AI at [[Tesla]]
- Founding Member and Lead at [[OpenAI]]
- Instructor for [[Stanford University]] CS 231n

## Biography & Context

Andrej Karpathy is a leading figure in the field of artificial intelligence, recognized for his ability to bridge the gap between complex research and practical engineering. He established a strong academic foundation with a BSc from the University of Toronto, an MSc from the University of British Columbia in Robotics/ML, and a PhD from the Stanford Vision Lab.

Throughout his career, Karpathy has held pivotal roles in both academia and industry. At Tesla, he served as the Director of AI, where he led the computer vision teams responsible for Autopilot and the Optimus robot. He later returned to OpenAI, where he has focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation. 

Karpathy is widely celebrated for his educational contributions, specifically his "Zero to Hero" series and his role in scaling Stanford’s CS 231n course. His technical repertoire is extensive, spanning the development of autograd engines like `micrograd`, research on PixelCNN++, and the creation of various open-source tools to democratize AI knowledge.

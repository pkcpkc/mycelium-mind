# Andrej Karpathy

## Affiliations & Roles

- Director of AI at [[Tesla]] (Autopilot/Optimus)
- Founding Member and Researcher at [[OpenAI]]
- Instructor at [[Stanford University]] (CS 231n)

## Biography & Context

Andrej Karpathy is a leading figure in the artificial intelligence landscape, recognized for bridging the gap between complex neural network theory and practical industrial implementation. He began his academic journey by obtaining a BSc from the University of Toronto in 2005, followed by an MSc from the University of British Columbia in 2009 with a focus on machine learning for robotics. He later conducted doctoral research at the Stanford Vision Lab under the supervision of Fei-Fei Li, where he specialized in connecting images and natural language.

Karpathy's career is marked by significant roles in both academia and industry. At Stanford, he designed and taught the renowned CS 231n course, scaling it significantly to accommodate hundreds of students. In the industrial sector, he served as the Director of AI at Tesla, leading the computer vision team for Autopilot. He was also a founding member of OpenAI in 2015 and recently returned to the organization to lead initiatives involving midtraining and synthetic data generation. 

Beyond his corporate and academic roles, Karpathy has established a massive educational footprint through his "Zero to Hero" YouTube series and the development of open-source tools such as `micrograd`, `arxiv-sanity`, and `ConvNetJS`. His research interests encompass deep neural networks, large language models (LLMs), convolutional neural networks (CNNs), and reinforcement learning.

## Biography & Context

Jensen Huang is the co-founder and CEO of NVIDIA, a company that has become the cornerstone of modern artificial intelligence and high-performance computing. Under his leadership, NVIDIA transitioned from a graphics processing unit (GPU) manufacturer to a global leader in accelerated computing. He is widely recognized for his strategic foresight in positioning NVIDIA's hardware as the foundational infrastructure for deep learning, large language models (LLMs), and autonomous systems. His influence extends across the technology landscape, impacting everything from data centers and gaming to robotics and generative AI.

# Jensen Huang

## Affiliations & Roles

- Co-founder and CEO at [[NVIDIA]]

## Biography & Context

Jensen Huang is a prominent technology executive and entrepreneur best known as the co-founder and CEO of NVIDIA. Under his leadership, the company has transitioned from a graphics processing unit (GPU) manufacturer to a global leader in accelerated computing, data centers, and artificial intelligence. He is widely credited with spearheading the shift toward parallel computing, which has become the foundational architecture for modern deep learning, large language models (LLMs), and high-performance computing.

# Andrew Ng

## Affiliations & Roles

- Educator and Researcher at [[Stanford University]]
- Founder of Landing AI
- Co-founder of Coursera

## Biography & Context

Andrew Ng is a leading figure in the field of artificial intelligence, recognized globally for his work in making machine learning accessible to millions. He has played a pivotal role in the academic and industrial development of deep learning, notably through his leadership in educational initiatives and his research on neural networks. He is recognized as a mentor and teacher to many in the field, including [[Andrej Karpathy]], who was one of his students.

## Biography & Context

Geoff Hinton is widely recognized as one of the "Godfathers of AI," having made fundamental contributions to the development of neural networks. His work has been instrumental in the advancement of deep learning, specifically in training multi-layer networks and establishing the foundations for modern artificial intelligence.

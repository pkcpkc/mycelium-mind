# Summary of: Andrej Karpathy

## Context Metadata
Andrej Karpathy is a prominent AI researcher, educator, and engineer known for his contributions to deep learning at organizations like OpenAI and Tesla. He is highly regarded for his ability to explain complex technical concepts to both experts and the general public.

## Executive Summary
Karpathy's career spans foundational research in computer vision and natural language processing, leadership in autonomous vehicle AI, and large-scale deployment of neural networks. He is a pioneer in educational content for AI, having designed Stanford's CS 231n course and maintaining a popular YouTube channel. His work bridges the gap between theoretical research (PhD, OpenAI) and industrial application (Tesla Autopilot).

## Key Highlights
- 🎓 **Academic Foundation**: Earned a BSc from U of Toronto (2005) and an MSc from UBC (2009). Completed a PhD at Stanford (2016) under Fei-Fei Li, focusing on the intersection of CV and NLP.
- 🏫 **Educational Leadership**: Designed and instructed Stanford's CS 231n, which scaled from 150 to 750 students. Currently produces technical and general audience tracks on YouTube (e.g., "Zero to Hero").
- 🏎️ **Tesla AI Leadership**: Served as Director of AI at Tesla, leading the computer vision team for Autopilot and early work on Optimus. Managed in-house data labeling and custom chip deployment.
- 🤖 **OpenAI & Research**: Founding member of OpenAI (2015) and later returned to build teams for midtraining and synthetic data generation.
- 🛠️ **Open Source & Projects**: Created notable tools including `micrograd` (autograd engine), `arxiv-sanity`, and `ConvNetJS`.
- 📝 **Thought Leadership**: Author of influential posts on "Software 2.0," training recipes, and a survival guide to PhDs.

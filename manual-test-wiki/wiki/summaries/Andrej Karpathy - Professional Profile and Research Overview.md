# Summary of: Andrej Karpathy

## Context Metadata
- **Subject**: Andrej Karpathy, a prominent AI researcher, educator, and engineer.
- **Core Expertise**: Deep learning architectures, computer vision, large-scale model training, and educational content creation.
- **Key Affiliations**: OpenAI (Founding Member), Tesla (Director of AI), Stanford University (Instructor).

## Executive Summary
Andrej Karpathy is a leading figure in the artificial intelligence landscape, known for his contributions to both industrial applications and academic education. His career spans foundational research in computer vision and NLP, leadership roles at Tesla and OpenAI, and a massive educational footprint via his "Zero to Hero" YouTube series. He is recognized for bridging the gap between complex neural network theory and practical implementation, evidenced by his work on Tesla's Autopilot and his development of open-source tools like `micrograd` and `ConvNetJS`.

## Key Highlights
- ⚓ **Academic Foundation**: Earned a BSc from the University of Toronto (2005) and an MSc from the University of British Columbia (2009), focusing on machine learning for robotics.
- ⚓ **Stanford PhD & Teaching**: Conducted doctoral research at the Stanford Vision Lab under Fei-Fei Li. He designed and taught CS 231n, which scaled from 150 to over 750 students.
- ⚓ **Industrial Leadership**: Served as the Director of AI at Tesla, leading the computer vision team for Autopilot and early work on Optimus.
- ⚓ **OpenAI Tenure**: Was a founding member of OpenAI (2015) and recently returned to the company to lead a team focused on midtraining and synthetic data.
- ⚓ **Educational Content**: Operates a high-impact YouTube channel featuring technical tracks (e.g., "Let's build GPT") and general audience guides to LLMs.
- ⚓ **Open Source & Projects**: Developed various tools including `micrograd` (autograd engine), `arxiv-sanity` (paper discovery), and `ConvNetJS` (browser-based deep learning).

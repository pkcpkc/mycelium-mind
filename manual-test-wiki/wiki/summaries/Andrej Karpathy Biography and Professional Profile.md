## Executive Summary
Andrej Karpathy is a prominent AI researcher, educator, and engineer known for his significant contributions to deep learning and computer vision. He has held high-level leadership roles at Tesla (Director of AI) and OpenAI (Founding Member). Karpathy is equally recognized for his educational impact, having designed Stanford's first deep learning course (CS 231n) and maintaining a popular YouTube channel that bridges the gap between technical "Zero to Hero" tutorials and general audience AI insights. His work spans from large-scale infrastructure (Tesla's Autopilot) to foundational research and practical educational tools.

## Key Highlights
*   **🎓 Academic Foundation:** Earned a BSc from the University of Toronto (2005) and an MSc from the University of British Columbia (2009), followed by a PhD at Stanford supervised by Fei-Fei Li.
*   **🚗 Tesla Leadership:** Served as Director of AI at Tesla, leading the computer vision team for Autopilot and early work on Optimus, overseeing in-house data labeling and neural network deployment.
*   **🤖 OpenAI Tenure:** Was a founding member of OpenAI (2015) and later returned to build teams focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation.
*   **🏫 Educational Influence:** Architect of Stanford's CS 231n; creator of the "Zero to Hero" YouTube series and various "spelled-out" deep learning tutorials.
*   **🛠️ Open Source & Projects:** Developed notable projects including `micrograd` (autograd engine), `char-rnn`, `arxiv-sanity`, and `ConvNetJS`.
*   **📝 Research & Publications:** Author of numerous papers on image captioning, video classification, and visual-semantic alignments, with a focus on the intersection of vision and language.

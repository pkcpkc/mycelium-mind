# Summary of: Andrej Karpathy

## Context Metadata
- **Subject:** Professional profile and academic history of Andrej Karpathy.
- **Key Affiliations:** OpenAI (Founding Member), Tesla (Director of AI), Stanford University (Instructor/PhD).
- **Core Expertise:** Deep neural networks, computer vision, large-scale data training, and AI education.

## Executive Summary
Andrej Karpathy is a prominent AI researcher, educator, and engineer who has held leadership roles at major technology firms including OpenAI and Tesla. He is well-known for his academic contributions to computer vision, his role in developing Tesla's Autopilot computer vision team, and his extensive educational content on YouTube. His work spans from foundational research in convolutional neural networks to modern applications of Large Language Models (LLMs).

## Key Highlights
- 🎓 **Academic Foundation:** Earned a BSc from the University of Toronto (2005) and an MSc from the University of British Columbia (2009). Completed a PhD at Stanford Vision Lab under Fei-Fei Li, focusing on the intersection of CV and NLP.
- 🏫 **Education & Teaching:** Designed and instructed Stanford's CS 231n, which grew to over 750 students in 2017. He maintains a popular YouTube channel featuring "Zero to Hero" technical tracks and general audience guides on LLMs.
- 🚗 **Tesla Leadership:** Served as Director of AI at Tesla, leading the computer vision team for Autopilot and Optimus. His work involved in-house data labeling, neural network training, and custom chip deployment.
- 🤖 **OpenAI & Research:** Was a founding member of OpenAI (2015) and recently returned to the company to lead a team focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation.
- 🛠️ **Open Source & Projects:** Created several notable projects including `micrograd` (autograd engine), `convnetjs` (browser-based DL library), and `arxiv-sanity`.
- 🎤 **Public Speaking:** Frequent speaker at major AI venues including NVIDIA GTC, ICML, CVPR, and various high-profile podcasts (Lex Fridman, etc.).

## Biography & Context

Villeroy de Galhau serves as the Governor of the Bank of France, where he plays a pivotal role in steering national monetary operations and advising on macroeconomic strategy. His tenure has been closely aligned with France's ongoing efforts to navigate high fiscal uncertainty, implement structural reforms, and maintain robust financial sector oversight. As a key voice in French economic governance, he has actively engaged with multilateral institutions, including the [[IMF]], to address challenges surrounding public debt sustainability, fiscal consolidation, and long-term productivity growth. His policy approach emphasizes prudent debt management, innovation-driven development, and resilience against geopolitical and economic headwinds, ensuring alignment with broader European economic integration and stability goals.

# Alex Stomper

## Affiliations & Roles

- Presenter at [[HU Berlin]]

## Biography & Context

Alex Stomper is a researcher and presenter focused on financial economics for citizens, with a particular emphasis on sovereign credit rating frameworks. In 2024, he presented an analysis of Standard & Poor's sovereign credit rating methodology at [[HU Berlin]]. His work highlights the five core assessment pillars used by rating agencies—institutional, economic, external, fiscal, and monetary—and emphasizes the significant role of discretionary judgment in final ratings. Motivated by the lack of full replicability in institutional rating processes, he advocates for a citizen science initiative to systematically apply S&P's methodology to European countries, aiming to document opacity and inconsistencies in financial assessments.

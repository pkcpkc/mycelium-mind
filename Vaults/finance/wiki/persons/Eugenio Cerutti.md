# Eugenio Cerutti

## Affiliations & Roles

- Deputy Director at [[International Monetary Fund]]
- Head of the Monetary and Capital Markets Department at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Eugenio Cerutti is a senior economist and policy advisor at the International Monetary Fund, currently serving as Deputy Director. In his leadership role overseeing the Monetary and Capital Markets Department, he directs IMF surveillance and analytical work on global financial stability, macroeconomic policy coordination, and structural reform implementation across member economies. His expertise is instrumental in shaping staff assessments for Article IV consultations, including the 2025 review of France, where his department's frameworks inform evaluations on fiscal consolidation pathways, pension and labor market adjustments, and the mitigation of geoeconomic fragmentation. Cerutti's work emphasizes strengthening capital allocation mechanisms, advancing financial sector resilience, and supporting sustainable debt trajectories in an increasingly volatile macroeconomic environment.

# Manuela Goretti

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Manuela Goretti is a staff member of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) who participated in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for France. Her work contributed to the IMF's comprehensive assessment of the French economy's resilience, fiscal consolidation trajectory, structural reform priorities, and financial sector oversight. The consultation process, which concluded in mid-2025, evaluated France's progress on addressing elevated public debt, advancing labor market and pension reforms, and navigating geoeconomic headwinds. Goretti's involvement aligns with the IMF's broader mandate to support sustainable macroeconomic stabilization and policy coordination within the European Union.

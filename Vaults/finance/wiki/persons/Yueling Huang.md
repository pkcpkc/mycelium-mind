# Yueling Huang

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Mission Staff at International Monetary Fund (IMF)

## Biography & Context

Yueling Huang served as a staff member during the International Monetary Fund's 2025 Article IV Consultation mission to Italy. She participated in the staff discussions with Italian officials and contributed to the Executive Board's analysis of Italy's macroeconomic performance, fiscal consolidation progress, and structural reform agenda. Her work focused on evaluating the country's public debt trajectory, labor market dynamics, productivity challenges, and the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP) amid evolving global uncertainties and demographic headwinds.

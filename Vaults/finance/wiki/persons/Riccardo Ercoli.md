# Riccardo Ercoli

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Economist at International Monetary Fund (IMF)
- Consultation Team Member for Italy (2025 Article IV Consultation)
- Consultation Team Member for Malta (2024 Article IV Consultation)

## Biography & Context

Riccardo Ercoli is an IMF staff economist who has participated in multiple Article IV Consultations. In the 2025 consultation for Italy, he contributed to the analysis of Italy's macroeconomic developments, fiscal consolidation efforts, and structural reform priorities, providing technical guidance on growth trajectory, debt sustainability, labor market dynamics, and the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP). More recently, he was involved in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta. His work on Malta focused on evaluating the country's decade of robust economic expansion, moderating growth trends, fiscal consolidation plans under the EU’s new fiscal framework, financial sector stability, and structural reforms aimed at shifting from labor-intensive growth to a productivity-driven model. His responsibilities include assessing climate goals, innovation support, and anti-money laundering compliance to ensure sustainable development and investor confidence.

## Biography & Context

Alexander Pitt is an IMF staff member recognized for his contribution to the 2025 Malta Country Report and 2024 Article IV Consultation. His work supported the staff appraisal and Executive Board assessment of Malta's economic trajectory, focusing on macroeconomic stabilization, fiscal consolidation under the EU framework, financial sector macroprudential oversight, and structural reforms targeting innovation and climate resilience. As the Maltese economy transitions from labor-intensive expansion to a productivity-driven model, Pitt's analysis helped outline strategic priorities including corporate tax alignment with Pillar II directives, real estate risk management, and sustainable tourism practices.

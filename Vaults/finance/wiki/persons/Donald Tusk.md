# Donald Tusk

## Affiliations & Roles

- Prime Minister of Poland
- European Council Member (Former)
- Civic Platform (Civic Coalition)

## Biography & Context

Donald Tusk is a central figure in contemporary Polish governance, currently directing the national administration through a period of extensive institutional and legislative recalibration. In 2025, his government has prioritized the implementation of the EU Rule of Law Action Plan, focusing on resolving the status of judiciary appointees under post-2017 reforms, restructuring anti-corruption competences by dissolving the Central Anti-Corruption Bureau, and aligning media regulations with the European Media Freedom Act. Under his leadership, Poland has moved to separate the Prosecutor General from the Minister of Justice, fully operationalized the European Public Prosecutor Office, and initiated reforms to restore transparency in public broadcasting and public procurement. Tusk's tenure is characterized by a strategic push to improve Poland's civic space rating, strengthen legislative consultation mechanisms, and address longstanding impunity clauses, all while navigating complex constitutional dynamics with the Constitutional Tribunal and maintaining close engagement with EU institutions and the Venice Commission.

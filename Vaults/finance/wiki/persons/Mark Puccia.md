# Mark Puccia

## Affiliations & Roles

- Credit Analyst at S&P Global Ratings

## Biography & Context

Mark Puccia is a credit analyst associated with S&P Global Ratings, known for his involvement in the development and implementation of the agency's sovereign credit evaluation frameworks. He contributed to the creation of the 2017 Sovereign Rating Methodology, which replaced previous criteria and introduced a comprehensive five-pillar scoring system. His work focused on refining the institutional, economic, external, fiscal, and monetary assessments used to gauge sovereign creditworthiness, ensuring the methodology accurately reflected transition dynamics, contingent liabilities, and monetary union constraints.

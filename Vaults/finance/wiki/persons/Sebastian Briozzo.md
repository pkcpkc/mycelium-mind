# Sebastian Briozzo

## Affiliations & Roles

- Executive/Contributor at [[S&P Global Ratings]]

## Biography & Context

Sebastian Briozzo is a recognized figure within [[S&P Global Ratings]], primarily noted for his contributions to sovereign credit assessment frameworks. He played a key role in the development and publication of the updated Sovereign Rating Methodology in December 2017, which introduced a comprehensive five-pillar evaluation system (institutional, economic, external, fiscal, and monetary) and revised key rating parameters, including local-currency rating caps and debt burden assessments. His work focuses on refining how sovereign creditworthiness is measured, particularly in the context of monetary unions, external liquidity, and contingent liability risks.

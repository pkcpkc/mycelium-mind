Holzmann’s policy focus aligns with the report’s emphasis on mitigating demographic aging pressures, expanding labor supply, and enhancing healthcare and pension sustainability. Additionally, he plays a key role in overseeing the financial sector’s resilience, particularly in managing commercial real estate vulnerabilities and maintaining robust capital buffers amid global trade uncertainties. Austrian authorities, including Holzmann, have committed to implementing targeted fiscal adjustments, comprehensive labor market reforms, and sustained cooperation on anti-corruption and financial stability measures.

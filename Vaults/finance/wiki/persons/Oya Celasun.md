## Biography & Context

Oya Celasun is an IMF staff analyst recognized for her analytical contributions to multiple Article IV consultations, including the 2025 assessments for Austria and Slovakia. Her work centers on evaluating post-recession economic recovery, fiscal consolidation strategies, and financial sector stability across Central European economies. Operating within the IMF framework, she assesses structural reforms, labor market dynamics, and macroeconomic policy impacts, with a particular focus on navigating demographic headwinds, inflationary pressures, and governance risks. Her recent analyses emphasize the necessity of balanced fiscal consolidation, strengthening financial sector resilience, and implementing structural reforms to enhance long-term competitiveness, growth, and deeper integration with European institutional frameworks.

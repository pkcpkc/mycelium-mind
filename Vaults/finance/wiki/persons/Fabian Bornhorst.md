# Fabian Bornhorst

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Economist at International Monetary Fund (IMF)

## Biography & Context

Fabian Bornhorst is an International Monetary Fund (IMF) economist who played a central role in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Poland. His work focused on evaluating Poland's macroeconomic recovery trajectory, fiscal consolidation pathways, and structural reform priorities. Bornhorst contributed to the assessment of projected economic growth, inflation dynamics driven by labor market tightness, and the long-term fiscal sustainability challenges posed by demographic aging and climate transition requirements. His involvement in the staff report preparation, Executive Board discussions, and policy recommendations underscores his expertise in European macroeconomic stabilization, monetary-fiscal coordination, and financial sector oversight.

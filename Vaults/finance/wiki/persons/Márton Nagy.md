## Biography & Context

Márton Nagy is an official associated with the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF). He served on the mission team for the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation with [[Hungary]], participating in the assessment of the country's macroeconomic policies, fiscal sustainability, and structural reform agenda. His work focused on analyzing Hungary's economic stagnation, persistent inflation above the central bank's target, and the macroeconomic distortions caused by regulatory interventions, price caps, and windfall taxes. The consultation, concluded in August 2025, emphasized the need for strengthened fiscal buffers, tight monetary policy, and governance reforms to unlock suspended [[EU Funds]] and restore competitiveness. Nagy's contributions align with the IMF staff's broader policy recommendations aimed at rebuilding macroeconomic stability and fostering long-term growth.

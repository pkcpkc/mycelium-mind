# Daphne Caruana Galizia

## Affiliations & Roles

- Independent Journalist and Political Commentator
- Investigative Reporter focusing on government corruption and public accountability

## Biography & Context

Daphne Caruana Galizia was a prominent Maltese journalist and political commentator known for her fearless investigative reporting on corruption, money laundering, and abuses of power. Her assassination remains an unresolved case that continues to cast a long shadow over Malta's anti-corruption efforts and highlights critical vulnerabilities in media safety and pluralism. According to the 2025 EU Commission Rule of Law Report, her unresolved case underscores the urgent need for stronger institutional safeguards, independent media oversight, and effective protections for journalists. Her legacy remains a central reference point in ongoing national discussions regarding judicial independence, public procurement transparency, and the restoration of democratic checks and balances.

# Kenneth Farrugia

## Affiliations & Roles

- Economic Policy Official at [[Malta]]

## Biography & Context

Kenneth Farrugia is a Maltese government official who participated in the 2024 [[Article IV Consultation]] conducted by the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF) for [[Malta]]. He was involved in staff discussions concerning Malta's macroeconomic trajectory, fiscal consolidation under the EU’s new fiscal framework, and structural reforms aimed at shifting the economy from labor-intensive growth to a productivity-driven model. His work centered on aligning national strategies with EU directives, including corporate tax reform compliance with Pillar II, the phase-out of fixed energy subsidies, and improvements in financial stability and anti-money laundering frameworks. Farrugia collaborated with IMF mission leads and domestic stakeholders to develop roadmaps for innovation, digitalization, and climate mitigation aligned with [[Malta Vision 2050]].

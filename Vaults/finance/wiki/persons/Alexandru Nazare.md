# Alexandru Nazare

## Affiliations & Roles

- Key Government Official at [[Romania]]
- Fiscal Policy Representative at [[IMF]] Article IV Consultations

## Biography & Context

Alexandru Nazare has been a central figure in Romania's macroeconomic management during a period of significant fiscal adjustment. As a leading representative of the Romanian authorities, he has been directly involved in designing and implementing the 2025–26 comprehensive fiscal reform package aimed at curbing widening twin deficits. This includes overseeing measures such as VAT increases, higher excise and dividend taxes, and public sector wage freezes. Nazare has maintained continuous dialogue with the [[IMF]] to align national policies with Article IV consultation recommendations, focusing on reducing the fiscal deficit below 6% of GDP by 2026 and stabilizing sovereign debt. His work also encompasses coordinating structural reforms to unlock stalled EU funds, improve public investment management, and strengthen the overall resilience of Romania's economy against external shocks and domestic inflationary pressures.

# Silvio Schembri

## Affiliations & Roles

- Government Official / Political Representative at [[Malta]]

## Biography & Context

Silvio Schembri is referenced as a key Maltese official involved in high-level dialogues during the 2024 [[Article IV Consultation]] conducted by the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF). His engagements with IMF staff centered on [[Malta]]'s macroeconomic performance, fiscal consolidation strategies under the EU’s new fiscal framework, and structural reforms aimed at shifting from labor-intensive growth to a productivity-driven model. He participated in discussions regarding [[Corporate Income Tax]] reform, compliance with the EU’s Pillar II directive, financial sector stability measures, and alignment with the recently launched [[Malta Vision 2050]]. His involvement underscores the government's focus on balancing robust economic expansion with sustainable development, anti-money laundering progress, and climate mitigation goals.

# Yueshu Zhao

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Yueshu Zhao is an International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff member who participated in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for [[Malta]]. Involvement included staff discussions with Maltese officials and contributing to the IMF Executive Board's assessment of Malta's economic trajectory. The consultation evaluated Malta's decade of robust economic expansion, labor market dynamics, real estate market activity, and the need for structural reforms to shift from labor-intensive growth to a productivity-driven model. Zhao's work aligns with the IMF's recommendations for fiscal consolidation, corporate income tax reform, financial stability safeguards, and climate change mitigation strategies to ensure sustainable development through 2030.

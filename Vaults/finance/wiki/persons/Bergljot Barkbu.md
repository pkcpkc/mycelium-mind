# Bergljot Barkbu

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Member / Mission Lead at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Bergljot Barkbu is an International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff member who has contributed to major Article IV Consultations across multiple European economies. She initially participated in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Austria, where her analytical work supported macroeconomic projections and policy recommendations addressing fiscal consolidation, financial sector stability, demographic headwinds, inflation dynamics, and housing supply bottlenecks.

Barkbu also contributed to the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania, assisting in the assessment of the country's economic adjustment amid sluggish growth and widening twin deficits. Her work focused on evaluating necessary fiscal consolidation measures (including VAT increases, higher excise/dividend taxes, and public wage freezes), analyzing persistent inflation pressures driven by energy price adjustments and nominal wage growth, and examining financial sector vulnerabilities. Her analytical contributions support the IMF's recommendations for monetary policy normalization, enhanced exchange rate flexibility, structural reforms to unlock EU funds and improve state-owned enterprise governance, and long-term growth strategies such as boosting labor force participation and advancing low-carbon transitions.

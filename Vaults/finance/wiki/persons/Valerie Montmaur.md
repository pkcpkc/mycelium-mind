# Valerie Montmaur

## Affiliations & Roles

- Contributor at [[S&P Global Ratings]]

## Biography & Context

Valerie Montmaur is a financial professional recognized for her contributions to sovereign credit analysis and rating methodologies. She is associated with the 2017 update to S&P Global Ratings' sovereign credit assessment framework, which introduced a comprehensive five-pillar approach to evaluating government and monetary authority creditworthiness. Her work supports the analysis of institutional, economic, external, fiscal, and monetary factors that determine sovereign ratings.

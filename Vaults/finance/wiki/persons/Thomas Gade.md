# Thomas Gade

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Economist at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Thomas Gade is an economist at the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF) who served as a key contributor to the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta. His analytical work centered on evaluating Malta’s decade-long economic expansion, labor market dynamics, and financial sector resilience. Gade assisted in drafting staff assessments on fiscal consolidation strategies, corporate tax reforms aligned with EU Pillar II directives, and macroprudential safeguards for the banking sector. His research also addressed structural bottlenecks in tourism, real estate, and innovation, while outlining policy recommendations to transition Malta toward a productivity-driven growth model. Through this mission, Gade collaborated closely with Maltese authorities and fellow IMF economists to assess macroeconomic trajectories, mitigate downside risks, and advance sustainable development and climate resilience goals through 2030.

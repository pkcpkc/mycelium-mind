# Joong Shik Kang

## Affiliations & Roles

- Economist at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Joong Shik Kang is an economist at the International Monetary Fund (IMF) who contributed to the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania. His work focuses on analyzing macroeconomic vulnerabilities, fiscal consolidation strategies, and structural reforms aimed at addressing widening twin deficits and persistent inflationary pressures. He has collaborated with Romanian authorities and fellow IMF staff to evaluate the impact of policy measures, including VAT increases, higher excise and dividend taxes, and public sector wage freezes. His assessments support broader institutional efforts to stabilize sovereign debt trajectories, enhance banking sector resilience, and promote long-term economic modernization through improved public investment management and labor force participation.

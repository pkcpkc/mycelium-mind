# Anke Weber

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[IMF]]
- Macroeconomic Policy Analyst for [[Hungary]]

## Biography & Context

Anke Weber is an International Monetary Fund (IMF) professional who played a central role in the 2025 Article IV consultation with [[Hungary]]. Her work focuses on evaluating macroeconomic stability, fiscal sustainability, and the implementation of structural reforms across member economies. During the consultation process, she contributed to analyzing Hungary's persistent inflation, public debt dynamics, and market-distorting regulatory interventions, while helping formulate policy recommendations aimed at restoring fiscal buffers, tightening monetary transmission, and unlocking suspended [[EU Funds]]. Her analytical framework emphasizes data-dependent monetary stances, targeted fiscal adjustments, and governance reforms to enhance competitiveness and foster sustainable growth.

# Geoff Gottlieb

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Geoff Gottlieb is an International Monetary Fund staff member who contributed to the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Poland. His work focused on evaluating Poland's macroeconomic outlook, including projected economic growth, fiscal consolidation pathways, inflation dynamics driven by labor market tightness, and financial sector stability. He played a key role in assessing the implementation of the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan, analyzing the impact of NGEU fund absorption, and evaluating structural reform priorities such as labor market integration, demographic challenges, and the climate transition. His analysis supports IMF recommendations aimed at stabilizing public debt, managing monetary-fiscal coordination, and fostering long-term convergence amid downside risks from geopolitical tensions and European economic slowdowns.

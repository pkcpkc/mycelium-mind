# Moritz Kraemer

## Affiliations & Roles

- Sovereign Credit Ratings Analyst at [[S&P Global Ratings]]

## Biography & Context

Moritz Kraemer is a credit ratings professional at S&P Global Ratings, recognized for his contributions to the development and refinement of sovereign creditworthiness evaluation frameworks. He played a key role in the 2017 update of the Sovereign Rating Methodology, which restructured the assessment of government and monetary authority credit risk into a comprehensive five-pillar model. This framework evaluates institutional quality, economic strength, external vulnerability, fiscal performance, and monetary policy credibility to determine sovereign credit ratings. His expertise focuses on translating macroeconomic indicators, institutional stability, and debt dynamics into actionable credit assessments, with a particular emphasis on local-currency sovereign debt, monetary union members, and contingent liability risks.

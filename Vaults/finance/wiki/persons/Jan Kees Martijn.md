# Jan Kees Martijn

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at International Monetary Fund

## Biography & Context

Jan Kees Martijn is an International Monetary Fund staff member who contributed to the 2025 Poland Article IV Consultation. His work centers on evaluating Poland’s macroeconomic trajectory, with a focus on fiscal consolidation strategies, inflation dynamics, and structural reform priorities. He collaborated with IMF mission teams to assess the country’s economic recovery, labor market conditions, and long-term growth challenges, including demographic pressures, climate transition imperatives, and refugee integration. His analysis supports the IMF’s recommendations for rebuilding fiscal buffers, maintaining appropriate monetary policy stances, and fostering deeper financial market development to sustain Poland’s convergence and competitiveness.

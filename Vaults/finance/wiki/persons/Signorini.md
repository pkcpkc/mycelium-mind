# Signorini

## Affiliations & Roles

- Economic Policy Official at [[Italian Government]]

## Biography & Context

Signorini was referenced as a key official engaged during the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation with Italy. The consultation process involved staff discussions with Italian authorities to evaluate the country's moderate economic growth, resilient labor market, and improved fiscal performance. Key topics addressed during engagements included the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), structural reforms to boost productivity, and a medium-term fiscal consolidation plan aimed at placing public debt on a downward trajectory amid demographic headwinds and global uncertainties.

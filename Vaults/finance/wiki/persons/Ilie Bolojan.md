## Biography & Context

Ilie Bolojan serves as a central figure within the Romanian authorities during the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation period, spearheading the nation's comprehensive fiscal consolidation package. His responsibilities center on implementing critical economic adjustments aimed at curbing widening twin deficits, which reached 8.7% of GDP in the fiscal balance and 8.2% in the current account. Bolojan's policy portfolio encompasses the administration of VAT and excise tax increases, dividend taxation adjustments, and strict freezes on public sector wages and pensions. These measures are designed to stabilize public finances, bring the deficit below 6% of GDP by 2026, and lay the groundwork for a medium-term target below 3% by 2030. His work directly intersects with efforts to accelerate EU fund absorption, improve public investment management, and navigate complex macroeconomic headwinds, including inflationary pressures from energy price liberalization and strong wage growth. Bolojan collaborates closely with international financial institutions and domestic regulatory bodies to ensure Romania maintains economic stability, market confidence, and structural modernization amid shifting global trade and geopolitical landscapes.

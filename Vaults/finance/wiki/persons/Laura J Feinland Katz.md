# Laura J Feinland Katz

## Affiliations & Roles

- Financial Analyst & Methodology Contributor at [[S&P Global Ratings]]

## Biography & Context

Laura J Feinland Katz is a financial professional and credit rating analyst associated with S&P Global Ratings. She served as a key contributor to the development and publication of the updated Sovereign Rating Methodology framework released in December 2017. Her work focuses on sovereign creditworthiness assessments, particularly refining the institutional, economic, and fiscal evaluation pillars used to grade government debt. She has collaborated extensively with fellow S&P analysts to integrate complex metrics—such as net general government debt, contingent liabilities, and monetary union dynamics—into the agency's rating architecture.

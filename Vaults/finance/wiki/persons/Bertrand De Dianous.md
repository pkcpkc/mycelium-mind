# Bertrand De Dianous

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher at [[S&P Global Ratings]]

## Biography & Context

Bertrand De Dianous is a financial analyst and researcher associated with [[S&P Global Ratings]]. He played a key role in developing the updated Sovereign Rating Methodology released in December 2017, which restructured the criteria for evaluating sovereign creditworthiness across institutional, economic, external, fiscal, and monetary dimensions. His work centers on sovereign debt assessment, credit rating frameworks, and macroeconomic risk evaluation.

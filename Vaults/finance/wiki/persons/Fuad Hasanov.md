# Fuad Hasanov

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]
- Participant in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for [[Malta]]

## Biography & Context

Fuad Hasanov serves as a staff member within the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF), where he contributed to the 2024 Article IV Consultation for [[Malta]]. This comprehensive review evaluated Malta's robust economic expansion, particularly in export-oriented service sectors such as [[Tourism]] and [[Online Gaming]], while addressing mounting capacity constraints in infrastructure, labor markets, and the [[Real Estate]] sector. Hasanov's involvement centered on assessing Malta's progress in fiscal consolidation, financial stability, structural reforms, and climate mitigation goals. The consultation highlighted the need for a strategic pivot toward a productivity-driven economic model, emphasizing corporate tax alignment with EU Pillar II directives, macroprudential oversight for the banking sector, and sustained innovation and anti-money laundering reforms. His work supports the IMF's broader mission to ensure sustainable macroeconomic stability and growth alignment with EU frameworks in Maltese policy planning.

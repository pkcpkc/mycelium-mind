# Alexander Wunsch

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Representative for Belgium at [[International Monetary Fund]]
- Policy Liaison on Fiscal Consolidation and Structural Reforms at [[Government of Belgium]]

## Biography & Context

Alexander Wunsch is a central figure referenced in the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation for Belgium. His work focuses on engaging with Belgian authorities to navigate significant macroeconomic headwinds, including slowing growth, persistent core inflation, and declining labor-cost competitiveness. He has been actively involved in staff discussions with Belgian officials regarding the implementation of a seven-year fiscal consolidation plan, structural labor market reforms, and adjustments to automatic wage indexation. His role encompasses facilitating policy dialogue between the IMF and Belgian policymakers to align on long-term growth strategies, public debt sustainability trajectories, and comprehensive structural adjustments targeting the 2025–2030 horizon.

# Kotaro Ishi

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Kotaro Ishi is a member of the International Monetary Fund staff who contributed to the 2024 Article IV Consultation for [[Malta]]. Working within the IMF's macroeconomic and fiscal assessment framework, Ishi helped analyze Malta's decade-long economic expansion driven by export-oriented service sectors, notably [[Tourism]] and [[Online Gaming]]. The consultation process evaluated the country's transition from labor-intensive growth to a productivity-driven model, with Ishi's work supporting policy recommendations on fiscal consolidation, financial stability, structural reforms, and climate mitigation strategies. His analysis focused on addressing capacity constraints in the labor market and real estate sector, while aligning Malta's macroeconomic trajectory with EU fiscal directives and long-term development goals.

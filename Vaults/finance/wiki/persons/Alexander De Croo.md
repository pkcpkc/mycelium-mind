# Alexander De Croo

## Affiliations & Roles

- Prime Minister of Belgium
- Member of the Belgian Federal Government
- Lead representative for IMF Article IV Consultations

## Biography & Context

Alexander De Croo serves as the Prime Minister of Belgium, directing federal economic policy during a period of significant macroeconomic adjustment. In 2025, his administration engaged in comprehensive discussions with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) to address structural deficits, persistent inflation, and a projected public debt trajectory exceeding 120% of GDP. Under his leadership, Belgium has committed to a seven-year fiscal consolidation plan designed to improve the structural primary balance by 0.6 percentage points annually, while safeguarding public investment in healthcare, education, and infrastructure.

De Croo has been instrumental in advancing the structural reforms outlined in international assessments, including modernizing the automatic wage indexation framework, enhancing labor market flexibility, and implementing tax reforms that gradually shift the fiscal burden from labor to capital. His government continues to navigate demographic aging pressures and decarbonization mandates, emphasizing strengthened federal-regional coordination to sustain long-term economic resilience and competitiveness within the European Union.

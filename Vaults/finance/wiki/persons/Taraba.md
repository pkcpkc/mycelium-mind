## Biography & Context

Taraba is referenced in the context of the International Monetary Fund's 2025 Article IV Consultation for the Slovak Republic. During the virtual and in-person discussions held in late 2024 and early 2025, Taraba engaged with IMF staff on critical economic policy matters, including fiscal consolidation strategies, financial sector resilience, and structural reforms aimed at addressing demographic challenges and governance risks. The discussions focused on aligning Slovakia's economic roadmap with EU frameworks, managing inflationary pressures, and ensuring long-term debt sustainability through expenditure-based measures and institutional strengthening.

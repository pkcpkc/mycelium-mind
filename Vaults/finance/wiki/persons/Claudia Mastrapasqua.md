# Claudia Mastrapasqua

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Claudia Mastrapasqua is referenced in the context of the 2024 [[Article IV Consultation]] for [[Malta]]. She participated in IMF staff discussions and contributed to the preparation of the 2025 Malta Country Report, which evaluates the country's macroeconomic performance, fiscal consolidation trajectory, financial sector stability, and structural reforms. Her work supports the IMF's assessment of Malta's economic policies and its alignment with broader European Union frameworks.

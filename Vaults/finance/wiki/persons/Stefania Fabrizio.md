# Stefania Fabrizio

## Affiliations & Roles

- Economist at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Stefania Fabrizio is an economist at the International Monetary Fund (IMF) who specializes in European macroeconomic stability, fiscal consolidation, and structural policy analysis. She served as a core member of the IMF mission team for Slovakia's 2025 Article IV Consultation, where she contributed to evaluating the country's economic transition, growth projections, and inflationary pressures. Her work focuses on designing policy roadmaps for sustainable debt management, financial sector resilience, and governance reforms to unlock EU fund disbursements. Through her involvement in the Slovakia mission, she helped synthesize recommendations on pension system adjustments, Active Labor Market Policies, and environmental levy implementations to align national strategies with Euro Area and broader EU economic frameworks.

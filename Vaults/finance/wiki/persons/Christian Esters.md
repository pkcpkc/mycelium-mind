# Christian Esters

## Affiliations & Roles

- Credit Ratings Expert at [[S&P Global Ratings]]

## Biography & Context

Christian Esters is a financial analyst and credit ratings professional associated with [[S&P Global Ratings]]. He contributed to the development and publication of the updated Sovereign Rating Methodology in December 2017, which modernized the framework for evaluating sovereign creditworthiness through five core pillars: institutional, economic, external, fiscal, and monetary assessments. His work centers on sovereign debt analysis, rating criteria refinement, and financial risk assessment within the global credit ratings industry.

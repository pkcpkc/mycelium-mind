## Biography & Context

Kamenický is identified as a Slovak official engaged in the International Monetary Fund's 2025 Article IV Consultation for the Slovak Republic. He participated in the policy dialogue that commenced with virtual discussions in December 2024 and transitioned to in-person meetings in Bratislava in January 2025. His involvement centers on navigating the country's complex economic transition, particularly focusing on fiscal consolidation, financial sector resilience, and structural reforms to address demographic headwinds and governance risks. Through these consultations, he contributed to aligning Slovak economic strategy with IMF recommendations aimed at achieving moderate growth recovery, inflation moderation, and long-term debt sustainability.

# Thomas Elkjaer

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Economist at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Thomas Elkjaer served as a key staff member during the International Monetary Fund's 2025 Article IV Consultation mission to Italy. His work focused on assessing the country's macroeconomic performance, fiscal consolidation trajectory, and structural reform priorities amid demographic headwinds and global uncertainties. Elkjaer collaborated closely with IMF leadership and fellow mission staff to analyze Italy's moderate GDP growth, resilient labor market, and high public debt dynamics, contributing to recommendations on tax compliance, NRRP implementation, and medium-term fiscal planning.

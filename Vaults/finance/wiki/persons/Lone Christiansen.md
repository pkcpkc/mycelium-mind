# Lone Christiansen

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Economist at [[International Monetary Fund]]
- Article IV Consultation Mission Member for Italy at [[IMF Executive Board]]

## Biography & Context

Lone Christiansen serves as an economist at the International Monetary Fund, where she contributed to the 2025 Article IV Consultation mission for Italy. Her work centered on evaluating Italy's macroeconomic trajectory, including its resilient labor market, fiscal consolidation progress, and the urgent need for structural reforms to address demographic pressures and stagnant productivity. During the consultation, she helped assess the sustainability of Italy's public debt trajectory, the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), and the broader implications of global trade tensions and climate risks on European economic stability. Her analysis supported the IMF's recommendations for sustained fiscal adjustment, enhanced tax compliance, and targeted industrial policy coordination at the EU level.

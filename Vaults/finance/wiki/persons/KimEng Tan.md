# KimEng Tan

## Affiliations & Roles

- Analyst / Methodology Contributor at [[S&P Global Ratings]]

## Biography & Context

KimEng Tan is a credit rating professional at [[S&P Global Ratings]] who contributed to the 2017 revision of the Sovereign Rating Methodology. This comprehensive framework update restructured sovereign creditworthiness assessments into a five-pillar model (Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary) and introduced key changes such as capping local-currency rating uplifts and basing fiscal assessments on net rather than gross government debt. Through this initiative, Tan helped standardize evaluation criteria for transition dynamics, monetary union members, and contingent liability risks across global sovereign portfolios.

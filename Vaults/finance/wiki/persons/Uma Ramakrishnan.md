# Uma Ramakrishnan

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Uma Ramakrishnan serves as a staff member and researcher at the International Monetary Fund, focusing on macroeconomic policy analysis and country assessments. She was a key contributor to the IMF team that conducted the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Poland, where she helped synthesize findings on the country's economic recovery, fiscal consolidation challenges, inflation dynamics, and structural reform priorities. Her work in this context involved evaluating the interplay between monetary policy trajectories, financial sector stability, and long-term demographic and climate transition pressures. Through this consultation process, she contributed to developing policy recommendations aimed at stabilizing public debt, managing wage-driven inflation, and fostering sustainable labor market integration and green transition investments.

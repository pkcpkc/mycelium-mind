# Vincent Van Peteghem

## Affiliations & Roles

- Policy representative during the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation (Belgian Government)

## Biography & Context

Vincent Van Peteghem is a Belgian official who participated in high-level economic dialogues during the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation. He was among the national stakeholders engaged in staff discussions focused on Belgium's macroeconomic resilience, fiscal consolidation pathways, and structural reform priorities. His involvement aligns with broader federal efforts to navigate projected public debt trajectories, improve labor market flexibility, and implement pension adjustments as recommended in the IMF's 2025 assessment of Belgium's policy framework.

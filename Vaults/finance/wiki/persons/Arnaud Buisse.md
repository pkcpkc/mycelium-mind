# Arnaud Buisse

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Economist at [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF)
- Contributor to the 2025 Article IV Consultation for France

## Biography & Context

Arnaud Buisse is an economist associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF), recognized for his contributions to macroeconomic analysis and policy assessment. He played a key role in the IMF's 2025 Article IV consultation for France, which evaluated the French economy's resilience amid high uncertainty and tight financial conditions. His work focused on analyzing fiscal consolidation strategies, structural reforms, and financial sector stability, contributing to recommendations for sustainable debt trajectories and long-term growth. The consultation highlighted France's progress in managing public debt, advancing labor market and pension reforms, and navigating geoeconomic headwinds.

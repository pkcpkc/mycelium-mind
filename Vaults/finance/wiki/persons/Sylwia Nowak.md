# Sylwia Nowak

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Sylwia Nowak is a staff member of the International Monetary Fund (IMF), actively engaged in macroeconomic policy analysis and country-specific consultations. Her work is prominently documented in the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy, where she contributed to the evaluation of Italy's economic expansion, fiscal consolidation efforts, structural reforms, and financial sector stability. Her analytical focus centers on navigating Italy's demographic headwinds, managing high public debt levels, and promoting productivity-boosting measures to sustain long-term economic resilience.

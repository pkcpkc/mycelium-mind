# Rishi Goyal

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Rishi Goyal is an International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff member who participated in the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy. His work focused on analyzing Italy's moderate economic expansion, resilient labor market, and fiscal consolidation efforts. He contributed to the assessment of structural reforms needed to address demographic headwinds, boost productivity, and ensure sustainable public debt trajectories. Goyal's involvement also encompassed evaluating the resilience of Italy's financial sector and navigating near-term macroeconomic risks, including global trade tensions and climate-related shocks.

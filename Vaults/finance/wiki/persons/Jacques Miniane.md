# Jacques Miniane

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Country Team Member at [[IMF]]

## Biography & Context

Jacques Miniane is an International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff member who contributed to the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Hungary. His analysis focused on Hungary's prolonged economic stagnation, persistent inflation above the central bank's target, and structural macroeconomic vulnerabilities. The consultation highlighted the necessity of restoring fiscal buffers through high-quality consolidation, maintaining a tight monetary policy stance, and implementing structural reforms to enhance competitiveness. Miniane's work emphasized phasing out distortive retail subsidies, broadening the tax base, rationalizing public administration, and advancing governance reforms to unlock suspended [[EU Funds]] and strengthen long-term growth potential.

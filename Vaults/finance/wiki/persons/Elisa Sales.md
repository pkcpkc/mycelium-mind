# Elisa Sales

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff / Consultant at [[International Monetary Fund]]
- Official Representative for [[Malta]] 2024 Article IV Consultation

## Biography & Context

Elisa Sales is documented in the 2025 IMF Malta Country Report as a key participant in the 2024 Article IV Consultation. Alongside a team of international experts and Maltese officials, she contributed to the IMF staff discussions and Executive Board assessments regarding Malta's economic trajectory, fiscal consolidation plans, financial sector stability, and structural reforms. Her involvement encompassed reviewing the country's macroeconomic policies, labor market conditions, real estate dynamics, and climate mitigation strategies throughout the 2024–2025 consultation period.

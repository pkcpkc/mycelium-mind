## Biography & Context

Lombard is identified as a key stakeholder associated with the [[IMF]]'s 2025 Article IV Consultation for [[France]]. The consultation highlighted France's economic resilience, the urgent need for fiscal consolidation to address public debt exceeding 113% of GDP, and critical structural reforms including pension adjustments, labor market integration, and green transition initiatives. Lombard's involvement centers on high-level policy discussions surrounding fiscal sustainability, macroeconomic stability, and financial sector oversight within the French governmental and international economic framework.

# Giorgetti

## Affiliations & Roles

- Official representative engaged in the [[IMF]] 2025 Article IV Consultation for [[Italy]]
- Participant in [[National Recovery and Resilience Plan]] implementation reviews

## Biography & Context

Giorgetti is listed among the Italian officials who participated in the end-of-discussions phase of the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation with [[Italy]]. These discussions, which took place in May 2025, centered on Italy's fiscal consolidation trajectory, structural reform priorities, and demographic headwinds. The consultations highlighted the need for sustained primary surpluses to reduce public debt, improve tax compliance, and implement the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP). Giorgetti's involvement aligns with the broader Italian administration's efforts to navigate moderate economic growth, strengthen financial sector stability, and accelerate productivity-boosting reforms amid global uncertainties.

# Mugur Isarescu

## Affiliations & Roles

- Governor at [[National Bank of Romania]]

## Biography & Context

Mugur Isarescu serves as the Governor of the National Bank of Romania (NBR), where he directs national monetary policy and oversees financial sector stability. During the 2024–2025 period of economic adjustment, Isarescu steered a cautious monetary response to rising inflation and deteriorating public finances. Following the removal of electricity price caps and tax adjustments that pushed headline inflation to 9.9% in September 2025, he paused planned policy rate cuts and maintained the benchmark interest rate at 6.5%. His strategy prioritizes anchoring inflation expectations, tempering strong nominal wage growth, and advocating for greater exchange rate flexibility to mitigate external shocks. Under his leadership, the NBR has also emphasized heightened supervisory vigilance over the sovereign-bank nexus, unhedged foreign exchange corporate loans, and buoyant consumer credit expansion to safeguard banking sector fundamentals.

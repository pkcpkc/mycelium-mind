# 2025 Country Report - Romania

The 2025 Country Report for Romania highlights a nation in a state of rapid convergence toward EU income and productivity levels (reaching nearly 80% of the EU average in 2024), while simultaneously grappling with significant structural vulnerabilities. The report identifies a transition point where Romania must move from a growth model based on low labor costs toward one driven by innovation, high-tech investment, and infrastructure modernization.

### Economic Performance and Fiscal Challenges
Romania's economic growth decelerated to 0.8% in 2024, hampered by high inflation (5.8%) and a widening current account deficit (8.4% of GDP). The primary driver of concern is the high government deficit (9.3% of GDP in 2024), fueled by generous public sector wage increases and social transfers. To counter this, the government has committed to a Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan (MTP) aiming for a 7% deficit in 2025 and 4.3% net expenditure growth by 2028. Key reforms include a comprehensive tax overhaul and the restructuring of public spending.

### Labor Market and Human Capital
While employment rates have improved (69.5% in 2024), Romania suffers from some of the highest rates of youth unemployment and NEETs (Not in Education, Employment, or Training) in the EU. Significant barriers remain for women, people with disabilities, and the Roma community. The report stresses a critical "skills gap," where 70% of adults lack basic digital skills and the tertiary education attainment rate (23.2%) is nearly half the EU average. To address this, the report emphasizes modernizing Public Employment Services (PES) and enhancing vocational training (VET) to align with the demands of a green and digital economy.

### Innovation and Business Environment
Romania is currently classified as an "emerging innovator" with the weakest innovation performance in the EU (34% of the average). Key hurdles include:
- **R&D Underinvestment:** Romania recorded the lowest R&D intensity in the EU (0.52% of GDP).
- **Regulatory Burden:** 90% of firms cite uncertainty as the top barrier to investment, and nearly a quarter of companies dedicate significant staff resources solely to regulatory compliance.
- **Access to Finance:** Companies rely heavily on internal funding (73%), with a nascent venture capital ecosystem and underdeveloped capital markets.

### Decarbonization and Energy
Romania has made strides in renewables (48% of electricity generation in 2024), but faces high energy prices (averaging 103 EUR/MWh) and structural bottlenecks in storage and grid capacity. The report advocates for:
- Shifting the fiscal burden from electricity to fossil fuels to incentivize electrification.
- Accelerating the decommissioning of coal and lignite plants by 2025.
- Implementing "contracts for difference" (CfD) and power purchase agreements (PPAs) to support industrial decarbonization.

### Social Fairness and Infrastructure
Poverty remains a critical issue, with the AROPE rate at 27.9% (well above the EU average of 21.0%). The report highlights a severe housing crisis, particularly in rural areas, where overcrowding and lack of affordable social housing are prevalent. Infrastructure remains a bottleneck; Romania's transport network is among the least dense in the EU, and railway modernization remains slow.

### Key Recommendations
The European Commission concludes that Romania must:
1. **Accelerate RRP Implementation:** Swiftly execute milestones and targets to unlock EU funding.
2. **Fiscal Discipline:** Follow a resolute path to reduce government and current account deficits.
3. **Innovation Boost:** Increase public/private R&D and support business-driven innovation.
4. **Digital Transition:** Improve digital skills for the workforce and digitalize public services.
5. **Regulatory Reform:** Streamline procedures for businesses and deregulate certain professions to improve mobility.
6. **Energy Affordability:** Decarbonize the manufacturing sector while protecting vulnerable consumers from price spikes.

(source: [SWD(2025) 223 final](https://data.europa.eu/eli/SWD/2025/223_final))

* [[IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy]] - The IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation highlights Italy's moderate economic growth and improved fiscal performance, while stressing the urgent need for structural reforms, continued debt reduction, and productivity-boosting measures to address demographic headwinds and global uncertainties.
* [[IMF 2025 Malta Country Report - 2024 Article IV Consultation]] - IMF staff report and Executive Board assessment outlining [[Malta]]'s economic performance, fiscal consolidation plans, financial sector stability, structural reforms, and climate goals for 2024-2030.
* [[IMF 2025 Poland Country Report Article IV Consultation|IMF 2025 Poland Country Report: Article IV Consultation]] - A comprehensive assessment of Poland's economic recovery, fiscal challenges, inflation trends, and structural reform priorities following the 2024 Article IV consultation.
* [[IMF 2025 Romania Article IV Consultation Report]] - Assessment of Romania's economic outlook, fiscal consolidation efforts, inflation trends, and structural reforms amid widening twin deficits.

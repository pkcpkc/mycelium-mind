# Digitalisation

## Summary

Digitalisation refers to the adoption and implementation of digital technologies to modernize institutional processes and improve operational efficiency. In the context of France's justice system, it encompasses strategic efforts to reduce case backlogs, streamline administrative workflows, and enhance access to legal services through dedicated digital infrastructure.

## Key Details

- Central to ongoing judicial reform initiatives, with specific focus on digitalising the justice system to address chronic efficiency challenges and reduce case resolution times.
- Implementation relies on core digital infrastructure, most notably the **[[Portalis]]** platform, which serves as a primary tool for modernizing court operations and case management.
- While progress is recognized, persistent challenges remain regarding implementation speed, technical stability, and the need for sustained budgetary support to ensure successful deployment.
- The Ministry of Justice employs targeted assessments and "flash missions" to optimize digital tool integration alongside broader structural measures aimed at improving sentence execution and overall judicial efficiency.

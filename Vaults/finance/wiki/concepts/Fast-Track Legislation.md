# Fast-Track Legislation

## Summary

Fast-Track Legislation refers to the expedited processing of bills through parliamentary procedures, often at the expense of standard deliberation and consultation phases. In Slovakia's 2025 Rule of Law Report, this practice is highlighted as a recurring mechanism that systematically bypasses meaningful stakeholder consultation, thereby placing significant strain on institutional checks and balances and narrowing democratic oversight.

## Key Details

- Frequently utilized in the Slovak legislative agenda to accelerate bill passage and reduce parliamentary debate time.
- Systematically circumvents standard stakeholder consultation, public feedback windows, and inter-ministerial review.
- Contributes to weakened institutional checks and balances by limiting opportunities for cross-party scrutiny and expert input.
- Correlates with broader rule of law concerns, including reduced transparency in policy formulation and diminished civil society engagement.
- Recognized by the European Commission as a structural factor that undermines legislative quality, legitimacy, and long-term policy sustainability.

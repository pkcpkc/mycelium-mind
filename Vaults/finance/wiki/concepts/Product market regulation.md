# Product market regulation

## Summary

Product market regulation refers to the legal, administrative, and policy frameworks that govern competition, entry conditions, and operational constraints within domestic and supranational markets. Within Belgium's 2025 macroeconomic context, targeted reforms in this area are highlighted as essential for improving firm dynamics, boosting total factor productivity, and sustaining long-term economic resilience amid fiscal consolidation pressures.

## Key Details

- Implementation of streamlined insolvency regimes to facilitate efficient market exits and accelerate resource reallocation.
- Reduction of administrative, licensing, and sector-specific regulatory barriers to improve market entry and competition.
- Integration with broader European integration initiatives, particularly the deepening of the EU single market and the advancement of a [[Capital market union]], to enhance the international competitiveness of domestic enterprises.
- Positioned as a critical component of structural reform agendas aimed at counteracting declining labor-cost competitiveness and supporting sustainable macroeconomic trajectories.

# SMEs

## Summary

Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) represent the backbone of the economy, particularly in France. They are characterized by their smaller scale compared to large corporations and are vital drivers of innovation, employment, and regional development.

## Key Details

- **Barriers to Growth:** French SMEs face significant structural hurdles, most notably late payments (reported by 58% of French SMEs) and a pervasive lack of basic digitalization.
- **Innovation Support:** There is a strategic shift toward refocusing innovation support (such as the *crédit impôt recherche*) toward SMEs and start-ups, as current mechanisms have shown limited effectiveness for large firms.
- **Productivity & Competitiveness:** SMEs are impacted by high taxes on production and the broader "skills gap," particularly in sectors requiring green and digital transitions.
- **Economic Context:** SMEs are key beneficiaries of the Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP) and are central to the goals of improving France's economic competitiveness and social fairness.

# Federal Prosecution Office

## Summary

The Federal Prosecution Office represents a proposed institutional reform in Austria aimed at ensuring prosecutorial independence from political oversight. Currently, prosecutorial functions operate under the direct instruction of the Minister of Justice with significant reporting burdens, prompting ongoing legislative efforts to establish a fully autonomous office aligned with European rule-of-law standards.

## Key Details

- Currently subject to individual instructions by the Minister of Justice, creating a direct channel for political influence over prosecutorial decisions.
- Prosecutors endure heavy administrative and reporting burdens, which complicate case management and operational efficiency.
- The 2025 government program has committed to drafting new legislation to formally separate prosecutorial supervision from political control.
- Progress toward establishing full institutional independence remains limited, with structural reform prioritized to address systemic vulnerabilities and align with EU judicial standards.
- The reform is closely tied to broader justice system modernization, including workload management, digitalisation initiatives, and anti-corruption enforcement.

# Justice System Reform

## Summary

Justice system reform encompasses legislative, administrative, and structural changes designed to improve judicial efficiency, transparency, accessibility, and institutional oversight. Recent assessments highlight ongoing modernisation efforts, including digital court integration and staffing expansions, while noting persistent challenges in procedural delays, resource allocation, and constitutional realignments.

## Key Details

- The High Council for the Judiciary is executing new oversight mandates following comprehensive administrative reforms.
- A proposed constitutional separation of judges and prosecutors is currently under parliamentary review, encountering judicial pushback.
- The Constitutional Court has validated recent structural adjustments to the tax court system.
- Ongoing magistrate recruitment aims to address persistent staffing shortages, though technical hurdles continue to delay full digitalisation of criminal courts.
- Judicial efficiency shows marginal improvement and backlog reduction, yet the length of proceedings remains a critical concern under Council of Europe supervision.

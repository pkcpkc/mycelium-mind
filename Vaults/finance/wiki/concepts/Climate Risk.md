# Climate Risk

## Summary

Climate Risk refers to the vulnerability of infrastructure, agriculture, and economic systems to extreme weather events and long-term environmental changes. In the context of the European Union and specifically Italy, it represents a significant threat to fiscal stability and social well-being due to high exposure to extreme weather.

## Key Details

- **Climate Vulnerability:** Italy is identified as one of the most exposed EU states to extreme weather, leading to substantial economic losses in infrastructure and agriculture.
- **Water Stress:** Severe water stress is a critical risk factor driven by high demand in the manufacturing and agricultural sectors, compounded by aging infrastructure and leakage.
- **Energy Transition Impacts:** High electricity prices, influenced by the gas mix and taxes, impact industrial competitiveness during the transition to cleaner energy.
- **Mitigation Context:** Risks are balanced against Italy's leadership in clean technologies such as solar PV, wind, and heat pumps.

# Education

## Summary
Education encompasses the structured processes of teaching, learning, and skill development that underpin human capital formation and social mobility. In recent macroeconomic assessments, such as the 2025 European Commission Country Report on Malta, education is identified as a critical yet strained sector. The system faces significant structural challenges that directly impact broader economic competitiveness, labour market efficiency, and social equity.

## Key Details
- **Skill Mismatches:** The education system struggles to align curricula with labour market demands, resulting in systemic skill gaps that hinder productivity and innovation.
- **Low Vocational & Adult Learning Participation:** Engagement in vocational training and lifelong learning programs remains insufficient, limiting workforce adaptability and upskilling opportunities.
- **Socioeconomic Stratification:** The school structure is highly stratified, which amplifies existing socioeconomic disparities and limits upward mobility for disadvantaged groups.
- **Policy Intervention Focus:** Targeted reforms and funding mechanisms, including the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF), are prioritizing digital upskilling and educational modernization to address these bottlenecks.

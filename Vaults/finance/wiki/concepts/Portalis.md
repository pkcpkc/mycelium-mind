# Portalis

## Summary

Portalis is a digitalisation tool deployed within the French justice system to support judicial processes and efficiency. It serves as part of broader budgetary efforts to modernize the justice sector, aiming to enhance digital capabilities despite persistent challenges related to implementation speed and technical performance.

## Key Details

- Portalis is utilized as a primary tool for digitalisation within the French justice system.
- Budgetary efforts are focused on improving digitalisation through Portalis and similar technological tools to address systemic inefficiencies.
- Implementation of Portalis has encountered challenges regarding the speed of deployment and technical functionality, which persist despite its intended benefits.
- The tool is integrated into the context of ongoing judicial reforms in France, including updates to magistrate status and initiatives to reduce case backlogs.
- Portalis is referenced in the context of efforts to enhance the efficiency of courts and improve the execution of sentences through technological means.

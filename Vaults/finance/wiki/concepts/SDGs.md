# SDGs

## Summary

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a universal call to action to end poverty, protect the planet, and ensure that by 2030 all people enjoy peace and prosperity. In the context of national reporting, such as the 2025 Country Report for France, SDGs serve as a framework for measuring progress in areas like decarbonisation, social fairness, and economic resilience.

## Key Details

- **Contextual Application:** Used as a benchmark for evaluating national progress on economic performance, fiscal sustainability, and environmental targets.
- **Decarbonisation Alignment:** Directly linked to climate goals, including France's target to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 50% by 2030.
- **Social Fairness:** Integrated into metrics for addressing poverty (AROPE rate), employment gaps for vulnerable groups, and educational equity.
- **Economic Sustainability:** Tied to the transition toward a green economy through the Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP) and REPowerEU.

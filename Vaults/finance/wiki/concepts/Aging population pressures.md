# Aging population pressures

## Summary

Demographic shifts characterized by a rising median age and a shrinking working-age population. These pressures create significant strain on public pension systems, healthcare budgets, and long-term fiscal sustainability, often requiring comprehensive policy interventions to maintain economic stability.

## Key Details

- Drives urgent fiscal consolidation needs to ensure long-term debt sustainability and counteract rising age-related public expenditures.
- Necessitates structural pension reforms, including measures to raise the effective retirement age and adjust benefit formulas.
- Exacerbates labor market challenges such as skills mismatches and fragmentation, highlighting the need for lifelong learning and flexible employment policies.
- Intersects with wage-setting mechanisms and automatic indexation, influencing inflation dynamics, unit labor costs, and international competitiveness.
- Requires coordinated macroeconomic and social policies to balance intergenerational equity with sustained economic growth.

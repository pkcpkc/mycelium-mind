# Pension Reform

## Summary

The [[Pension Reform]] enacted in France during 2023 raised the statutory retirement age to 64. Identified by the IMF as a necessary structural step, this reform aims to counter significant demographic pressures and supports broader efforts toward fiscal consolidation and debt sustainability.

## Key Details

- **Implementation Year:** 2023.
- **Core Measure:** Raised the statutory retirement age to 64.
- **Objective:** Counters demographic pressures, supports fiscal consolidation, and facilitates structural adjustment.
- **IMF Assessment:** Viewed as a necessary step to address long-term sustainability challenges within the context of high public debt and fiscal slippages.
- **Recommendations:** Further harmonization of pension rules and implementation of healthy aging policies are recommended to maximize labor market participation and equity.

# HATVP

## Summary

The **High Authority for Transparency in Public Life (HATVP)** is a French independent administrative authority tasked with safeguarding transparency and integrity in public office. It plays a central role in France's anti-corruption framework by overseeing asset declarations, managing conflicts of interest, and regulating lobbying activities.

## Key Details

- Responsible for examining asset declarations submitted by public officials and politicians to prevent conflicts of interest.
- Has significantly expanded its operational resources to manage increasing workloads, particularly in processing asset declarations and monitoring compliance.
- Operates as a key pillar alongside the Anti-Corruption Agency (AFA), OCLCIFF, and the National Financial Prosecutor (PNF) in France's broader anti-corruption strategy.
- Contributes to the implementation of new ethics measures for Members of Parliament, ministers, and magistrates.
- Supports whistleblower protection mechanisms by processing and forwarding relevant reports to competent authorities.

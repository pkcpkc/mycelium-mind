# Social Inclusion

## Summary

Social inclusion refers to the process of improving the terms on which individuals and groups take part in society. It involves ensuring that all members of a community have access to resources, opportunities, and rights, while addressing systemic barriers that lead to social exclusion or poverty.

## Key Details

- **Current Trends in Hungary:** Recent assessments indicate that poverty and social exclusion are on the rise, reversing previous positive trends.
- **Education Gaps:** Significant barriers to inclusion are identified through high rates of early school leaving and a low tertiary education attainment rate (32.3% for 25-34 year-olds).
- **Healthcare Access:** Challenges include a hospital-centric system with insufficient spending on prevention and a shortage of health professionals, which can hinder equitable access to care.
- **Housing Affordability:** Rapidly increasing house prices (the highest increase in the EU since 2010) and a shortage of social housing are major drivers of social exclusion.
- **Policy Recommendations:** Improving social dialogue with employers and trade unions, particularly in the public sector, is highlighted as a key pathway to fostering a more inclusive environment.

# Artificial Intelligence

## Summary

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a foundational technology sector increasingly recognized as a critical driver of structural economic reforms and productivity enhancement. Within recent macroeconomic assessments, it is highlighted as a key area for targeted investment, requiring streamlined innovation financing and R&D tax incentives to maximize its impact on long-term growth and industrial modernization.

## Key Details

- Central to structural reform agendas, AI development is prioritized to boost corporate productivity, address skill mismatches, and enhance economic resilience.
- Requires enhanced innovation financing mechanisms to support research, development, and commercial deployment across public and private sectors.
- Streamlining R&D tax expenditures is recommended to reduce financial barriers and accelerate AI adoption and scaling.
- Synergizes closely with the [[Green Transition]], creating pathways for sustainable industrial upgrading, resource optimization, and decarbonization.
- Capital allocation for AI initiatives is facilitated by deeper financial market integration and coordinated industrial policies, particularly through the [[EU Savings and Investment Union]].

# Lobbying Reform

## Summary

Legislative reform aimed at establishing rules for lobbying activities, which in the context of Belgium's 2025 Rule of Law Report has stalled with the new government yet to decide on draft rules.

## Key Details

- Legislative reform has stalled, with the new government yet to decide on draft rules.
- The stagnation is identified within the broader integrity and governance assessment, alongside limited progress on revolving doors regulations and whistleblower protection.
- The issue is evaluated as part of the European Commission's review of Belgium's anti-corruption framework and compliance with rule of law standards.

# Climate Change Mitigation

## Summary

Climate change mitigation encompasses coordinated policy and economic strategies designed to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and enhance environmental resilience. As highlighted in recent national economic assessments, it serves as a foundational pillar for sustainable development, requiring structural shifts in energy systems, tourism management, and public investment to balance rapid economic expansion with long-term ecological stability.

## Key Details

- Phasing out fixed [[Energy]] subsidies to redirect fiscal resources toward green investment and climate infrastructure.
- Accelerated adoption of renewable energy sources to meet ambitious national decarbonization and climate targets.
- Integration of sustainable tourism practices to mitigate the environmental footprint of high-volume visitor economies.
- Alignment with broader macroeconomic frameworks, including [[Fiscal Consolidation]] and the [[Recovery and Resilience Plan]], to secure funding for transition initiatives.
- Emphasis on building systemic environmental resilience as a counterbalance to capacity constraints driven by demographic influxes and industrial growth.

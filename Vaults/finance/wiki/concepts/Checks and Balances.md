# Checks and Balances

## Summary

Institutional checks and balances refer to the systemic mechanisms designed to prevent the concentration of power and ensure mutual oversight among state branches. In recent rule of law assessments, these mechanisms are identified as critically weakened when executive authorities bypass parliamentary oversight through emergency powers and when judicial independence is compromised by political appointment processes.

## Key Details

- Government extensively utilizes emergency powers to bypass parliamentary oversight, creating legal uncertainty for businesses and foreign investors.
- Constitutional Court independence is questioned due to a politically driven appointment process and its expanded role in reviewing ordinary court decisions.
- Structural safeguards for mutual institutional oversight are frequently undermined, leading to a concentration of executive authority.
- Ongoing EU rule of law monitoring highlights the erosion of these balances as a key indicator of democratic backsliding and institutional fragility.

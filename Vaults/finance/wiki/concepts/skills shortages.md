# Skills Shortages

## Summary
Skills shortages refer to a structural imbalance where the supply of qualified workers fails to meet economic or sectoral demand. This condition is frequently exacerbated by demographic trends, participation gaps, and mismatches between educational outputs and industry needs.

## Key Details
- Identified as a critical structural challenge within Austria's labour market, occurring alongside a continuous decline in average working hours.
- Compounded by widespread part-time work arrangements, particularly among highly educated women, and persistently low participation rates among older workers and migrant populations.
- Policy interventions to alleviate shortages include expanding high-quality early childhood education and care (ECEC) systems to reduce caregiving burdens and boost maternal labour force participation.
- Linked to broader macroeconomic constraints, as labour market rigidities and demographic shifts limit overall economic potential and productivity growth.

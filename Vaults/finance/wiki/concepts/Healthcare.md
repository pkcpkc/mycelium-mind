# Healthcare

## Summary

Healthcare refers to the organized system of services, institutions, and professionals dedicated to maintaining, restoring, and improving human health. In the context of recent regional assessments, it is characterized by structural challenges involving infrastructure balance, professional staffing, and the shift toward preventative care.

## Key Details

- **Systemic Structure:** Characterized as a hospital-centric system in certain regional contexts (e.g., Hungary), which can lead to imbalances in service delivery.
- **Preventative Care:** Identified as a critical area for improvement, with current systems often showing low spending on prevention compared to acute treatment.
- **Workforce Challenges:** Faces a notable shortage of health professionals, impacting the quality and accessibility of care.
- **Social Policy Integration:** Healthcare is a core component of social fairness, linked to broader goals of social inclusion and poverty reduction.

# Prosecutor General Powers

## Summary

The Prosecutor General holds significant authority within Slovakia's prosecutorial system, notably including the power to annul final prosecutorial decisions. This centralized control has been a focal point of concern regarding judicial independence and the effectiveness of anti-corruption efforts, especially following the dissolution of specialized anti-corruption bodies.

## Key Details

- Possesses the authority to annul final prosecutorial decisions, a mechanism frequently scrutinized for potential impacts on institutional independence and prosecutorial autonomy.
- The exercise of these powers is compounded by the dissolution of specialized anti-corruption bodies, such as the Special Prosecutor's Office and National Crime Agency, resulting in investigation delays and a notable decline in corruption case outcomes.
- Identified as a key area of institutional concern in the European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report for Slovakia.
- Intersects with broader judicial and prosecutorial reform discussions aimed at improving court efficiency, oversight mechanisms, and adherence to European rule-of-law standards.

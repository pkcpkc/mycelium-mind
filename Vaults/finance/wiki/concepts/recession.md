# recession

## Summary

A recession is a significant, widespread, and prolonged downturn in economic activity within an economy. It typically manifests as consecutive periods of negative Gross Domestic Product (GDP) growth, accompanied by rising unemployment, reduced industrial production, and declining trade. In recent macroeconomic assessments, such as the European Commission's 2025 Country Report for [[Austria]], recessions have been characterized by structural industrial weakness, persistent cost pressures, and subdued consumer demand despite potential rebounds in disposable incomes. These periods often strain public finances, necessitating fiscal consolidation while highlighting underlying structural vulnerabilities in labour markets and innovation ecosystems.

## Key Details

- **GDP Contraction:** Typically defined by two or more consecutive quarters of negative economic growth, though broader indicators like employment, industrial output, and real income are used to assess severity and duration.
- **Primary Drivers:** Often triggered by external shocks, declining exports, industrial slowdowns, and persistent inflationary or cost pressures that erode national competitiveness.
- **Consumer Behavior:** Households tend to increase precautionary savings and reduce discretionary spending, leading to subdued aggregate demand even when nominal incomes or real disposable incomes temporarily rise.
- **Fiscal Impact:** Government revenues decline while automatic stabilizers increase spending, often pushing budget deficits above sustainable thresholds and complicating [[fiscal consolidation]] efforts.
- **Structural Amplification:** Labour market rigidities, skills shortages, and limited access to risk financing can prolong downturns by constraining adaptive capacity and productivity growth.
- **Policy Response:** Recovery typically requires coordinated measures including targeted fiscal support, structural labour market reforms, regulatory streamlining, and accelerated investments in green and digital transitions to foster sustainable growth.

# Ageing Costs

## Summary

Ageing costs refer to the escalating public expenditure burden on national budgets resulting from demographic shifts, particularly pension and long-term care provisions, which are projected to drive fiscal deterioration through 2070.

## Key Details

- **Primary Components:** Driven predominantly by pension expenditures and long-term care (LTC) costs, which scale directly with an aging population and rising life expectancy.
- **Fiscal Impact:** A major contributor to Belgium's projected headline deficit of 5.4% of GDP in 2025 and the expected climb of public debt toward 126.4% by 2035.
- **Long-term Trajectory:** Forecast to surge significantly through 2070, necessitating robust multiannual fiscal planning and structural reforms to maintain sovereign debt sustainability.
- **Policy Responses:** Directly linked to planned pension system reforms, including a bonus-malus mechanism designed to incentivize later retirement and improve long-term financial sustainability.
- **Labour Market Interconnection:** Exacerbated by low labour market participation among older workers and vulnerable demographics, which reduces tax base growth while simultaneously increasing dependency on social transfers and unemployment benefits.

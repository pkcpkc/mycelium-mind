# Governance

## Summary

Governance encompasses the institutional frameworks, administrative processes, and regulatory mechanisms that direct public policy implementation and ensure accountability. In macroeconomic contexts, effective governance is a prerequisite for maintaining fiscal discipline, executing structural reforms, and securing the disbursement of international financial support.

## Key Details

- Prioritizes anti-corruption measures and institutional integrity to safeguard economic stability and investor confidence.
- Requires strengthened judicial independence to ensure consistent rule of law and contract enforcement.
- Demands improved public administration efficiency to accelerate the implementation of [[Fiscal consolidation]] and structural reform agendas.
- Weak institutional quality directly threatens the timely release of critical external financing, such as [[European Union]] recovery and cohesion funds.
- Functions as a cross-cutting enabler for addressing broader macroeconomic headwinds, including demographic shifts and labor market inefficiencies.

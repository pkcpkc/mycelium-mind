# Venice Commission

## Summary

The Venice Commission serves as a critical advisory body and oversight mechanism in European judicial and democratic reforms. It provides expert monitoring to ensure legislative changes adhere to proportionality and fundamental legal standards, issuing formal recommendations on national legislation such as Justice Laws to guide member states in aligning domestic frameworks with EU rule of law principles.

## Key Details

- Closely monitors draft legislation regarding the status, appointment, and mandates of judges and prosecutors, with specific focus on structural reforms linked to bodies like the [[National Council for the Judiciary]].
- Evaluates reform processes to ensure they maintain proportionality and uphold principles of judicial review.
- Functions as a key reference point for the European Commission and national governments in aligning domestic legal frameworks with EU rule of law standards.
- Issues formal recommendations on national legislation, including Justice Laws, to address identified gaps in judicial independence and democratic safeguards.

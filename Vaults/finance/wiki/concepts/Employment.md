# Employment

## Summary

Employment refers to the state of individuals engaged in productive economic activity, serving as a core metric for labour market health and social inclusion. In the context of the 2025 Country Report on Malta, employment is a standout area of macroeconomic resilience, with the country exhibiting one of the highest employment rates in the European Union. Despite this aggregate success, the labour market is constrained by deep-seated structural issues, including significant gender disparities, acute skills mismatches, and heightened vulnerabilities among marginalized demographic groups.

## Key Details

- **Exceptional Performance:** Malta boasts an employment rate of 83%, ranking among the highest in the EU, supported by a robust economic environment.
- **Low Unemployment:** The unemployment rate is exceptionally low at 3.1%, indicating a tight labour market with high demand for workers.
- **Gender Inequality:** The gender employment gap is among the widest in the EU, directly contributing to the persistent gender pension gap and broader social fairness concerns.
- **Vulnerable Groups:** Employment risks are disproportionately concentrated among non-EU nationals, persons with disabilities, and single-parent households.
- **Skills Mismatches:** The labour market faces bottlenecks due to a shortage of highly skilled professionals, particularly in STEM fields, exacerbated by systemic issues in education and vocational training.
- **Policy Support:** Strategic investments via the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF) and Cohesion Policy funds are directed toward digital upskilling, labour market inclusivity, and social inclusion to address these gaps.

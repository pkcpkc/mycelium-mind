# ETS II

## Summary

ETS II refers to the European Union's Emissions Trading System II, a regulatory framework targeting greenhouse gas emissions specifically from the transport and building sectors. In the context of the Slovak Republic's economic outlook, Slovakia is urged to expeditiously implement this scheme as part of its strategy to meet 2030 and 2050 climate targets.

## Key Details

- **Scope:** Covers emissions from transport and buildings.
- **Implementation Status:** Slovakia is advised to implement the scheme expeditiously.
- **Strategic Goal:** Aids in meeting national and EU climate targets for 2030 and 2050.
- **Policy Context:** Part of the broader fiscal and structural reform agenda, often discussed alongside measures to raise environmental levies.
- **Economic Impact:** Implementation contributes to the structural reforms required to address environmental challenges and align with EU directives.

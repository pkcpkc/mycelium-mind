# BICRA

## Summary
The Banking Industry Credit Risk Assessment (BICRA) is an analytical framework utilized by S&P Global Ratings to quantify contingent liability risks stemming from a domestic banking sector, which are subsequently integrated into sovereign fiscal debt burden assessments.

## Key Details
- **Primary Function:** Evaluates the vulnerability of a country's banking system to financial shocks and estimates the potential fiscal contingent liabilities (e.g., capital injections, guarantees) that could transfer from private banks to the sovereign.
- **Methodological Integration:** Findings are directly incorporated into the `[[Fiscal Assessment]]` pillar of the `[[Sovereign Rating Methodology (2017)]]`, specifically informing the `[[Debt Burden]]` component by adjusting net general government debt projections for banking sector risks.
- **Assessment Scope:** Analyzes banking sector asset quality, capitalization, profitability, and macroeconomic sensitivity to determine the likelihood and magnitude of government support requirements during periods of stress.
- **Rating Impact:** A weaker BICRA outcome can lead to a downward adjustment in sovereign credit ratings by reflecting higher implicit fiscal burdens and reduced government financial flexibility.

# Innovation and Digitalization

## Summary

Innovation and Digitalization represents the structural imperative for Malta to transition from a labor-intensive growth model to a productivity-driven economy. This concept encompasses the acceleration of innovation ecosystems, expansion of venture capital support, and comprehensive education reforms aimed at boosting STEM enrollment and digital skills. It is a focal point for fiscal reallocation, moving resources away from fixed energy subsidies toward green investment and technological advancement to ensure sustainable development.

## Key Details

- **Productivity Pivot:** Critical for overcoming structural bottlenecks and shifting the economy from reliance on foreign labor to productivity-enhancing mechanisms.
- **Venture Capital:** Report underscores the need to expand venture capital support to foster startups and high-growth enterprises.
- **Human Capital:** Requires education reform to increase STEM enrollment and enhance digital skills across the workforce.
- **Fiscal Reallocation:** Government is advised to redirect resources from fixed energy subsidies toward innovation, health services, and green investments.
- **Sustainability Link:** Innovation efforts are closely tied to climate goals, supporting renewable energy adoption and sustainable tourism practices.

# European Public Prosecutor Office

## Summary

The European Public Prosecutor Office (EPPO) is an independent EU institution tasked with combating serious crimes that threaten the financial interests of the European Union. Its full operationalization in Poland represents a pivotal development in the country's anti-corruption landscape, signaling a commitment to aligning national prosecutorial frameworks with EU standards and enhancing accountability mechanisms.

## Key Details

- Functions as a decentralized EU office with delegated members operating across participating member states to investigate, prosecute, and adjudicate crimes such as fraud, corruption, and money laundering.
- Poland's operationalization involves a major restructuring of national anti-corruption competences, including the dissolution of the Central Anti-Corruption Bureau and the redistribution of its functions to police and security agencies for coordinated action with the EPPO.
- Plays a central role in safeguarding EU funds by targeting high-risk sectors such as public procurement and political party financing, addressing previous weaknesses in accountability and impunity clauses.
- Supports the implementation of the Rule of Law Action Plan by strengthening institutional checks, improving cross-border cooperation, and ensuring proportionate judicial review in anti-corruption procedures.
- Operates alongside national authorities and the European Court of Justice to ensure consistent application of EU law and effective recovery of misused financial resources.

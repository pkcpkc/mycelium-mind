# Skills Mismatch

## Summary

Skills mismatch refers to the discrepancy between the qualifications, skills, and experience of the labor force and the specific demands of employers. It represents a structural hurdle to economic productivity, where available human capital is underutilized or misaligned with industrial needs.

## Key Details

- **Prevalence in Italy:** Italy exhibits one of the highest skills mismatch rates within the European Union.
- **Critical Sectors:** The mismatch is particularly acute in high-demand industries including:
    - Construction
    - Healthcare
    - Information and Communication Technology (ICT)
- **Root Causes:**
    - Inadequate vocational education and training (VET) systems.
    - Low commercialization of university research.
    - Rapid technological shifts outpacing the current educational curriculum.
- **Economic Impact:** Contributes to a persistent productivity gap, limits industrial competitiveness, and hinders the growth of innovative startups.

# Justice Laws

## Summary

Justice Laws encompass the legislative frameworks and ongoing structural reforms within Romania's justice system, specifically targeting judicial independence, appointment mandates, and procedural transparency. Recent developments align with external recommendations to stabilize high-level judicial roles and enhance institutional accountability, though efficiency challenges in case resolution persist.

## Key Details

- Legislative measures are currently underway to extend the mandates of high-ranking prosecutors, aimed at ensuring continuity and reducing political interference within the prosecution service.
- New statutory safeguards have been introduced to strengthen the operational independence of the judicial police.
- Transparency mechanisms for disciplinary sanctions against judicial personnel have been improved, contributing to more predictable institutional accountability.
- The Ministry of Justice is actively evaluating a centralized IT system reform, with explicit legislative commitments to preserve judicial independence during digital transformation.
- Progress on these laws reflects broader rule of law efforts, yet challenges remain regarding court backlogs and the length of civil and administrative proceedings.

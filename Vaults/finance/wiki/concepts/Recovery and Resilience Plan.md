# Recovery and Resilience Plan

## Summary

The Recovery and Resilience Plan is identified as a core entity within the [[IMF 2025 Malta Country Report - 2024 Article IV Consultation]]. It is referenced in the context of the [[International Monetary Fund]]'s assessment of [[Malta]]'s economic trajectory, fiscal consolidation plans, and strategic reforms aimed at addressing capacity constraints and ensuring sustainable growth.

- Listed as a significant entity in the IMF staff report, indicating its relevance to Malta's policy framework and economic governance.
- Contextually linked to fiscal consolidation efforts under the EU's new fiscal framework and discussions on redirecting resources toward investment and reform.
- Associated with broader goals of enhancing economic resilience, addressing structural bottlenecks, and aligning with long-term development objectives such as [[Malta Vision 2050]].
- Appears in the entity list of the IMF Executive Board consultation, reflecting its importance for monitoring macroeconomic stability and policy implementation.

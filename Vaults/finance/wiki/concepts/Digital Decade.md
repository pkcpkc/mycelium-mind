# Digital Decade

## Summary

The **Digital Decade** is a strategic framework and initiative by the European Union designed to foster a high-tech, digitalized economy. It focuses on accelerating the adoption of advanced technologies, improving digital infrastructure, and ensuring that the benefits of digitalization—such as AI, cloud analytics, and high-speed connectivity—are distributed across all sectors of society and geography.

## Key Details

- **Innovation Gap:** Current reports highlight a significant gap in Slovakia and other regions regarding the adoption of advanced digital technologies, specifically in AI and cloud analytics.
- **Infrastructure Requirements:** A critical component of the Digital Decade involves closing the digital infrastructure gap, particularly in rural areas, to meet EU targets.
- **Economic Competitiveness:** The initiative seeks to bridge the "Innovation Gap" by increasing R&D expenditure and improving the digital business environment for SMEs and startups.
- **Skills Development:** Addressing the severe shortage of ICT specialists is a core pillar for achieving the goals of the Digital Decade.
- **Policy Implementation:** Progress is often tied to the acceleration of the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRP) to fund green and digital transitions.

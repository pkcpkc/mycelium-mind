# STEM

## Summary

STEM refers to the collective fields of Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. These disciplines are fundamental to economic competitiveness, industrial innovation, and the transition toward a digital and green economy. In the context of national development, particularly within the EU, STEM is viewed as a primary driver for overcoming "talent development traps" and fostering high-tech manufacturing.

## Key Details

- **Education Reform:** Identified as a priority area for improvement due to declining basic skills (reading, math, science) among adolescents.
- **Enrolment Challenges:** Current reports highlight a need to address the low proportion of students enrolling in natural sciences.
- **Economic Impact:** STEM competencies are essential for the adoption of advanced technologies, such as AI and 5G infrastructure, which currently face adoption hurdles in small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).
- **Innovation Catalyst:** STEM fields are central to Poland's efforts in becoming a "clean tech hub," specifically in lithium battery manufacturing and renewable energy transition.

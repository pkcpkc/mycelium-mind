# ETS2

## Summary

The EU Emissions Trading System 2 (ETS2) is a market-based environmental policy mechanism designed to reduce greenhouse gas emissions, particularly from sectors not previously covered by the existing EU ETS. It serves as a critical instrument for advancing the transition to a low-carbon economy and is highlighted in macroeconomic reports as vital for sustaining long-term growth potential.

## Key Details

- Extends emissions trading to buildings and road transport sectors, complementing the existing EU ETS framework.
- Functions as a cornerstone of structural reforms aimed at modernizing economic systems and aligning with climate neutrality objectives.
- Integrated into broader fiscal and investment strategies, often evaluated alongside public expenditure reforms and green transition funding.
- Expected to enhance long-term competitiveness by internalizing carbon costs and incentivizing clean technology adoption.

# Sustainable Development Goals

## Summary
A comprehensive policy framework and benchmark used to guide national progress toward balanced economic growth, social inclusion, and environmental sustainability. In the context of EU member state assessments, these goals serve as the primary alignment target for structural reforms, fiscal policies, and major funding instruments like the Recovery and Resilience Facility. They are used to evaluate a nation's progress across economic, social, and environmental dimensions, specifically regarding the transition to a circular economy and the reduction of social exclusion. The goals are increasingly leveraged as a metric for evaluating national competitiveness, fiscal sustainability, and the successful implementation of green and digital transitions.

## Key Details
- **Environmental Sustainability & Green Transition:** Encompasses decarbonization targets, renewable energy expansion, efficient waste management, and addressing resource constraints such as water stress. Includes specific focus on the transition to a **Circular Economy** and improving **Water Resilience**.
- **Social Fairness & Inclusive Growth:** Focuses on reducing poverty and inequalities, bridging gender gaps in employment and pensions, ensuring affordable healthcare, and improving education and skills alignment. Addresses critical issues such as **Poverty**, **Healthcare** access, and the provision of social housing to combat rising social exclusion.
- **Economic Resilience & Structural Reform:** Requires modernizing business environments, streamlining judicial processes, fostering research and innovation (R&D), and optimizing fiscal consolidation. Includes the need for stable medium-term budgetary frameworks and reducing market distortions caused by excessive state intervention.
- **Funding & Policy Alignment:** Acts as a conditional metric for EU Cohesion Policy and Recovery and Resilience Facility allocations, tying financial support to measurable progress. It serves as a framework for evaluating national strategies such as the **National Battery Strategy** and **REPowerEU** progress.
- **Competitiveness and Human Capital:** Integration of goals includes addressing skills mismatches (particularly in ICT and healthcare), promoting STEM education, and improving early childhood education to foster a more competitive workforce.
- **Fiscal and Regulatory Alignment:** Implementation of the goals is tied to rebalancing tax mixes (favoring capital and environmental taxes), improving governance/anti-corruption measures, and streamlining regulatory burdens to foster a more favorable business environment.
- **National Implementation Context (France):** Used as a benchmark for evaluating specific national challenges, including the reduction of the AROPE rate (poverty), improving childcare affordability for low-income families, and accelerating the decarbonization of transport and buildings.

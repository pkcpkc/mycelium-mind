# Labor Force Participation

## Summary

Labor force participation measures the proportion of the working-age population that is either employed or actively seeking work. In the context of Romania's 2025 economic adjustment, boosting labor force participation—particularly among women—is highlighted as a vital structural reform to expand the labor supply, support sustained economic growth, and mitigate demographic pressures on public finances.

## Key Details

- **Demographic Target:** Policy efforts are specifically directed at increasing participation rates among women to maximize the available workforce and address labor shortages.
- **Growth & Fiscal Impact:** Higher participation is critical for achieving projected real GDP growth of 1.0% (2025) and 1.4% (2026), while also supporting broader fiscal consolidation goals by expanding the tax base and reducing reliance on deficit financing.
- **Structural Reform Context:** Identified alongside the acceleration of EU/NGEU fund absorption, improvements to state-owned enterprise governance, and green transition initiatives as essential pillars for long-term economic resilience.
- **Policy Recommendations:** IMF staff emphasize that unlocking this potential requires complementary measures in childcare infrastructure, skills training, and flexible labor market arrangements to reduce barriers to entry.

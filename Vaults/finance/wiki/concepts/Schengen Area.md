# Schengen Area

## Summary

The Schengen Area is a zone of European countries that have officially abolished all passport and other types of border controls at their mutual borders. It facilitates the free movement of people, goods, and services within the participating nations, forming a cornerstone of European integration.

## Key Details

- **Accession Milestones:** Romania is projected to join the Schengen Area on 2025-01-01 as part of its integration process.
- **Border Policy:** The area allows for the free movement of persons across internal borders while maintaining external border controls.
- **Economic Impact:** Facilitates trade and labor mobility, which is critical for countries transitioning toward EU productivity levels.

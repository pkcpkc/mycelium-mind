# Recovery and Resilience Facility

## Summary

The [[Recovery and Resilience Facility]] (RRF) serves as a critical financial catalyst within the European Union's recovery framework, specifically designed to support member states in addressing structural bottlenecks and advancing policy priorities. It functions primarily as a coordinated funding mechanism that channels capital into targeted national reforms, ensuring that economic recovery aligns with long-term sustainability and resilience objectives. In specific contexts, such as the 2025 Country Report for Slovakia, it is identified as a key lever for accelerating green and digital transitions.

## Key Details

- Acts as a primary investment vehicle for financing structural reforms and priority transformation projects across EU member states.
- Targets critical national challenges including green infrastructure development, digital upskilling, healthcare modernization, and social inclusion.
- Operates synergistically with other EU financial instruments, notably the Cohesion Policy funds, to maximize resource allocation and policy coherence.
- Timely execution of RRF-funded measures is considered decisive for national alignment with EU sustainability targets, labor market inclusivity, and long-term economic resilience.
- Specifically recommended to accelerate the implementation of green and digital transitions in nations facing competitiveness hurdles and infrastructure gaps.

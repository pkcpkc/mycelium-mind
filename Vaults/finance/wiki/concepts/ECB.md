# ECB

## Summary

The European Central Bank (ECB) serves as the primary monetary authority for the eurozone, coordinating interest rates and monetary policy across member states to ensure economic stability. Its policy framework heavily influences national fiscal strategies, inflation trajectories, and financial sector conditions within countries like Italy.

## Key Details

- Establishes and maintains a medium-term inflation target of 2%, which acts as the primary benchmark for price stability across eurozone economies.
- Recent macroeconomic data indicates that inflation has gradually converged toward this target, settling near 1.7% in mid-2025 following periods of volatility.
- Coordinates monetary conditions that directly impact sovereign borrowing costs, debt sustainability calculations, and the efficacy of structural reform programs.
- Engages in cross-institutional monitoring alongside international bodies like the IMF to assess macro-financial linkages, banking sector resilience, and policy alignment within member states.

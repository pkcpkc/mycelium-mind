# ARCOM

## Summary

ARCOM is France's primary media and digital services regulator. It oversees broadcasting, the internet, and media pluralism, ensuring compliance with national and EU regulations regarding content, ownership transparency, and journalists' rights.

## Key Details

- Serves as the national authority responsible for regulating audiovisual media and digital platforms.
- Tasked with expanding its regulatory scope under the EU Digital Services Act (DSA) to monitor platform accountability and content moderation.
- Actively preparing for the implementation of the European Media Freedom Act (EMFA) to safeguard editorial independence and media pluralism.
- Driving initiatives to improve media ownership transparency, including the development of a comprehensive national ownership database.
- Plays a key role in balancing public interest, commercial broadcasting, and the rights of journalists, particularly concerning access to official documents and fair compensation for press content.

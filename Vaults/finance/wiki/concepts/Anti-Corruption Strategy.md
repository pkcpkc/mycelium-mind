# Anti-Corruption Strategy

## Summary

The Anti-Corruption Strategy represents the institutional and legislative framework deployed to combat corruption within a jurisdiction. In Romania's 2025 Rule of Law Report, the strategy demonstrates a positive operational track record through bodies like the National Anti-Corruption Directorate (DNA) and the National Integrity Agency (ANI). However, it faces systemic risks from judicial rulings on the statute of limitations, stagnant progress in lobbying and political party financing regulations, and persistent corruption risks in public procurement.

## Key Details

- Operates through specialized agencies like the National Anti-Corruption Directorate (DNA), which maintains a positive conviction record, and the National Integrity Agency (ANI), which manages asset declarations and conflicts of interest.
- Faces systemic judicial risks following High Court of Cassation and Justice rulings on the statute of limitations, leading to the closure of numerous corruption cases and annulled convictions.
- Transparency challenges persist regarding asset declarations for spouses and children following Constitutional Court rulings.
- Legislative progress on lobbying regulations for Members of Parliament and political party financing transparency remains stagnant.
- Corruption risks in public procurement remain high, prompting ongoing digitalization efforts under the Recovery and Resilience Plan.

# Demographic Aging

## Summary

Demographic Aging constitutes a pronounced structural headwind, particularly observed in economies like Austria, where aging-related expenditures are projected to add roughly 2% of GDP to fiscal pressures by 2030. Effective mitigation requires expanding labor supply through increased participation of women and elderly workers, scaling childcare and eldercare infrastructure, and improving immigrant integration to offset more than two decades of demographic decline.

## Key Details

- **Fiscal Impact:** Aging-related expenditures are estimated to increase fiscal burdens by approximately 2% of GDP by 2030.
- **Labor Market Offsets:** Expanding the labor supply among women, elderly workers, and immigrants can counterbalance over two decades of demographic shrinkage.
- **Infrastructure Requirements:** Significant investment in childcare and eldercare infrastructure is necessary to support broader workforce participation.

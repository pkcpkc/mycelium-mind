# Freedom of Information Act

## Summary
Legislation designed to enhance government transparency and public access to official information. Implementation varies significantly across jurisdictions, ranging from comprehensive transparency reforms to measures that may restrict access. Notable implementations include Austria's 2025 legislation aimed at strengthening institutional accountability, and Slovakia's 2025 amendments which introduce potential access barriers.

## Key Details
- **Austrian Implementation:** Prepared and implemented as part of Austria's 2025 Rule of Law Report initiatives to strengthen institutional accountability and combat systemic corruption risks. Officially scheduled to take effect on 1 September 2025 following extensive administrative and legislative preparation. Aims to improve transparency across public administration, support journalist access to data, and reinforce public oversight of state operations. Operates alongside enhanced whistleblower channels, public procurement oversight, and reforms to political appointment transparency to create a more open civic space.
- **Slovakian Amendments (2025):** Under the EU Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report assessment, Slovakia amended its FOI framework to introduce potential paywalls for extensive information requests and extended response times, contrasting with broader transparency goals.
> [!warning] Regulatory Divergence
> While Austrian reforms actively expand access and accountability mechanisms, the Slovakian amendments restrict information retrieval through financial and temporal barriers. This highlights jurisdiction-specific tensions between administrative efficiency and public transparency rights within the EU rule-of-law framework.

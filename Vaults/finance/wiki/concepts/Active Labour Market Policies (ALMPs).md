# Active Labour Market Policies (ALMPs)

## Summary

Active Labour Market Policies (ALMPs) are government-led interventions aimed at improving the employability of individuals and reducing unemployment. Unlike passive policies (such as unemployment benefits), ALMPs focus on proactive measures to integrate people into the workforce, addressing structural issues like skills gaps and barriers to entry.

## Key Details

- **Objective**: To move the labor market toward a high-tech, green, and digital economy by aligning worker skills with modern demands.
- **Core Components**: 
    - Modernizing Public Employment Services (PES) to improve job matching.
    - Enhancing Vocational Education and Training (VET) to bridge the "skills gap."
    - Targeted support for marginalized groups, including youth, people with disabilities, and the Roma community.
- **Contextual Application (Romania)**: In the context of Romania's economic transition, ALMPs are critical to addressing high youth unemployment and the fact that 70% of adults lack basic digital skills. These policies are essential for shifting from a low-cost labor model to one driven by innovation and high-tech investment.

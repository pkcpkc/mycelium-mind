# Water Stress

## Summary

Water stress is an environmental condition where regional water demand exceeds available renewable supply. In Malta, it constitutes a critical sustainability bottleneck that directly threatens the operational viability of the agricultural, tourism, and energy sectors.

## Key Details

- **Economic & Sectoral Vulnerability:** Acute water stress poses a direct threat to Malta's core economic pillars, particularly agriculture, tourism, and energy production.
- **Infrastructure & Compliance Deficits:** The severity of the stress is exacerbated by persistent compliance gaps in wastewater treatment infrastructure across key agglomerations, limiting water recovery and circularity.
- **Policy & Environmental Context:** Recognized as a major headwind for Malta's green transition, water stress intersects with broader challenges in decarbonization, renewable energy deployment, and sustainable resource management.

# Public Broadcasting

## Summary

Public broadcasting in France constitutes the public service media sector, which is currently undergoing significant governance reform as highlighted in the 2025 EU Rule of Law Report. The proposed restructuring aims to create a holding company named France Médias, a move that has ignited debate regarding the preservation of editorial autonomy and job security, leading to calls for strikes and increased scrutiny within the broader media pluralism landscape.

## Key Details

- **Governance Restructuring:** A proposed reform seeks to reorganize public broadcasting governance by establishing a holding company structure named France Médias.
- **Editorial Autonomy Concerns:** The reform has raised concerns among stakeholders regarding potential risks to editorial independence and safeguards against external influence.
- **Job Security:** The restructuring plan has sparked debate over employment conditions, resulting in calls for strikes to protect the workforce.
- **Regulatory Context:** The reform is evaluated within the framework of media pluralism and operates under the oversight of ARCOM, which is expanding its regulatory scope under EU directives such as the Digital Services Act.
- **Legislative Process:** The reform is part of broader legislative efforts to modernize media structures, intersecting with consultations like the États généraux de l'information.

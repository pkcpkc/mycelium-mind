# Digital Transition

## Summary

Digital Transition refers to the systemic integration of digital technologies into organizational processes, public services, and economic structures. In the context of Italy's 2025 economic landscape, it is a critical lever for overcoming productivity gaps, improving public service accessibility, and fostering innovation.

## Key Details

- **Infrastructure Progress:** Italy has achieved significant milestones in connectivity, including 99.5% 5G coverage.
- **Public Services:** Implementation of the "IO" app serves as a primary vehicle for digitalizing public services and administrative procedures.
- **Administrative Reform:** Ongoing efforts to simplify administrative procedures through digitalization to improve the business environment and reduce judicial backlogs.
- **Public Procurement:** Reforms are specifically aimed at increasing transparency, speed, and digitalization in the awarding of contracts.
- **Productivity Drivers:** Digitalization is viewed as a primary tool to address the "Skills Mismatch" and low R&D expenditure (0.52% of GDP) by modernizing the industrial base.
- **Regional Challenges:** A persistent rural-urban divide remains a hurdle, particularly in the "Mezzogiorno" (South), where infrastructure quality lags behind the North.

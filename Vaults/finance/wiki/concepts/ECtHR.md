# ECtHR

## Summary

The **ECtHR** (European Court of Human Rights) is the international judicial body established to ensure member states comply with the European Convention on Human Rights. In the context of France's 2025 Rule of Law assessment, the Court is identified as a key external oversight mechanism whose pending judgments highlight ongoing challenges in domestic judicial efficiency and rights implementation.

## Key Details

- Adjudicates individual and state applications alleging violations of the European Convention on Human Rights.
- Issues binding judgments that require domestic legal, administrative, or policy adjustments by member states.
- France faces persistent challenges in implementing the Court's judgments, with a significant number of leading judgments currently pending.
- The backlog of pending cases intersects with broader concerns regarding judicial efficiency, the rule of law, and the practical protection of fundamental rights within national legal systems.
- Functions as a critical safeguard in the European human rights architecture, complementing domestic constitutional and administrative courts.

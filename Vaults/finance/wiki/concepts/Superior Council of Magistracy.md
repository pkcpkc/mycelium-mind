# Superior Council of Magistracy

## Summary
The **Superior Council of Magistracy** is the primary constitutional body in France tasked with safeguarding judicial independence and overseeing the professional management of magistrates. In recent years, it has played a critical role in defending the judiciary against external pressures and personal attacks, particularly following high-profile decisions related to national elections.

## Key Details
- **Defender of Judicial Independence:** Actively intervenes to protect magistrates from political or public pressure, notably responding to personal attacks following election-related rulings as of March 2025.
- **Role in Judicial Reform:** Serves as a central pillar in France's ongoing justice system modernization, including the implementation of the Law of 20 November 2023 reforming magistrate status.
- **Institutional Context:** Operates within a broader framework of checks and balances, working alongside the Ministry of Justice, the European Commission's rule of law monitoring, and other judicial oversight bodies.
- **Career & Evaluation Management:** While the 2023 reform introduced 360° evaluations for court presidents and repealed age limits, the Council remains the authoritative body for magistrate appointments, disciplinary actions, and career progression.

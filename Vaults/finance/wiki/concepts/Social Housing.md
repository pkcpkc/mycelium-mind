# Social Housing

## Summary

Social housing refers to housing units provided by the state, local authorities, or non-profit organizations to ensure that low-income individuals and marginalized groups have access to safe, affordable, and stable living conditions. In the context of Slovakia, it is identified as a critical area for social fairness due to high house prices relative to incomes and a severe shortage of available rental stock.

## Key Details

- **Affordability Crisis:** High house prices relative to average incomes create significant barriers to entry for low-income households.
- **Supply Shortage:** There is a severe shortage of social rental housing, limiting the options for vulnerable populations.
- **Social Fairness:** Addressing housing shortages is a key component of reducing structural inequalities and improving the quality of life for marginalized groups.
- **Policy Context:** Identified as a key area for reform within the 2025 Country Report to improve social fairness and human capital development.

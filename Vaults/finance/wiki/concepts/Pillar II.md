# Pillar II

## Summary

Pillar II refers to the EU's implementation of the OECD/G20 international tax framework, specifically focusing on corporate taxation. As highlighted in the 2024 IMF Article IV Consultation for [[Malta]], it is a critical directive for modernizing [[Corporate Income Tax]] systems. The framework aims to prevent revenue leakage to other jurisdictions by ensuring multinational enterprises contribute a fair share of taxes where economic activities occur.

## Key Details

- Serves as a foundational component for national fiscal consolidation and tax reform roadmaps.
- Requires member states to align domestic legislation with international minimum tax standards.
- Directly addresses base erosion and profit shifting by closing cross-border tax optimization loopholes.
- Mandates the development of clear legislative timelines to ensure compliance and prevent competitive tax disadvantages.
- Intersects with broader macroeconomic policy goals, including sustainable fiscal management and investor confidence.

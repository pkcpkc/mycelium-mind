# France Médias

## Summary

**France Médias** is a proposed holding company intended to reform public broadcasting governance in France. The establishment of this entity has sparked significant debate regarding editorial autonomy and job security, resulting in calls for strikes.

## Key Details

- **Structure:** Proposed as a holding company to govern public broadcasting entities.
- **Controversy:** The reform has raised concerns regarding the independence of editorial content and the security of jobs within the public broadcasting sector.
- **Social Response:** The debate has triggered calls for strikes by stakeholders opposed to the proposed changes.
- **Regulatory Context:** The reform is discussed alongside the expanded scope of **[[ARCOM]]** and preparations for the **[[European Media Freedom Act (EMFA)]]**.

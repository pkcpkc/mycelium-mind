# Fiscal Adjustment

## Summary

Fiscal adjustment refers to the policy measures and structural reforms implemented by a government to reduce its budget deficit and manage public debt. In the context of Poland's recent economic landscape, it involves a medium-term fiscal-structural plan designed to stabilize public finances and maintain competitiveness within the EU framework.

## Key Details

- **Target Objectives:** Poland has endorsed a medium-term fiscal-structural plan aimed at reducing the budget deficit to below 3% of GDP by 2028.
- **Contextual Drivers:** The need for adjustment was triggered by a rising budget deficit (reaching 5.3% of GDP in 2023), which led to the initiation of an excessive deficit procedure.
- **Key Risk Factors:** Fiscal sustainability is currently challenged by rising defense spending and the high costs of large-scale infrastructure projects.
- **Efficiency Measures:** A primary focus of adjustment includes improving the efficiency of public spending, particularly regarding social programs.
- **Policy Mechanism:** The adjustment is part of a broader strategy to balance robust GDP growth with structural fiscal health.

# Climate Transition

## Summary

A strategic policy and economic shift toward low-carbon systems, identified as vital for long-term competitiveness. It encompasses decarbonization strategies, industrial modernization, and equitable adjustments for sectors undergoing structural change.

## Key Details

- Critical for sustaining economic competitiveness and supporting medium-term convergence goals.
- Requires extended carbon pricing mechanisms to internalize environmental costs and guide investment flows.
- Necessitates accelerated permitting processes for green infrastructure and renewable energy projects.
- Involves phasing out coal dependency while implementing just transition frameworks to manage social and economic impacts on mining regions.

# Government Emergency Ordinances

## Summary

Government Emergency Ordinances are executive legislative instruments that allow the government to enact decrees with the force of law, typically invoked during declared states of emergency or urgent necessity. Within Romania's institutional landscape, their frequent deployment has been highlighted by the European Commission as a primary factor contributing to legislative unpredictability and eroding long-term regulatory stability.

## Key Details

- Enable rapid policy implementation without full parliamentary debate, often bypassing standard legislative scrutiny.
- Frequently utilized in Romania, drawing institutional criticism for altering the balance of powers and creating legal uncertainty.
- Subject to subsequent constitutional review and parliamentary ratification, though repeated use can functionally substitute for normal lawmaking processes.
- Directly linked to broader challenges in institutional governance, transparency, and the overall rule of law framework.

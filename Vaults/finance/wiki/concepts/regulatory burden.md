# Regulatory Burden

## Summary

The regulatory burden refers to the cumulative administrative costs, compliance requirements, and complex permitting procedures that impede business operations, economic dynamism, and investment. In macroeconomic assessments, it is frequently cited as a primary structural bottleneck that stifles innovation, limits SME growth, and reduces overall productivity.

## Key Details

- Characterized by protracted permitting processes and high administrative compliance costs for enterprises.
- Acts as a significant barrier to business dynamism and the translation of R&D investment into measurable productivity growth.
- Particularly restricts access to risk financing and venture capital for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).
- Streamlining regulatory frameworks is a core policy recommendation to foster competitive business environments and accelerate high-impact innovation.
- Often addressed in tandem with modernizing professional services regulations and improving access to financing.

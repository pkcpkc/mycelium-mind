# Local Government Spending

## Summary

Local government spending refers to the budgetary expenditures managed by subnational and municipal authorities. Within France's 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation, it is highlighted as a pivotal lever for fiscal consolidation. The IMF emphasizes the rationalization of these expenditures as part of a broader structural adjustment strategy, aiming to stabilize public debt and bring the deficit below 3% of GDP by 2029 without relying on further tax increases.

## Key Details

- Prioritized by IMF staff for frontloaded structural fiscal effort, alongside central government and social security spending rationalization.
- Critical to the implementation of the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP), which targets deficit reduction and sustainable debt trajectories amid a 2024 baseline debt of 113.1% of GDP.
- Subject to elevated implementation risks due to domestic political dynamics, potential revenue shortfalls, and the decentralized structure of French public administration.
- Part of a balanced consolidation approach designed to preserve economic growth while addressing high and rising public debt.

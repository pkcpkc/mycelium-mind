# Tourism

## Summary

Tourism is a foundational export-oriented service sector that has been a primary driver of Malta's robust economic expansion over the past decade. It has significantly contributed to GDP growth and the convergence of GDP per capita with the EU average, though its rapid scale has introduced capacity constraints that strain local infrastructure and public services.

## Key Details

- **Economic Engine**: Alongside online gaming, tourism anchors Malta's service-based export economy, fueling historical double-digit growth peaks and sustaining medium-term outperformance relative to broader European averages.
- **Capacity & Infrastructure Pressure**: High volumes of tourist arrivals have intensified demand on housing, transport, utilities, and public services, highlighting structural bottlenecks that limit further labor-intensive growth.
- **Sustainability Imperative**: To balance continued economic gains with environmental resilience, the sector requires a strategic shift toward sustainable tourism practices aligned with national climate change mitigation targets.
- **Macroeconomic Interdependencies**: Tourism flows directly influence broader economic indicators, including wage dynamics, non-EU labor migration patterns, and inflation trends, making it a key variable in macroprudential and fiscal planning.

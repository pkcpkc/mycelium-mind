# National Recovery and Resilience Plan

## Summary

The National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP) is Italy's primary framework for allocating and executing funds from the European Union's NextGenerationEU program. Designed to accelerate structural reforms, modernize public infrastructure, and enhance human capital, the NRRP serves as a critical driver of short-to-medium term economic growth and long-term productivity gains.

## Key Details

- **Funding & Framework:** Financed through a combination of EU grants and loans under the NextGenerationEU initiative, structured around six core investment pillars including digital/eco transitions, cohesion, productivity, and health.
- **Economic Impact:** Direct infrastructure spending and investment flows have been a key support for GDP growth (e.g., contributing to 0.7% real growth in 2024) and peak spending periods directly influence near-term macroeconomic projections.
- **Structural Reforms:** Implementation is explicitly tied to mandate-driven reforms aimed at streamlining public administration, improving judicial efficiency, and upgrading the labor market.
- **Growth & Productivity:** Timely execution is vital to unlock human capital development, attract private investment, and offset demographic headwinds. Conversely, implementation delays or inefficiencies pose significant downside risks to productivity and fiscal consolidation efforts.
- **Fiscal & Debt Context:** While NRRP funds do not directly increase the sovereign debt burden (as they are largely grants), they enable necessary public investments without compromising fiscal targets. However, the plan's success in boosting potential growth is essential for placing Italy's high public debt trajectory on a sustainable downward path.

# Fiscal Framework

## Summary

A structural system for multiannual fiscal planning and budgetary governance that guides medium-term economic trajectories, public finance management, and institutional credibility. In the context of Belgium's 2025 assessment, the framework requires strengthening as it currently lacks a fully developed national multiannual planning system, risking deviations from medium-term fiscal trajectories and undermining policy credibility.

## Key Details

- Requires a fully developed multiannual fiscal planning system at the national level to ensure adherence to medium-term budgetary targets and prevent trajectory deviations.
- Serves as a foundational mechanism for maintaining institutional credibility and aligning short-term policy decisions with long-term sustainability goals.
- Belgium remains the only EU member state without a fully developed multiannual fiscal planning system, creating structural risks for public finance management.
- Directly intersects with ageing costs, tax and benefit reforms, and broader social fairness metrics to determine overall fiscal sustainability.
- Urgent governance streamlining across federal and regional levels is necessary to operationalize effective multiannual planning and RRP implementation.

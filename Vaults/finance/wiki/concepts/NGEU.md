# NGEU

## Summary

NGEU serves as a critical financing mechanism for accelerated investments in Romania, directly supporting economic recovery and growth projections. The effective deployment of NGEU resources is intertwined with the broader effort to unlock stalled EU funds, requiring robust implementation and structural reforms to maximize impact on fiscal stability and development.

## Key Details

- NGEU-funded investments are projected to underpin Romania's gradual economic recovery, contributing to GDP growth estimates of 1.0% in 2025 and 1.4% in 2026 following a sluggish 0.9% growth in 2024.
- Accelerating the absorption and utilization of NGEU funds is highlighted as essential for unlocking broader EU financial resources and stimulating domestic demand.
- Structural improvements in public investment management are emphasized to ensure efficient execution of NGEU-backed projects and prevent bottlenecks in fund deployment.

# Rule of Law Action Plan

## Summary

The Rule of Law Action Plan is a targeted European Commission framework established to address systemic rule of law deficiencies in Poland. It outlines specific legislative and institutional reforms aimed at restoring judicial independence, strengthening anti-corruption mechanisms, ensuring media pluralism, and reinforcing democratic checks and balances in full alignment with EU standards.

## Key Details

- **Judicial Reform Implementation:** Central to the plan is resolving the legal status of judges appointed under the post-2017 National Council for the Judiciary, with ongoing draft legislation subject to Venice Commission oversight to ensure proportionality and judicial review.
- **Institutional Restructuring:** Includes measures to dismantle controversial bodies such as the Chamber of Extraordinary Control and Public Affairs, directly supporting Supreme Court independence.
- **Cross-Pillar Integration:** Extends beyond the justice sector to encompass anti-corruption restructuring (e.g., full operationalization of the European Public Prosecutor Office), media regulatory reforms under the European Media Freedom Act, and whistleblower protection legislation.
- **Monitoring & Compliance:** Progress is systematically tracked through the annual Rule of Law Report, with implementation coordinated alongside EU infringement procedures and institutional recalibration efforts.
- **Democratic Safeguards:** Aims to improve civic space ratings and strengthen fundamental rights through enhanced legislative consultation rules, Ombudsperson coordination, and the gradual implementation of European Court of Human Rights judgments.

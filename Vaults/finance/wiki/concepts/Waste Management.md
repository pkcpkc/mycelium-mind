# Waste Management

## Summary

Waste management encompasses the systematic collection, transport, treatment, and disposal of waste materials. In the context of the 2025 Country Report on Malta, waste management is identified as a critical environmental challenge, with infrastructure and performance metrics lagging behind European Union benchmarks. The sector is characterized by inefficient waste generation patterns and insufficient recycling capabilities, necessitating targeted interventions to align with EU sustainability standards.

## Key Details

- **Infrastructure Deficiencies:** Waste management infrastructure in Malta falls short of EU benchmarks, indicating gaps in the capacity and efficiency of collection and processing systems.
- **High Waste Generation:** The nation exhibits high per-capita waste generation rates, straining existing infrastructure and disposal mechanisms.
- **Low Recycling Rates:** Recycling performance remains low, reflecting limited success in material recovery and circular economy implementation relative to regional expectations.

# Energy Security

## Summary

Energy security refers to the uninterrupted and affordable availability of energy resources, serving as a foundational element for macroeconomic stability and national resilience. In contemporary policy frameworks, it is closely integrated with climate risk mitigation and structural economic reforms to reduce dependency on volatile external supply chains.

## Key Details

- **Macroeconomic Priority:** Recognized as a macro-critical factor for sustaining growth, particularly in economies navigating global trade tensions, regional conflicts, and supply chain vulnerabilities.
- **Renewable Transition:** Accelerating the shift toward renewable energy sources is deemed essential to reduce exposure to fossil fuel price shocks and enhance long-term affordability.
- **Infrastructure Resilience:** Requires targeted investment in modernized, flexible, and climate-resilient energy infrastructure to withstand extreme weather events and demand fluctuations.
- **Policy Coordination:** Must be aligned with broader structural reforms, fiscal consolidation, and climate adaptation strategies to ensure sustainable development without exacerbating public debt pressures.

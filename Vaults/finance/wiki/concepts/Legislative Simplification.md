# Legislative Simplification

## Summary

Legislative simplification refers to targeted initiatives aimed at streamlining, consolidating, and reducing the complexity of existing laws and regulations. In the context of France's 2025 Rule of Law Report, these efforts are generally welcomed by the business community for improving regulatory clarity and operational efficiency. However, they also raise persistent concerns regarding the adequacy of stakeholder consultation and the increased reliance on accelerated legislative procedures to enact reforms.

## Key Details

- **Business Reception:** Corporate actors and enterprises generally support simplification initiatives for reducing compliance burdens, lowering administrative costs, and improving the overall regulatory environment.
- **Consultation Concerns:** Despite positive economic reception, stakeholders and watchdogs express concerns regarding the depth, timing, and inclusivity of consultation processes preceding legislative changes.
- **Accelerated Procedures:** The growing use of accelerated parliamentary procedures to pass simplification measures has sparked debate over potential trade-offs in legislative scrutiny, democratic oversight, and policy quality.
- **Rule of Law Context:** Evaluated within broader checks and balances frameworks, legislative simplification intersects with administrative efficiency, transparency, and the principle of proper law-making procedures under EU rule of law monitoring.

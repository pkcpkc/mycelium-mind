# National Integrity Agency

## Summary

The National Integrity Agency (ANI) is a Romanian public institution tasked with managing asset declarations, verifying conflicts of interest, and ensuring transparency in the exercise of public functions. It serves as a foundational component of Romania's anti-corruption infrastructure by monitoring compliance with integrity standards for public officials and civil servants.

## Key Details

- Manages the national database of asset declarations and monitors ongoing compliance with integrity regulations for public office holders.
- Operates as a key enforcement body within the [[Anti-Corruption Framework]], providing administrative oversight that complements criminal investigations.
- Faces ongoing transparency and operational challenges following a [[Constitutional Court]] ruling that modified the scope of asset declaration requirements for spouses and children of public officials.
- Contributes to broader institutional governance and [[Public Procurement]] transparency efforts under EU monitoring and technical assistance programs.

# Demographic challenges

## Summary

Demographic challenges refer to structural shifts in population composition, such as aging populations and shrinking working-age cohorts, that create significant headwinds for economic growth, labor market stability, and fiscal sustainability. In recent macroeconomic assessments, these challenges are highlighted as key structural impediments that necessitate coordinated policy responses to maintain productivity and balance public finances.

## Key Details

- **Aging Population & Labor Shortages:** Persistent demographic aging constrains labor supply, acting as a major structural headwind that limits economic expansion and increases dependency ratios.
- **Targeted Labor Force Expansion:** Policy frameworks must prioritize boosting labor force participation among women and the elderly to offset shrinking working-age populations and mitigate output gaps.
- **Migrant Integration Strategies:** Streamlining the integration of migrant workers is recommended as a critical mechanism to alleviate immediate labor market tightness and support economic resilience.
- **Pension System Sustainability:** Demographic pressures directly strain public pension systems, requiring comprehensive structural reforms to ensure long-term fiscal viability without compromising retirement security.
- **Fiscal & Structural Linkages:** Addressing demographic challenges is intrinsically linked to broader fiscal consolidation and structural reform agendas, as workforce dynamics directly influence potential growth rates and budgetary planning.

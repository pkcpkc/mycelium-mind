# R&D

## Summary

Research and Development (R&D) refers to the systematic activities undertaken by organizations, governments, and private entities to generate new knowledge, develop new products, improve existing processes, or create new technologies. It serves as a primary driver of innovation and long-term economic growth.

## Key Details

- **Economic Impact:** R&D is a critical component of a nation's innovation capacity; however, investment levels vary significantly across different regions.
- **Current Status in Hungary:** Public R&D spending is currently among the lowest in the European Union, at approximately 0.4% of GDP.
- **Challenges:** Low R&D investment can lead to stagnation in fundamental research and a decline in scientific excellence, potentially hindering labor productivity and technological competitiveness.
- **Strategic Importance:** Effective R&D is essential for transitioning to new economies, including the green energy transition and digital transformation.
- **Regional Innovation Gap:** A significant gap exists between Slovakia and other EU countries regarding R&D expenditure and the adoption of advanced digital technologies (such as AI and cloud analytics).
- **Competitiveness Link:** Insufficient R&D investment contributes to an innovation gap that hinders labor productivity and limits the ability to compete in high-tech markets.

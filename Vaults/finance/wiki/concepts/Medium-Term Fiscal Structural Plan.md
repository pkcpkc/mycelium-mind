# Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan

## Summary

The Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP) is a seven-year fiscal adjustment framework outlined by the Italian authorities. It aims to consolidate public finances and place Italy's persistently high [[Public Debt]] on a sustainable downward path. While it establishes the overarching trajectory for fiscal consolidation, IMF staff have cautioned that the plan currently lacks fully identified underlying measures to adequately counteract adverse interest-growth differentials and long-term demographic spending pressures.

## Key Details

- **Duration & Scope**: Outlines a gradual, seven-year fiscal adjustment trajectory aligned with Italy's macroeconomic stabilization goals.
- **Fiscal Targets**: Designed to support the achievement of an additional primary surplus of 3% of GDP by 2027, working alongside [[Tax Compliance]] improvements and the rationalization of [[Tax Expenditures]].
- **Structural Integration**: Intended to operate in tandem with broader [[Structural Reforms]] and infrastructure investments from the [[National Recovery and Resilience Plan]] to enhance long-term growth potential and offset demographic headwinds.
- **IMF Assessment**: Staff note that without concrete, identified underlying measures and efficiency gains, the plan may struggle to offset high debt levels driven by persistent interest-growth differentials and [[Population Aging]]-related fiscal pressures.
- **Implementation Context**: Functions within a broader policy ecosystem that includes monetary conditions set by the [[ECB]], financial sector stability measures, and coordinated EU-level industrial and climate strategies.

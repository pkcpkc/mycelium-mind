# NGEU Funds

## Summary

The NextGenerationEU (NGEU) Funds represent a major European Union recovery instrument that Poland has actively absorbed to stimulate domestic demand and support economic recovery. While these financial inflows currently bolster growth and investment, their temporary nature implies that medium-term economic expansion will eventually moderate once absorption peaks.

## Key Details

- Absorption of NGEU Funds is a primary driver supporting Poland's projected economic rebound in 2025.
- Inflows are temporary; medium-term growth is expected to normalize slightly below 3% as fund disbursements wind down.
- Funds are strategically allocated alongside broader fiscal consolidation and structural reform priorities, including climate transition initiatives and labor market integration.
- Effective management of these inflows requires coordination with monetary policy and fiscal consolidation to prevent overheating and maintain financial sector stability.

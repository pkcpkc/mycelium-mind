# Tax Compliance

## Summary

Tax compliance refers to the adherence to tax laws and regulations by entities and individuals to ensure accurate reporting and payment of taxes. In the context of Italy's 2025 Article IV Consultation, improved tax compliance was identified as a primary driver of strong revenue collection, enabling the government to reduce the headline deficit and achieve a fiscal surplus.

## Key Details

- **Revenue Generation:** Improved compliance contributed significantly to strong revenue collection in Italy during 2024, supporting a primary surplus of 0.4% of GDP.
- **Fiscal Consolidation:** Enhanced compliance played a crucial role in shrinking Italy's headline deficit to 3.4% of GDP, aiding broader stabilization efforts.
- **Debt Sustainability:** Strengthening compliance is essential to placing Italy's high public debt (135.3% of GDP) on a firmly downward path, with IMF staff recommending sustained consolidation efforts.
- **Structural Recommendations:** The IMF advises rationalizing tax expenditures, improving collection efficiency, and phasing out unproductive incentives to maintain fiscal discipline and achieve an additional primary surplus of 3% of GDP by 2027.

# Climate Risks

## Summary

Climate risks represent macro-critical downside threats to Italy's economic outlook, characterized by climate-related shocks that could undermine growth and stability. The IMF prioritizes mitigating these risks through the acceleration of renewable energy transitions and investments in resilient infrastructure, which are essential for enhancing energy security and long-term economic resilience.

## Key Details

- **Macro-Critical Risk Classification:** Climate-related shocks are flagged as significant downside risks to the near-term growth outlook, potentially offsetting positive drivers such as NRRP infrastructure spending and favorable trade spillovers.
- **Mitigation Priorities:** Accelerating the transition to renewable energy and investing in resilient infrastructure are deemed macro-critical priorities to address climate vulnerabilities.
- **Energy Security Integration:** Mitigation strategies are closely linked to strengthening energy security, reducing exposure to external shocks and market volatility.
- **Infrastructure Resilience:** Future infrastructure investments must incorporate climate resilience to protect economic assets and ensure sustainable development.

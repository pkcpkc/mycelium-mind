# Constitutional Tribunal

## Summary

The Constitutional Tribunal is Poland's highest constitutional court. As of 2025, it is embroiled in a deepening institutional crisis where the executive and legislative branches refuse to recognize its legitimacy, decline to publish its rulings, and block the appointment of new judges. This standoff has triggered an ongoing infringement procedure at the Court of Justice of the European Union, highlighting severe challenges to judicial independence and constitutional order in Poland.

## Key Details

- The executive and legislative branches systematically refuse to recognize the Tribunal's legitimacy.
- State authorities continue to withhold the publication of its rulings, undermining their legal effect.
- The appointment process for new judges is blocked, creating a severe operational and staffing crisis.
- The unresolved status of the Tribunal has triggered an ongoing infringement procedure at the Court of Justice of the EU.
- The crisis is a central pillar in Poland's broader rule of law challenges and judicial independence debates.

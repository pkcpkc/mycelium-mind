# Investor Citizenship Scheme

## Summary

The Investor Citizenship Scheme is a policy mechanism that allows non-citizens to obtain Maltese nationality through substantial financial contributions or investments. The program has drawn intense scrutiny from European Union institutions regarding its compliance with EU treaties, fundamental rights principles, and democratic accountability standards.

## Key Details

- The European Court of Justice ruled against the scheme in April 2025, declaring it a violation of EU law.
- The ruling represents a major legal and political setback for Malta's naturalization and immigration policies.
- The scheme remains heavily scrutinized within the broader context of institutional checks, balances, and anti-corruption frameworks.
- Ongoing legal debates focus on aligning citizenship acquisition processes with European judicial standards and transparency requirements.

# Corporate Income Tax

## Summary
Corporate income tax is a direct fiscal levy imposed on the net profits of incorporated entities. In Malta’s economic framework, it serves as a vital revenue stream that requires structural alignment with international tax standards to sustain fiscal consolidation and prevent profit shifting.

## Key Details
- **Pillar II Compliance:** A clear reform roadmap is necessary to align Malta’s corporate tax regime with the EU’s Pillar II directive, which establishes global minimum taxation rules.
- **Revenue Leakage Prevention:** Structural adjustments aim to close loopholes that allow multinational enterprises to shift profits to lower-tax jurisdictions, ensuring fair domestic tax contributions.
- **Fiscal Consolidation Support:** Optimizing corporate tax collection directly supports macroeconomic stability, freeing resources for public investments in health, innovation, and the green transition.
- **International Harmonization:** Reforms require strict adherence to OECD/G20 BEPS frameworks and EU state aid regulations to maintain investor confidence and regulatory compliance.

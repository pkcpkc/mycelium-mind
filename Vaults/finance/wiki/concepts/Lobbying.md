# Lobbying

## Summary

Lobbying is a concept referenced in the 2025 Rule of Law Report for France, positioned within the report's assessment of transparency, anti-corruption, and checks and balances. The report links lobbying oversight to the **[[High Authority for Transparency in Public Life (HATVP)]]** and highlights the implementation of ethics measures for public officials.

## Key Details

- Oversight mechanisms are associated with the **[[High Authority for Transparency in Public Life (HATVP)]]**, which has expanded its resources to manage increased workloads, including the examination of asset declarations.
- New ethics measures have been adopted for Members of Parliament, ministers, and magistrates to reinforce integrity standards.
- The concept is connected to the broader anti-corruption framework, where increasing whistleblower reports have prompted calls for improved support and protection.
- Legislative consultation processes are relevant to lobbying dynamics; however, the report notes persistent concerns regarding the consultation process and the use of accelerated legislative procedures.

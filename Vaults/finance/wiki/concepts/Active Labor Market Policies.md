# Active Labor Market Policies

## Summary

Active Labor Market Policies (ALMPs) refer to a set of interventions aimed at improving the functioning of labor markets, enhancing workforce skills, and facilitating employment. In the context of the Slovak Republic, the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation highlights the expansion of ALMPs as a critical structural reform to counter labor shortages, mitigate demographic pressures, and support long-term productivity growth and economic diversification.

## Key Details

- **Slovak Context:** The IMF stresses the importance of expanding ALMPs to address headwinds such as labor shortages and an aging population, which threaten productivity growth.
- **Economic Goals:** ALMPs are recommended to help diversify the economy beyond its current reliance on specific sectors and to sustain productivity growth.
- **Integration with Reforms:** ALMPs are positioned alongside other structural measures, including accelerating innovation and deepening integration with the European single market to foster cross-border capital flows.
- **Demographic Alignment:** The policies are part of a broader strategy to boost labor force participation and adapt to demographic challenges, including the integration of migrants and vulnerable groups.

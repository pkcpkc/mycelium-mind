# Venture Capital Fund

## Summary

A venture capital fund is a pooled investment vehicle that provides equity financing to early-stage, high-potential startups and small businesses in exchange for ownership stakes. Within the context of [[Malta]]'s 2024 economic strategy, expanding venture capital support is highlighted as a critical structural reform to foster entrepreneurship, reduce reliance on labor-intensive growth models, and drive long-term productivity.

## Key Details

- Serves as a primary funding mechanism for innovative startups and SMEs that lack access to traditional banking credit due to high risk or asset-light business models.
- Identified by the [[International Monetary Fund]] as a necessary expansion area to accelerate [[Innovation and Digitalization]] and support the structural shift toward a knowledge-based economy.
- Functions in tandem with educational and labor market reforms to ensure a steady pipeline of skilled talent, particularly in STEM and digital fields.
- Contributes to broader macroeconomic resilience by diversifying the national funding landscape and encouraging high-value, export-oriented service sectors.
- Requires supportive regulatory frameworks, stable fiscal policies, and robust anti-money laundering standards to attract both domestic and foreign institutional investors.

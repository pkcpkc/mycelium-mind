# Neighbouring Rights

## Summary

Neighbouring Rights refer to a legal concept and proposed legislative bill in France aimed at guaranteeing fair economic compensation for press publishers and journalists when their content is accessed, aggregated, or reused by digital platforms. This concept is highlighted within the 2025 Rule of Law Report as a key component of France's efforts to modernize media governance and protect the economic sustainability of traditional journalism.

## Key Details

- **Legislative Initiative**: A specific bill on neighbouring rights is being drafted to establish mandatory compensation mechanisms for digital platforms that utilize press content.
- **Regulatory Alignment**: The initiative operates under the oversight of [[ARCOM]] and complements broader European digital frameworks, including the [[Digital Services Act (DSA)]] and the [[European Media Freedom Act (EMFA)]].
- **Core Objectives**: Seeks to balance the rapid expansion of digital content distribution with the financial viability of newsrooms, thereby supporting journalistic independence and quality reporting.
- **Interconnected Reforms**: Functions alongside other media sector measures such as improved [[Media Ownership Transparency]], public broadcasting governance reforms, and protections for the [[Right to Information]].

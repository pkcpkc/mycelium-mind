# National Bank of Romania

## Summary
The National Bank of Romania (NBR) serves as the country's central banking institution, tasked with issuing currency, formulating monetary policy, and maintaining financial stability. Operating under Governor Mugur Isarescu, the NBR has adopted a cautious monetary stance amid persistent inflation and widening twin deficits, pausing rate cuts to maintain the benchmark policy rate at 6.5%. Its policy trajectory is closely aligned with IMF recommendations to prioritize inflation moderation and gradually enhance exchange rate flexibility.

## Key Details
- **Monetary Policy Stance:** Maintained a restrictive and cautious approach in 2025, halting recent easing cycles to counter headline inflation that surged to 9.9% following electricity price cap removals and tax adjustments.
- **Benchmark Interest Rate:** Held steady at 6.5% to anchor inflation expectations, stabilize the leu, and support broader macroeconomic adjustment efforts.
- **Leadership:** Directed by Governor Mugur Isarescu, who oversees the bank's response to fiscal consolidation pressures and structural economic reforms.
- **IMF Policy Recommendations:** Staff reports advise resuming rate cuts only after sustained moderation in inflation and nominal wage growth, while advocating for a gradual increase in exchange rate flexibility to bolster competitiveness and external resilience.
- **Financial Stability Oversight:** Monitors systemic risks including the sovereign-bank nexus, sizable unhedged foreign exchange corporate loans, and rapid consumer credit expansion, implementing macroprudential tools as necessary.

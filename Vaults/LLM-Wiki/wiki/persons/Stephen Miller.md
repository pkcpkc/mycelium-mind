## Biography & Context

Stephen Miller is a researcher who collaborated with [[Andrej Karpathy]] and [[Fei-Fei Li]] at the Stanford Vision Lab. He co-authored the paper **Object Discovery in 3D scenes via Shape Analysis** (ICRA 2013) with Karpathy and Li, focusing on 3D scene understanding through shape-based analysis.

# Justin Johnson

## Affiliations / Roles

- Assistant Professor, UC Berkeley (former)
- Researcher (former), Stanford University

## Biography & Context

Justin Johnson is a computer vision researcher known for his work on image captioning, neural style transfer, and visual recognition. He collaborated with Andrej Karpathy on extending neuraltalk2 to dense captioning, producing the DenseCap project published as a CVPR 2016 oral paper.

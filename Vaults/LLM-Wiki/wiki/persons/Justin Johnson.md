# Justin Johnson

## Affiliations/Roles

- AI researcher (affiliated with Stanford University)

## Biography & Context

Justin Johnson is an AI researcher who collaborated extensively with [[Andrej Karpathy]] and [[Fei-Fei Li]]. He co-authored several influential papers including DenseCap (CVPR 2016 Oral), Visualizing and Understanding Recurrent Networks (ICLR 2016 Workshop), and Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments for Generating Image Descriptions (CVPR 2015 Oral).

He also co-developed [[NeuralTalk2]], an early image captioning project, and collaborated on dense captioning research with Karpathy and Li.

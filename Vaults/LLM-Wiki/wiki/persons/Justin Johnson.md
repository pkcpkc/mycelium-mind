# Justin Johnson

## Affiliations & Roles

- AI Researcher at [[Stanford University]]

## Biography & Context

Justin Johnson is an AI researcher recognized for his contributions to computer vision and deep learning. He co-authored foundational research on image captioning, visual-semantic alignment, and recurrent neural networks alongside [[Andrej Karpathy]] and [[Fei-Fei Li]]. His work has significantly advanced the development of vision-language models and contributed to the broader ecosystem of deep learning education and open-source tooling.

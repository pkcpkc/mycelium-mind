# Diederik P. Kingma

## Affiliations & Roles

- Co-creator of the Adam optimization algorithm (with [[Andrew Ng]])
- Researcher in deep learning and generative models
- Co-author on PixelCNN++ (ICLR 2017)

## Biography & Context

Diederik P. Kingma is an AI researcher known for his contributions to deep learning optimization and generative modeling. He is best known for co-creating the Adam optimization algorithm, one of the most widely used optimizers in deep learning training. His research spans variational autoencoders, normalizing flows, and autoregressive generative models.

Kingma co-authored "PixelCNN++: A PixelCNN Implementation with Discretized Logistic Mixture Likelihood and Other Modifications" (ICLR 2017) with Andrej Karpathy, Tim Salimans, Xi Chen, and Yaroslav Bulatov. This paper improved upon the PixelCNN model for image generation by introducing a discretized logistic mixture likelihood and other modifications.

# Aditya Khosla

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher at [[Stanford University]] (Vision Lab / Stanford Vision Lab)

## Biography & Context

Aditya Khosla is a computer vision researcher known for his contributions to large-scale visual recognition benchmarks. He was part of [[Li Fei-Fei]]'s research group at the Stanford Vision Lab, where he co-authored the **ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge** paper published in the Journal of Machine Learning Research (JCV) in 2015. This paper introduced the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC), which became one of the most influential benchmarks in deep learning and computer vision.

Khosla's work on ImageNet helped establish the paradigm of large-scale supervised learning that drove rapid progress in [[Convolutional Neural Networks]] and deep visual recognition.

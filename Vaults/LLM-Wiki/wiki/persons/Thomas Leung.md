# Thomas Leung

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher at Stanford Vision Lab

## Biography & Context

Thomas Leung is a researcher affiliated with the Stanford Vision Lab. He is best known for co-authoring the paper **Large-Scale Video Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks** (CVPR 2014, Oral) alongside Andrej Karpathy, George Toderici, Sanketh Shetty, Rahul Sukthankar, and Fei-Fei Li. This work contributed to the growing body of evidence that CNNs could be effectively applied to video understanding at scale, a topic that was gaining significant traction in the computer vision community during that period.

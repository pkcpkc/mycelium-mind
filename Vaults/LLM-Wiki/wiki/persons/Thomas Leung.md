# Thomas Leung

## Affiliations/Roles

- Researcher
- Co-author on Large-Scale Video Classification paper

## Biography & Context

[[Thomas Leung]] was a co-author with [[Andrej Karpathy]], [[George Toderici]], [[Sanketh Shetty]], [[Rahul Sukthankar]], and [[Fei-Fei Li]] on the paper *Large-Scale Video Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks* (CVPR 2014, Oral). This was one of Karpathy's earliest oral presentations at CVPR.

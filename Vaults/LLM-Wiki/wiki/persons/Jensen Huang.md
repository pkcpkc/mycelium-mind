# Jensen Huang

## Affiliations & Roles

- Co-founder and CEO at [[NVIDIA]]

## Biography & Context

Jensen Huang is the co-founder and CEO of NVIDIA, a semiconductor company that has become one of the most influential forces in modern artificial intelligence through its design and manufacture of graphics processing units (GPUs). Under his leadership, NVIDIA's GPUs became the foundational hardware for deep learning training and inference, making the company central to the AI boom of the 2010s and 2020s.

Huang has been a prominent figure at major AI conferences, including delivering a keynote at NVIDIA GTC 2015 where he appeared alongside [[Andrej Karpathy]].

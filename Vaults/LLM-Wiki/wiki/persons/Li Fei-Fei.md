# Li Fei-Fei

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher & Advisor at [[Stanford University]]

## Biography & Context

Li Fei-Fei is a prominent AI researcher and academic figure known for her foundational contributions to [[Computer Vision]] and [[Deep Learning]]. During her tenure at [[Stanford University]], she served as the primary PhD advisor to [[Andrej Karpathy]], guiding his academic development and early research in neural networks. She has also actively collaborated with Karpathy as a co-author on multiple publications, further advancing the field through shared research efforts and mentorship.

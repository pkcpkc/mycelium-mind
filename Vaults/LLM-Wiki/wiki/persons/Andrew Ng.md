# Andrew Ng

## Affiliations & Roles

- Adjunct Professor at [[Stanford University]]
- Co-founder & CEO at DeepLearning.AI
- Co-founder at Coursera

## Biography & Context

Andrew Ng is a leading AI researcher and educator known for his contributions to [[Machine Learning]], [[Deep Learning]], and online education. He earned his PhD from UC Berkeley and later joined [[Stanford University]], where he collaborated extensively with faculty including [[Fei-Fei Li]], [[Daphne Koller]], and [[Sebastian Thrun]]. His work at Stanford helped shape foundational AI curricula, including courses that directly influenced researchers like [[Andrej Karpathy]].

Ng co-founded [[Coursera]] and [[DeepLearning.AI]], creating massively open online courses that democratized access to AI education globally. His research has significantly advanced the fields of [[Computer Vision]], [[Natural Language Processing]], and [[Reinforcement Learning]]. He has maintained a strong collaborative network within the AI community, frequently co-presenting educational content and research with peers such as [[Andrej Karpathy]], [[Pieter Abbeel]], and [[Christopher D. Manning]].

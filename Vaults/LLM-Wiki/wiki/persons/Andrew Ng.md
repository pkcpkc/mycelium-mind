# Andrew Ng

## Affiliations/Roles

- Professor (affiliated with Stanford University)
- AI researcher and educator

## Biography & Context

Andrew Ng is a well-known AI researcher and educator who served as one of [[Andrej Karpathy]]'s advisors during Karpathy's PhD at Stanford. He also collaborated with Karpathy on the paper "Grounded Compositional Semantics for Finding and Describing Images with Sentences" (TACL 2013).

Ng has been a significant figure in AI education and research, and Karpathy attended his classes and reading groups during his PhD rotation program at Stanford.

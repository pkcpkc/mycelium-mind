# George Toderici

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher involved in computer vision and video classification research

## Biography & Context

George Toderici is a researcher known for his work in computer vision and deep learning. He co-authored the paper **Large-Scale Video Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks** (CVPR 2014, Oral) with Andrej Karpathy, Sanketh Shetty, Thomas Leung, Rahul Sukthankar, and Li Fei-Fei.

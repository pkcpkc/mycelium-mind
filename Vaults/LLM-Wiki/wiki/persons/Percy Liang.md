# Percy Liang

## Affiliations / Roles

- Professor, Stanford University
- Associate Chair, Computer Science Department, Stanford

## Biography & Context

Percy Liang is a machine learning and natural language processing researcher at Stanford University. He co-authored the ICML 2017 paper "World of Bits: An Open-Domain Platform for Web-Based Agents" with Andrej Karpathy, Tianlin Shi, Linxi Fan, and Jonathan Hernandez.

# Sanketh Shetty

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher at [[Stanford Vision Lab]] under [[Fei-Fei Li]]

## Biography & Context

Sanketh Shetty is a researcher affiliated with the Stanford Vision Lab, working under [[Fei-Fei Li]]. He is known for co-authoring the paper **Large-Scale Video Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks** (CVPR 2014, Oral) alongside [[Andrej Karpathy]], [[George Toderici]], [[Thomas Leung]], [[Rahul Sukthankar]], and [[Fei-Fei Li]]. This work contributed to the growing body of evidence that CNNs could be effectively applied to video understanding at scale, a topic that was gaining significant traction in the computer vision community during that period.

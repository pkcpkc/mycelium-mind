# Sebastian Thrun

## Affiliations & Roles

- Professor at [[Stanford University]]
- Co-founder at [[Udacity]]
- Research Director at [[Google X]]

## Biography & Context

Sebastian Thrun is a prominent AI researcher, educator, and entrepreneur renowned for his foundational contributions to machine learning, computer vision, and autonomous driving. He served as a professor at [[Stanford University]], where he co-taught pioneering artificial intelligence courses and mentored numerous researchers. His academic development and professional collaborations extended across leading institutions, notably including research rotations and collaborations with [[Andrej Karpathy]], [[Daphne Koller]], [[Andrew Ng]], and [[Vladlen Koltun]]. Thrun's work bridged academic research and industrial application, significantly advancing deep learning, neural networks, and autonomous vehicle technology. His educational initiatives and open research contributions have had a lasting impact on the global AI community.

## Biography & Context

Sebastian Thrun is a German computer scientist and artificial intelligence pioneer known for his groundbreaking work in robotics, autonomous vehicles, and online education. He served as a professor at [[Stanford]], where he advised doctoral students including **Andrej Karpathy** during the first-year rotation program alongside **Fei-Fei Li**, **Daphne Koller**, and **Andrew Ng**.

At Stanford, Thrun directed the Stanford Artificial Intelligence Laboratory and conducted research in machine learning, robotics, and autonomous systems. His academic work laid the foundation for later industry applications of AI.

Thrun co-founded **Udacity**, a massive open online course (MOOC) platform that democratized access to computer science and engineering education. He also initiated the Google Self-Driving Car Project, which became a cornerstone of autonomous vehicle development at [[Google]].

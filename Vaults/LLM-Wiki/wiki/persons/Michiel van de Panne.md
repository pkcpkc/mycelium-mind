# Michiel van de Panne

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher at University of British Columbia

## Biography & Context

Michiel van de Panne is a researcher at the University of British Columbia, known for his work applying machine learning to agile robotics and locomotion control in physically-simulated environments. During his time at UBC, he supervised Andrej Karpathy's MSc research (2009–2011) on learning controllers for physically-simulated figures, an early application of reinforcement learning principles to robotics in simulation.

Van de Panne co-authored several influential papers on locomotion skills for simulated quadrupeds, including "Locomotion Skills for Simulated Quadrupeds" published at SIGGRAPH 2011 alongside Stelian Coros, Andrej Karpathy, Benjamin Jones, and Lionel Reveret. He also co-authored "Curriculum Learning for Motor Skills" published at AI 2012 with Andrej Karpathy, exploring curriculum-based approaches to learning motor control.

His research group at UBC focused on machine learning for agile robotics, learning controllers for physically-simulated figures, and locomotion skill development — work that sits at the intersection of reinforcement learning, simulation, and robotic control.

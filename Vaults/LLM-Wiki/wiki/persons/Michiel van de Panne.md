## Biography & Context

Michiel van de Panne is a computer science academic and researcher recognized for his contributions to robotics simulation. During the master's program at the University of British Columbia, he collaborated closely with Andrej Karpathy, providing research supervision and guidance on robotics simulation projects. His expertise in simulation environments has supported foundational work in interactive character control and robotics, contributing to the broader development of AI and machine learning research pipelines.

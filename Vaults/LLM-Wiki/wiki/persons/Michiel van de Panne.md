# Michiel van de Panne

## Affiliations/Roles

- Professor at [[University of British Columbia]]
- Robotics and computer graphics researcher

## Biography & Context

[[Michiel van de Panne]] was [[Andrej Karpathy]]'s MSc advisor at the [[University of British Columbia]] (2009–2011). Karpathy's research focus under van de Panne was on learning controllers for physically-simulated figures, applying machine learning to agile robotics in physical simulation.

They co-authored several publications including *Curriculum Learning for Motor Skills* (AI 2012) and *Locomotion Skills for Simulated Quadrupeds* (SIGGRAPH 2011).

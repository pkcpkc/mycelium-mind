# Michiel van de Panne

## Affiliations & Roles

- Professor, University of British Columbia
- Researcher in machine learning for robotics and simulation

## Biography & Context

Michiel van de Panne is a professor at the University of British Columbia, where he supervised Andrej Karpathy's MSc research. Karpathy worked with van de Panne on learning controllers for physically-simulated figures, applying machine learning to agile robotics in physical simulations.

# Michiel van de Panne

## Affiliations / Roles

- Professor, University of British Columbia

## Biography & Context

Michiel van de Panne is a computer science professor at the University of British Columbia. He supervised Andrej Karpathy's MSc studies (2009–2011), where Karpathy worked on learning controllers for physically-simulated figures, focusing on machine-learning for agile robotics in simulation.

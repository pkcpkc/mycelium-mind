# Michiel van de Panne

## Affiliations/Roles

- AI researcher (affiliated with University of British Columbia)

## Biography & Context

Michiel van de Panne is an AI researcher who served as [[Andrej Karpathy]]'s MSc advisor at the University of British Columbia (2009-2011). Their work focused on learning controllers for physically-simulated figures, applying machine learning to agile robotics in physical simulation.

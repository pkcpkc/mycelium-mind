# Koray Kavukcuoglu

## Affiliations/Roles

- Director of Research at [[DeepMind]]
- Machine learning researcher

## Biography & Context

[[Koray Kavukcuoglu]] was a collaborator of [[Andrej Karpathy]] during Karpathy's 2015 internship at [[DeepMind]], where they worked on deep reinforcement learning together. Karpathy also collaborated with [[Vlad Mnih]] during this period.

Karpathy's experience at DeepMind in reinforcement learning complemented his earlier work at [[Google Brain]] (2011) on unsupervised learning from videos and his PhD research at [[Stanford University]] on computer vision and natural language processing.

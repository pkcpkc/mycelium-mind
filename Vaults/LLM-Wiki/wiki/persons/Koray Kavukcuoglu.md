## Affiliations & Roles

## Biography & Context

**Koray Kavukcuoglu** is a computer scientist and researcher known for his work in deep learning and reinforcement learning. He served as a research scientist at [[Google Research]] and was part of the [[DeepMind]] team during a period of significant breakthroughs in deep reinforcement learning.

In 2015, [[Andrej Karpathy]] completed an internship at [[DeepMind]], where he worked on deep reinforcement learning alongside Koray Kavukcuoglu and [[Vlad Mnih]]. This period at DeepMind coincided with the lab's foundational work in deep reinforcement learning, which would later produce notable breakthroughs including AlphaGo and AlphaFold.

Kavukcuoglu's research contributions at Google and DeepMind helped advance the state of the art in deep learning applications, particularly in the intersection of neural networks and reinforcement learning strategies.

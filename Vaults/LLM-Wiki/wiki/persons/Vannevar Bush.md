# Vannevar Bush

## Affiliations & Roles

- Proposed Memex system at Independent Research (1945)

## Biography & Context

In 1945, Vannevar Bush proposed the Memex, a conceptual device for information retrieval and associative storage. His visionary framework emphasized a private, curated knowledge store with associative trails, establishing foundational principles for modern personal knowledge management. His work continues to inspire contemporary LLM-driven wiki architectures that prioritize persistent, interlinked knowledge compilation over transient retrieval methods.

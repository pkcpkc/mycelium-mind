# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher, educator, and former founding member of OpenAI. Known for his work in computer vision, deep learning education, and large language models.

## Current Role

Director of AI at OpenAI, leading a team focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation.

## Career Timeline

- **2015:** Founding member at OpenAI
- **2015:** Returned to OpenAI (midtraining/synthetic data team)
- **2017:** Director of AI at Tesla (led computer vision for Autopilot)
- **2011–2015:** PhD at Stanford
- **2009–2011:** MSc at University of British Columbia
- **2005–2009:** BSc at University of Toronto

## Key Positions

- **Director of AI, Tesla (2017–2024):** Led computer vision team for Autopilot and briefly Optimus. Managed in-house data labeling, neural network training, deployment on custom inference chip
- **Founding Member, OpenAI (2015):** Early team member
- **Director/Instructor, CS 231n, Stanford:** Architect and lead instructor of first deep learning course at Stanford

## Research Focus

- Convolutional neural networks
- Recurrent neural networks
- Computer vision
- Natural language processing
- Deep reinforcement learning

## Education

- **PhD:** Stanford Vision Lab (Fei-Fei Li as advisor). Rotations with Daphne Koller, Andrew Ng, Sebastian Thrun, Vladlen Koltun
- **MSc:** University of British Columbia (Michiel van de Panne). Machine learning for physically-simulated figures
- **BSc:** University of Toronto. Double major in CS and physics, minor in math. Attended Geoff Hinton's classes

## Internships

- **Google Brain (2011):** Unsupervised learning from videos
- **Google Research (2013):** Large-scale supervised learning on YouTube videos
- **DeepMind (2015):** Deep reinforcement learning with Koray Kavukcuoglu and Vlad Mnih

## Content Creation

YouTube educational videos in two tracks:
- Technical: Zero to Hero playlist
- General audience: Deep Dive into LLMs, How I Use LLMs, Intro to Large Language Models

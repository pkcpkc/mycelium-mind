# Lex Fridman

## Affiliations & Roles

- Host of the [[Lex Fridman Podcast]], featuring long-form conversations with prominent figures in AI, science, technology, and culture
- Previously affiliated with [[MIT]] as a research scientist in the [[Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory]] (CSAIL)

## Biography & Context

Lex Fridman is a podcaster and AI researcher best known for hosting the Lex Fridman Podcast, one of the most popular technology and science podcasts globally. The podcast features extended, unscripted conversations with leading figures in artificial intelligence, neuroscience, physics, history, and culture.

Fridman previously worked as a research scientist at [[MIT]], where he contributed to research in areas such as autonomous driving, deep learning, and human-robot interaction. His podcast has featured numerous AI researchers and engineers, including [[Andrej Karpathy]], who appeared in 2022 to discuss topics related to AI, deep learning, and his work at [[Tesla]] and [[OpenAI]].

The podcast has featured a wide range of guests including AI researchers, tech CEOs, philosophers, martial artists, and public intellectuals, making it a significant platform for in-depth exploration of ideas across disciplines.

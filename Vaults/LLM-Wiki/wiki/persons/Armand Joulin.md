## Biography & Context

Armand Joulin is a machine learning researcher known for his contributions to natural language processing and computer vision. He appears in the entity list of the Andrej Karpathy bio summary, indicating professional collaboration with Karpathy and the broader Stanford/Google Research research network.

Joulin co-authored the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge paper published in the Journal of Machine Learning Research (JCV) in 2015, which introduced the ILSVRC benchmark that became foundational for deep learning and computer vision research. This work connects him to the Stanford Vision Lab community alongside [[Aditya Khosla]], [[Olga Russakovsky]], [[Jia Deng]], [[Hao Su]], [[Jonathan Krause]], and [[Fei-Fei Li]].

Joulin is also known for creating fastText, a library for efficient text classification and representation learning. He co-authored the influential paper "Bag of Tricks for Efficient Text Classification" with [[Sebastian Schütze]], which demonstrated that simple baseline methods combined with careful engineering could achieve strong performance on text classification tasks.

# Fei-Fei Li

## Affiliations & Roles

- Professor of Computer Science at [[Stanford University]]
- Co-Director at [[Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence]]
- Chief Science Advisor at [[Google Cloud]]

## Biography & Context

Fei-Fei Li is a pioneering computer scientist and artificial intelligence researcher known for her foundational contributions to [[Computer Vision]] and deep learning. She is best recognized for leading the creation of the ImageNet dataset, which served as a catalyst for the modern AI revolution. During her academic career at [[Stanford University]], she established the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) and mentored a generation of AI researchers. Notably, she advised [[Andrej Karpathy]] during his doctoral studies and co-authored influential research with him and other colleagues like [[Justin Johnson]]. Her work spans academic innovation, industry leadership, and the promotion of ethical, human-centered AI development.

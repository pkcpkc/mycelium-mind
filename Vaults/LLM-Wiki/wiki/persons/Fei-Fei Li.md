# Fei-Fei Li

## Affiliations/Roles

- Professor at Stanford University
- Director of Stanford Vision Lab
- Creator of [[ImageNet]]

## Biography & Context

Fei-Fei Li is a prominent computer vision researcher and professor at Stanford University. She directed the Stanford Vision Lab and created the [[ImageNet]] dataset, which became a foundational resource for the computer vision revolution.

She was the PhD adviser of [[Andrej Karpathy]], whose thesis "Connecting Images and Natural Language" (2016) focused on convolutional/recurrent neural networks and their applications in computer vision and natural language processing.

Li co-authored several influential papers with Karpathy including DenseCap (CVPR 2016 Oral), Visualizing and Understanding Recurrent Networks (ICLR 2016 Workshop), Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments for Generating Image Descriptions (CVPR 2015 Oral), and the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge paper (JCV 2015).

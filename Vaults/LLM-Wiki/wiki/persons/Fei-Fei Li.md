## Biography & Context

[[Fei-Fei Li]] is a professor at [[Stanford University]] and a leading researcher in computer vision and AI. She created the ImageNet dataset and led the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC).

She was the PhD advisor of [[Andrej Karpathy]] at [[Stanford University]] (2011–2017), where Karpathy conducted his research in the Stanford Vision Lab. Their collaborative work includes multiple influential papers on image captioning, visual-s semantic alignments, and large-scale video classification.

## Key Collaborations with Karpathy

- **DenseCap: Fully Convolutional Localization Networks for Dense Captioning** (CVPR 2016, Oral) — Co-authored with [[Justin Johnson]] and [[Andrej Karpathy]]
- **Visualizing and Understanding Recurrent Networks** (ICLR 2016 Workshop) — Co-authored with [[Justin Johnson]] and [[Andrej Karpathy]]
- **Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments for Generating Image Descriptions** (CVPR 2015, Oral) — Co-authored with [[Andrej Karpathy]]
- **Large-Scale Video Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks** (CVPR 2014, Oral) — Co-authored with [[Andrej Karpathy]], [[George Toderici]], [[Sanketh Shetty]], [[Thomas Leung]], [[Rahul Sukthankar]]
- **Deep Fragment Embeddings for Bidirectional Image-Sentence Mapping** (NIPS 2014) — Co-authored with [[Andrej Karpathy]] and [[Armand Joulin]]
- **Object Discovery in 3D scenes via Shape Analysis** (ICRA 2013) — Co-authored with [[Andrej Karpathy]] and [[Stephen Miller]]
- **ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge** (JCV 2015) — Co-author on the ImageNet challenge paper

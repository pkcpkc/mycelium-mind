# Fei-Fei Li

## Affiliations & Roles

- PhD Advisor at Stanford (Stanford Vision Lab, circa 2011–2017)

## Biography & Context

Fei-Fei Li is an AI researcher and computer vision pioneer who led the Stanford Vision Lab. She served as the PhD advisor for Andrej Karpathy, who earned his PhD from Stanford with a focus on convolutional and recurrent neural networks applied to computer vision, natural language processing, and their intersection.

Li co-authored several influential papers with Karpathy and collaborators. **DenseCap** (CVPR 2016, Oral) was a fully convolutional localization network for dense captioning, co-authored with Justin Johnson. **Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments for Generating Image Descriptions** (CVPR 2015, Oral) was co-authored with Karpathy. She also co-authored **Visualizing and Understanding Recurrent Networks** (ICLR 2016 Workshop) with Karpathy and Johnson.

Li was a co-author of the **ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge** paper published in the Journal of Machine Learning Research (JCV) in 2015, which introduced the ILSVRC benchmark that became foundational for deep learning and computer vision research. Among the co-authors were Olga Russakovsky, Jia Deng, Hao Su, Jonathan Krause, Aditya Khosla, Michael Bernstein, Alexander C. Berg, and others associated with the Stanford Vision Lab.

She also co-authored **Deep Fragment Embeddings for Bidirectional Image-Sentence Mapping** (NIPS 2014) with Karpathy and Armand Joulin, **Large-Scale Video Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks** (CVPR 2014, Oral) with Karpathy, George Toderici, Sanketh Shetty, Thomas Leung, and Rahul Sukthankar, and **Object Discovery in 3D scenes via Shape Analysis** (ICRA 2013) with Karpathy and Stephen Miller.

Li designed and taught **Stanford CS231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition** before Karpathy became the primary instructor. The course grew from 150 students in 2015 to 750 by 2017, becoming one of Stanford's largest classes.

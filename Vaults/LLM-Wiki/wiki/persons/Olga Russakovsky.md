# Olga Russakovsky

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher at Stanford (Stanford Vision Lab, circa 2015)

## Biography & Context

Olga Russakovsky is a computer vision researcher associated with the Stanford Vision Lab. She was a rotation advisor for Andrej Karpathy during his first year of PhD studies at Stanford, where he worked with Fei-Fei Li, Daphne Koller, Andrew Ng, Sebastian Thrun, and Vladlen Koltun.

Russakovsky was a co-author of the **ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge** paper published in the Journal of Machine Learning Research (JMLR) in 2015, which introduced the ILSVRC benchmark that became foundational for deep learning and computer vision research. The paper was co-authored with Jia Deng, Hao Su, Jonathan Krause, Sanjeev Satheesh, Sean Ma, Zhiheng Huang, Andrej Karpathy, Aditya Khosla, Michael Bernstein, Alexander C. Berg, and Li Fei-Fei.

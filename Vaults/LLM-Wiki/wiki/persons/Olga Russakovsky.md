# Olga Russakovsky

## Affiliations/Roles

- Computer vision researcher
- Co-author on ImageNet LSVRC paper

## Biography & Context

[[Olga Russakovsky]] was a co-author on the *ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge* paper published in JCV 2015, which documented the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC). Other co-authors included [[Fei-Fei Li]], [[Jia Deng]], [[Hao Su]], [[Jonathan Krause]], [[Andrej Karpathy]], and others.

[[Fei-Fei Li]] created the ImageNet dataset and led the ImageNet LSVRC challenge.

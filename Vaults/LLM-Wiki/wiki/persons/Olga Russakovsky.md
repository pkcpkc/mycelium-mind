# Olga Russakovsky

## Affiliations/Roles

- AI researcher

## Biography & Context

Olga Russakovsky is an AI researcher who co-authored the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge paper (JCV 2015) alongside [[Jia Deng]], [[Hao Su]], [[Jonathan Krause]], [[Sanjeev Satheesh]], [[Sean Ma]], [[Zhiheng Huang]], [[Andrej Karpathy]], [[Aditya Khosla]], [[Michael Bernstein]], [[Alexander C. Berg]], and [[Fei-Fei Li]].

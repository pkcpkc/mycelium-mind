# Hao Su

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher in computer vision

## Biography & Context

Hao Su is a co-author of the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge paper published in JCV 2015, which was a landmark publication in the field of computer vision. The paper also included contributions from Olga Russakovsky, Jia Deng, and Andrej Karpathy among others. ([Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

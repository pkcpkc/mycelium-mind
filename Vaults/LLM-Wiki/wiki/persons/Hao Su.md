## Affiliations/Roles

## Biography & Context

Hao Su is an AI researcher who co-authored the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge paper (JCV 2015) alongside [[Olga Russakovsky]], [[Jia Deng]], [[Jonathan Krause]], [[Sanjeev Satheesh]], [[Sean Ma]], [[Zhiheng Huang]], [[Andrej Karpathy]], [[Aditya Khosla]], [[Michael Bernstein]], [[Alexander C. Berg]], and [[Fei-Fei Li]].

# Geoff Hinton

## Affiliations & Roles

- Professor at [[University of Toronto]]

## Biography & Context

Geoff Hinton is a prominent researcher and educator whose academic work and teaching at the University of Toronto have significantly influenced the modern AI landscape. He is particularly noted for his deep learning courses, which sparked the early career interests of several leading figures in the field, including Andrej Karpathy. Through his mentorship and research, Hinton has helped establish the foundational principles of neural networks that continue to drive advancements in computer vision and large language models.

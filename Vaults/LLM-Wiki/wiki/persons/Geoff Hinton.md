# Geoff Hinton

## Affiliations/Roles

- Pioneering researcher in deep learning and artificial intelligence
- Professor at [[University of Toronto]]

## Biography & Context

[[Geoff Hinton]] is a pioneering researcher in deep learning and artificial intelligence. He introduced [[Andrej Karpathy]] to deep learning during Karpathy's undergraduate studies at the [[University of Toronto]].

From 2005 to 2009, Karpathy attended Hinton's classes and reading groups at the [[University of Toronto]] during his BSc in computer science and physics. This exposure sparked Karpathy's interest in deep learning, leading to his career in the field.

Hinton's contributions to deep learning include pioneering work on backpropagation, deep belief networks, dropout regularization, and word embeddings (Word2Vec co-author). His work had a lasting impact on Karpathy's career trajectory, which later led to his PhD at [[Stanford University]], founding membership at [[OpenAI]], and his role as Director of AI at [[Tesla]].

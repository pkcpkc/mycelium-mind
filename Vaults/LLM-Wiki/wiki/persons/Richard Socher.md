# Richard Socher

## Affiliations / Roles

- Co-Founder, Brain Corporation
- Chief Scientist, Salesforce Research (former)

## Biography & Context

Richard Socher is a natural language processing and deep learning researcher. He collaborated with Andrej Karpathy on "Grounded Compositional Semantics for Finding and Describing Images with Sentences" (TACL 2013), working alongside Quoc V. Le, Christopher D. Manning, and Andrew Ng.

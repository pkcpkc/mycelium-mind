# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator. Founding member of OpenAI and former Director of AI at Tesla, where he led the computer vision team for Autopilot.

## Biography

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator. He was a founding member of OpenAI and later the Director of AI at Tesla, where he led the computer vision team of the Autopilot. During his PhD at Stanford he was the architect and lead instructor of the first deep learning course at Stanford (CS231n), which has become one of its most popular classes. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## Career Timeline

- **2025**: Returned to OpenAI, built a new team working on midtraining and synthetic data generation. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **2017**: Director of AI at Tesla, led computer vision team for Tesla Autopilot and Tesla Optimus. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **2015**: Research scientist and founding member at OpenAI. PhD from Stanford. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **2015**: Internship at DeepMind working on deep reinforcement learning. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **2013**: Internship at Google Research on large-scale supervised learning. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **2011**: Internship at Google Brain on unsupervised learning from videos. PhD from Stanford advised by Fei-Fei Li. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **2009-2011**: MSc at University of British Columbia, worked on learning controllers for physically-simulated figures. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **2005-2009**: BSc at University of Toronto, double major in computer science and physics, minor in math. Attended Geoff Hinton's class. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## Education

- **PhD**: Stanford University, Stanford Vision Lab. Advisor: Fei-Fei Li. Worked with Daphne Koller, Andrew Ng, Sebastian Thrun, and Vladlen Koltun. PhD focused on convolutional/recurrent neural networks and their applications in computer vision, NLP, and their intersection. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **MSc**: University of British Columbia. Worked with Michiel van de Panne. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **BSc**: University of Toronto. Double major in computer science and physics, minor in math. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## Teaching

Created educational videos on AI on his YouTube channel with two parallel tracks: technical track (Zero to Hero playlist) and general audience track (Deep Dive into LLMs, How I Use LLMs, Intro to Large Language Models). Designed and was primary instructor for Stanford CS 231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition, which grew from 150 students in 2015 to 750 students in 2017. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## Notable Projects

- **micrograd**: Tiny scalar-valued autograd engine implementing backpropagation over a dynamically built DAG. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **char-rnn**: Torch character-level language model built with LSTMs/GRUs/RNNs. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **arxiv-sanity**: Tool to discover relevant papers on Arxiv, search/sort by similarity, see recent/popular papers. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **neuraltalk2**: Early image captioning project in (lua)Torch. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **ConvNetJS**: Deep learning library written from scratch in Javascript with web-based demos. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- **ulogme**: Privacy-first productivity tracker. ([source](assets/2026-06-09/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

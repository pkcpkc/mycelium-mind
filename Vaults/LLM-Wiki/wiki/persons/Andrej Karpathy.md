## Biography & Context

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator known for his contributions to computer vision, deep learning, and AI education. He was a founding member of OpenAI in 2015 and later joined Tesla as Director of AI, where he led the computer vision team for Tesla Autopilot and briefly for Tesla Optimus.

During his PhD at Stanford University, Karpathy was the architect and lead instructor of CS231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition — the first deep learning course at Stanford. The course grew from 150 enrolled students in 2015 to 750 students in 2017, becoming one of the largest at Stanford.

He returned to OpenAI where he built a new team working on midtraining and synthetic data generation.

Karpathy is also known for his educational YouTube channel, with popular lectures including "Let's build GPT: from scratch, in code, spelled out" (3.5M views), "Intro to Large Language Models" (1M views), and "The spelled-out intro to neural networks and backpropagation" (1.2M views).

He has created several notable projects including [[micrograd]], [[char-rnn]], [[arxiv-sanity]], [[NeuralTalk2]], [[ConvNetJS]], and [[ulogme]].

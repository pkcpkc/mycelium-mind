## Biography & Context

Adam Coates is a researcher known for his work in unsupervised feature learning and computer vision. He co-authored the paper "Emergence of Object-Selective Features in Unsupervised Feature Learning" published at NIPS 2012 alongside Andrej Karpathy and Andrew Ng. The paper investigated how object-selective features emerge in unsupervised learning models, contributing to the understanding of feature representation in deep learning systems.

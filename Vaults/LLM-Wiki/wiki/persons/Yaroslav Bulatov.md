# Yaroslav Bulatov

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher (co-author of PixelCNN++)

## Biography & Context

Yaroslav Bulatov is an AI researcher known for contributions to deep learning and generative modeling. He is best known as a co-author of "PixelCNN++: A PixelCNN Implementation with Discretized Logistic Mixture Likelihood and Other Modifications" (ICLR 2017), which improved upon the PixelCNN autoregressive model for image generation by introducing a discretized logistic mixture likelihood and other modifications. The paper was co-authored with Andrej Karpathy, Tim Salimans, Diederik P. Kingma, and Xi Chen.

# Jia Deng

## Affiliations & Roles

- Professor of Computer Science at [[Stanford University]]
- Creator and Principal Investigator of the [[ImageNet]] Project

## Biography & Context

Jia Deng is a leading computer scientist and researcher whose work has fundamentally shaped modern [[Deep Learning]] and [[Computer Vision]]. He is best known for creating the [[ImageNet]] dataset, a massive visual database that powered the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC). This initiative played a crucial role in catalyzing the deep learning revolution by providing standardized benchmarks for training and evaluating [[Neural Networks]] and [[Convolutional Neural Networks]].

During his academic career at [[Stanford University]], Deng collaborated closely with fellow faculty and researchers, including [[Fei-Fei Li]], [[Oliver Russakovsky]], and [[Hao Su]]. His research on large-scale visual understanding and machine learning has influenced generations of AI practitioners. He has also contributed to the broader AI ecosystem through collaborations with researchers exploring [[Computer Vision]] and [[Large Language Models]], including [[Andrej Karpathy]], whose work on neural network training, autonomous systems, and educational AI tools intersects significantly with Deng's foundational datasets and vision research.

# Alexander C. Berg

## Affiliations/Roles

- Computer vision researcher
- Co-author on ImageNet LSVRC paper

## Biography & Context

[[Alexander C. Berg]] was a co-author on the *ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge* paper published in JCV 2015. The paper documented the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC), created by [[Fei-Fei Li]]. Other co-authors included [[Olga Russakovsky]], [[Jia Deng]], [[Hao Su]], [[Jonathan Krause]], [[Sanjeev Satheesh]], [[Sean Ma]], [[Zhiheng Huang]], [[Aditya Khosla]], [[Michael Bernstein]], [[Andrej Karpathy]], and others.

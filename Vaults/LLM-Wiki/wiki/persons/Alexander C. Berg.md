# Alexander C. Berg

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher at [[Stanford University]] (Stanford Vision Lab)

## Biography & Context

Alexander C. Berg is a computer vision researcher known for his contributions to large-scale visual recognition benchmarks. He was part of [[Li Fei-Fei]]'s research group at the Stanford Vision Lab, where he co-authored the **ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge** paper published in the Journal of Computer Vision (JCV) in 2015 alongside Olga Russakovsky, Jia Deng, Hao Su, Jonathan Krause, Sanjeev Satheesh, Sean Ma, Zhiheng Huang, Andrej Karpathy, Aditya Khosla, Michael Bernstein, and Li Fei-Fei. This paper introduced the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC), which became one of the most influential benchmarks in deep learning and computer vision.

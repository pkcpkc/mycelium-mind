# Andrej Karpathy

**Andrej Karpathy** is an AI researcher and educator specializing in deep learning, computer vision, and large language models.

## Biography

Karpathy was a founding member of [[OpenAI]] in 2015 and returned later to lead a team focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation. He served as Director of AI at [[Tesla Autopilot]], leading the computer vision team for Tesla's autonomous driving systems and briefly working on [[Tesla Optimus]].

He designed and taught Stanford's CS231n deep learning course, which grew from 150 to 750 students. He runs a popular YouTube channel with educational AI videos including the Zero to Hero series.

## Education

- PhD, Stanford University (advisor: [[Fei-Fei Li]]) — research on convolutional/recurrent neural networks
- MSc, University of British Columbia (advisor: [[Michiel van de Panne]])
- BSc, University of Toronto — computer science and physics, minor in math

## Work History

- **OpenAI**: Founding member (2015), returned to lead midtraining/synthetic data team
- **Tesla**: Director of AI, led computer vision for Autopilot
- **Stanford**: Instructor for CS231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition
- **Google Brain**: Intern (2011) — unsupervised learning from videos
- **Google Research**: Intern (2013) — large-scale supervised learning on YouTube videos
- **DeepMind**: Intern (2015) — deep reinforcement learning

## Key Contributions

- [[Software 2.0]]: Framework for building AI systems using data and learning algorithms
- [[Neural Talk 2]]: Early image captioning system
- [[ConvNetJS]]: Deep learning library in JavaScript
- Co-authored research on [[ImageNet]], [[DenseCap]], and other computer vision milestones

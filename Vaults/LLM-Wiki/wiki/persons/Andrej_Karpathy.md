# Andrej Karpathy

- AI educator via YouTube channel

## Biography & Context

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator known for creating accessible educational content on deep learning and large language models.

At [[Tesla]], Karpathy served as Director of AI where he led the computer vision team behind [[Tesla Autopilot]] and briefly [[Tesla Optimus]]. His responsibilities encompassed the full ML pipeline including in-house data labeling at scale, neural network training and deployment, and optimization for Tesla's custom inference hardware. The Autopilot work focuses on improving driving safety and convenience with the long-term goal of achieving Full Self-Driving at scale, showcased in the August 2021 Tesla AI Day.

Karpathy later returned to [[OpenAI]], where he built a new team focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation — critical stages in the model development pipeline between pretraining and instruction tuning.

He is also well known for his YouTube channel producing educational AI videos organized into parallel tracks: Zero to Hero (a technical track building language models from scratch), Deep Dive into LLMs, How I Use LLMs, and an introductory video on large language models.

Karpathy is most active on X/Twitter and GitHub, where he shares his latest work and insights.

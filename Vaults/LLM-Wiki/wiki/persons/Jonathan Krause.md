# Andrej Karpathy

## Affiliations & Roles

- Director of AI at [[Tesla]]
- Founding Member at [[OpenAI]]
- Researcher & PhD Candidate at [[Stanford University]]
- Graduate Student at [[University of British Columbia]]

## Biography & Context

Andrej Karpathy is a prominent AI researcher and educator recognized for his foundational work in [[Deep Learning]], [[Computer Vision]], and [[Large Language Models]]. He currently works at [[OpenAI]], focusing on midtraining techniques and synthetic data generation. Previously, Karpathy served as the Director of AI at [[Tesla]], where he led the computer vision team for [[Tesla Autopilot]] and Optimus, overseeing data labeling pipelines, neural network training, and deployment on custom inference hardware. He was also a founding member of [[OpenAI]] in 2015.

Karpathy earned his PhD from [[Stanford University]], studying under the supervision of [[Fei-Fei Li]]. His academic development included research rotations and collaborations with prominent figures such as [[Daphne Koller]], [[Andrew Ng]], [[Sebastian Thrun]], and [[Vladlen Koltun]]. Earlier, during his master's studies at the [[University of British Columbia]], he worked with [[Michiel van de Panne]] on robotics simulation. His early exposure to deep learning was significantly influenced by attending lectures from [[Geoff Hinton]] at the University of Toronto.

Beyond research, Karpathy is a dedicated educator who designed and taught [[CS 231n]], one of Stanford's largest and most influential courses. He has expanded his teaching reach through a popular [[YouTube]] channel featuring series like "Zero to Hero" and detailed tutorials on building LLMs from scratch. He is also known for creating widely-used open-source educational tools such as [[micrograd]], [[char-rnn]], [[arxiv-sanity]], [[neuraltalk2]], and [[ConvNetJS]].

His research portfolio spans image captioning, recurrent neural networks, and visual-semantic alignment. He has co-authored key papers with [[Justin Johnson]], [[Diederik P. Kingma]], and [[Tim Salimans]]. Karpathy has been recognized as a keynote speaker at major industry events, including [[NVIDIA GTC]] alongside [[Jensen Huang]], and has shared his insights on podcasts like [[Lex Fridman]] and [[Dwarlesh]].

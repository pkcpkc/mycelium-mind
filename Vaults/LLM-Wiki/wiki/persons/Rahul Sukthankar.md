# Rahul Sukthankar

## Affiliations & Roles

- Researcher at Stanford Vision Lab

## Biography & Context

Rahul Sukthankar is a researcher affiliated with the Stanford Vision Lab, working under [[Fei-Fei Li]]. His primary research focus is on computer vision and deep learning, particularly large-scale video classification using convolutional neural networks. He is best known for co-authoring the paper **Large-Scale Video Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks** (CVPR 2014, Oral) alongside [[Andrej Karpathy]], [[George Toderici]], [[Sanketh Shetty]], [[Thomas Leung]], and [[Fei-Fei Li]]. This work contributed to the growing body of evidence that CNNs could be effectively applied to video understanding at scale, a topic that was gaining significant traction in the computer vision community during that period.

# Andrej Karpathy

Summary of Andrej Karpathy's biography, career, teaching, projects, and publications based on his personal website content.

## Overview

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator known for his work in deep learning, computer vision, and large language models. He was a founding member of OpenAI, Director of AI at Tesla, and created one of the most popular deep learning courses at Stanford (CS 231n).

## Key Highlights

- **OpenAI**: Founding member (2015), returned in 2025 to lead midtraining and synthetic data team.
- **Tesla**: Director of AI, led Autopilot computer vision team.
- **Stanford**: Created and taught CS 231n, one of Stanford's largest classes.
- **YouTube**: Created extensive educational content on neural networks and LLMs with millions of views.
- **Research**: Pioneering work in image captioning, visual recognition, and language modeling.

## Overview

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher, educator, and former founding member of OpenAI. He currently works at OpenAI building a team focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation.

## Career Highlights

- **OpenAI (2015):** Founding member, returned later to lead midtraining/synthetic data team
- **Tesla:** Director of AI, led computer vision team for Autopilot and briefly Optimus. Managed in-house data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on custom inference chip
- **Stanford:** Designed and taught CS 231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition — one of Stanford's largest classes (grew from 150 in 2015 to 750 in 2017)
- **PhD:** Stanford Vision Lab under Fei-Fei Li, with work alongside Daphne Koller, Andrew Ng, Sebastian Thrun, and Vladlen Koltun. Research focused on convolutional/recurrent neural networks and their applications in computer vision, NLP, and their intersection
- **Internships:** Google Brain (2011), Google Research (2013), DeepMind (2015)

## Education

- **MSc:** University of British Columbia (2009–2011) — machine learning for robotics in physical simulation with Michiel van de Panne
- **BSc:** University of Toronto (2005–2009) — double major in CS and physics, minor in math. Introduced to deep learning via Geoff Hinton's classes

## Content Creation

Produces educational AI videos on YouTube in two tracks:
1. Technical track (Zero to Hero playlist)
2. General audience track (Deep Dive into LLMs, How I Use LLMs, Intro to Large Language Models)

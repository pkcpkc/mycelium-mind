# Andrej Karpathy — Biography and Background

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator known for creating accessible educational content on deep learning and large language models.

## YouTube Channel

Karpathy runs a YouTube channel producing educational AI videos organized into parallel tracks for technical and general audiences:

- **Zero to Hero** — A technical track that walks through building language models from scratch.
- **Deep Dive into LLMs** — An exploration of the under-the-hood fundamentals of models like ChatGPT.
- **How I Use LLMs** — A practical guide offering real-world examples from Karpathy's own life.
- **Intro to Large Language Models** — An earlier, longer-form introductory video on the topic.

## OpenAI

Karpathy returned to OpenAI, where he built a new team focused on **midtraining** and **synthetic data generation** — critical stages in the model development pipeline between pretraining and instruction tuning.

## Tesla

Karpathy served as **Director of AI at Tesla**, where he led the computer vision team behind **Tesla Autopilot** and briefly **Tesla Optimus**. His responsibilities encompassed the full ML pipeline:

- In-house data labeling at scale
- Neural network training and deployment
- Optimization for Tesla's custom inference hardware

The Autopilot system improves driving safety and convenience, with the long-term goal of achieving **Full Self-Driving** at scale. The work was showcased in the August 2021 Tesla AI Day.

## Presence

Karpathy is most active on **X/Twitter** and **GitHub**, where he shares his latest work and insights.

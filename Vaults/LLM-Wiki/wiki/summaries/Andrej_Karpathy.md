# Andrej Karpathy

## Overview

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator. He was a founding member of OpenAI and later the Director of AI at Tesla, where he led the computer vision team for Autopilot and (briefly) Tesla Optimus. During his PhD at Stanford he was the architect and lead instructor of CS 231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition, which became one of Stanford's largest classes (growing from 150 students in 2015 to 750 in 2017).

He currently works at OpenAI, where he built a team focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation.

## Biography

- **Education:**
  - PhD in Computer Science, Stanford University (2011) — advisor: [[Fei-Fei Li]]. Research focused on convolutional/recurrent neural networks and their applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and their intersection. Also worked with [[Daphne Koller]], [[Andrew Ng]], [[Sebastian Thrun]], and [[Vladlen Koltun]] during the first-year rotation program.
  - MSc, University of British Columbia (2009-2011) — worked with [[Michiel van de Panne]] on learning controllers for physically-simulated figures (machine-learning for agile robotics in simulation).
  - BSc, University of Toronto (2005-2009) — double major in computer science and physics, minor in math. First exposure to deep learning through [[Geoff Hinton]]'s classes and reading groups.

- **Career:**
  - 2023 — Returned to [[OpenAI]], built a new team working on midtraining and synthetic data generation.
  - Director of AI at [[Tesla]] — led the computer vision team for Tesla Autopilot and Tesla Optimus. Handled all in-house data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on Tesla's custom inference chip.
  - 2015 — Research scientist and founding member at [[OpenAI]].
  - 2015 — Internship at [[DeepMind]] working on deep reinforcement learning with [[Koray Kavukcuoglu]] and [[Vlad Mnih]].
  - 2013 — Internship at [[Google Research]] working on large-scale supervised learning on YouTube videos.
  - 2011 — Internship at (baby) [[Google Brain]] working on unsupervised learning from videos.
  - Stanford University — Designed and taught CS 231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition.

## Key Projects & Contributions

- **[[OpenAI]]** — Founding member (2015).
- **Tesla Autopilot** — Led computer vision team, neural network training, and deployment.
- **CS 231n** — Stanford's first deep learning course, one of its most popular classes.
- **micrograd** — Tiny scalar-valued autograd engine with backpropagation (reverse-mode autodiff) over a dynamically built DAG and a small neural networks library with a PyTorch-like API.
- **char-rnn** — Torch character-level language model built from LSTMs/GRUs/RNNs.
- **arxiv-sanity** — Platform for discovering and sorting research papers from Arxiv.
- **neuraltalk2** — Early image captioning project in (lua)Torch, later extended with [[Justin Johnson]] to dense captioning.
- **ConvNetJS** — Deep learning library written entirely in JavaScript, enabling browser-based neural network demos.
- **ulogme** — Privacy-first productivity tracker.

## Educational Content

Karpathy produces educational videos on AI via his YouTube channel across multiple tracks:

1. **Technical track** — Zero to Hero series covering LLM internals from scratch (e.g., "Let's build GPT", "The spelled-out intro to neural networks and backpropagation").
2. **General audience track** — Practical guides on using LLMs in everyday life.
3. **Intro to Large Language Models** — Parallel introductory video.

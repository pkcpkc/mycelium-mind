## About

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator. He was a founding member of OpenAI and later the Director of AI at Tesla, where he led the computer vision team for [[Tesla Autopilot]] and [[Tesla Optimus]]. During his PhD at Stanford he was the architect and lead instructor of the first deep learning course at Stanford (CS231n), which has become one of its most popular classes. He returned to OpenAI where he built a new team working on midtraining and synthetic data generation.

## Key Facts

- Founding member at OpenAI (2015)
- Director of AI at Tesla (led computer vision team for Autopilot)
- PhD at Stanford under [[Fei-Fei Li]], focused on convolutional/recurrent neural networks and their applications in computer vision, natural language processing
- Advisor: [[Fei-Fei Li]], with work under [[Daphne Koller]], [[Andrew Ng]], [[Sebastian Thrun]], and [[Vladlen Koltun]]
- Internships at Google Brain (2011), Google Research (2013), and DeepMind (2015)
- MSc at University of British Columbia under [[Michiel van de Panne]]
- BSc at University of Toronto (double major in computer science and physics, minor in math)
- Attended [[Geoff Hinton]]'s class and reading groups at University of Toronto

# DeepMind

[[DeepMind]] is a British artificial intelligence company acquired by [[Google]] in 2014. [[Andrej Karpathy]] completed an internship at DeepMind in 2015, working on deep reinforcement learning.

## Karpathy's Work at DeepMind

- **2015** — Internship at [[DeepMind]]
- **Research focus:** Deep reinforcement learning
- **Collaborators:** [[Vlad Mnih]] and [[Koray Kavukcuoglu]]

## Context

Karpathy's experience at DeepMind in reinforcement learning complemented his earlier work at [[Google Brain]] (2011) on unsupervised learning from videos and his PhD research at [[Stanford University]] on computer vision and natural language processing.

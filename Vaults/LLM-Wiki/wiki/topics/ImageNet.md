# ImageNet

[[ImageNet]] is a large-scale visual recognition challenge and dataset used to benchmark computer vision models. [[Andrej Karpathy]] gained notoriety for competing against an early ConvNet on the ImageNet image categorization task, becoming jokingly referred to as the "reference human" for ImageNet.

## Karpathy's ImageNet Work

- Competed against an early ConvNet on categorizing images into 1,000 classes
- Required custom tooling and extensive learning about dog breeds
- The event was covered in his blog post *What I Learned from Competing Against a ConvNet on ImageNet* (2014) and in a [[Wired]] article

## ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge

Karpathy was a co-author on the ImageNet LSVRC paper:

- **ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge** (JCV 2015)
  - Authors: [[Olga Russakovsky]], [[Jia Deng]], [[Hao Su]], [[Jonathan Krause]], [[Sanjeev Satheesh]], [[Sean Ma]], [[Zhiheng Huang]], [[Andrej Karpathy]], [[Aditya Khosla]], [[Michael Bernstein]], [[Alexander C. Berg]], [[Fei-Fei Li]]

## Related Projects

- **[[ConvNetJS]]** — Browser-based CNN demos that could run on ImageNet-like tasks
- **t-SNE visualization of CNN codes** — Visualizing learned features from ImageNet

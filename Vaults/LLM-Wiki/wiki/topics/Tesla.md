# Tesla

[[Tesla]] is an electric vehicle and clean energy company. [[Andrej Karpathy]] served as Director of AI at Tesla from 2016 to 2022, where he led the computer vision team for the Autopilot program and briefly for Tesla Optimus. His team handled all in-house data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on Tesla's custom inference chip.

## Key Projects

- **Tesla Autopilot** — Autopilot system that increased driving safety and convenience; the team's goal was to make Full Self-Driving a reality at scale
- **Tesla Optimus** — Humanoid robot project, briefly worked on with Karpathy
- **Custom inference chip** — Tesla's proprietary AI inference hardware

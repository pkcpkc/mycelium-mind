# Convolutional Neural Networks

[[Convolutional Neural Networks]] (CNNs) are a class of deep neural networks primarily used for image recognition and processing. [[Andrej Karpathy]] has been a leading educator and researcher in CNNs, from his PhD work on visual recognition to his popular CS231n course.

## Key Contributions

### Education

- **[[CS231n]]** — Designed and taught the first deep learning class at [[Stanford University]], focusing on CNNs for visual recognition. The course grew from 150 students (2015) to 750 students (2017).

### Research

- **Large-Scale Video Classification** (CVPR 2014, Oral) — Early application of CNNs to video
- **ImageNet** — Competed against early ConvNet on image classification
- **DenseCap** (CVPR 2016, Oral) — Fully convolutional localization networks
- **Deep Fragment Embeddings** (NIPS 2014) — Image-sentence mapping with CNNs

## Tools and Libraries

- **[[ConvNetJS]]** — Deep learning library written entirely in JavaScript, enabling browser-based CNN training and demos
- **[[Micrograd]]** — Tiny autograd engine that supports building CNN components from scratch

## Related Concepts

- **[[Deep Learning]]** — CNNs are a cornerstone of deep learning
- **[[Computer Vision]]** — CNNs are the dominant architecture for computer vision tasks
- **[[Backpropagation]]** — The training algorithm used to optimize CNNs

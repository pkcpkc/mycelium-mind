# Andrew Ng

[[Andrew Ng]] is a prominent AI researcher, educator, and entrepreneur. He served as one of [[Andrej Karpathy]]'s mentors during his PhD at [[Stanford University]] and has been a collaborator throughout Karpathy's career.

## Relationship with Karpathy

- **2011–2017** — Worked with Karpathy during his first-year rotation program at [[Stanford University]]
- Collaborated on various research and educational initiatives

## Notable Collaborations

- **2017 "Heroes of Deep Learning"** — Joint appearance with [[Andrej Karpathy]]
- Both contributed to the development of deep learning education and research at Stanford

## Key Contributions to AI Education

- Co-founded [[Coursera]], bringing university-level courses online
- Created the popular Machine Learning course on Coursera
- Founded [[DeepLearning.AI]]
- Co-founded [[Coursera]] with [[Daphne Koller]]

## Other Affiliations

- Co-founder and former CCO of [[Baidu]]
- Founder of [[DeepLearning.AI]]
- Adjunct professor at [[Stanford University]]

# Computer Vision

[[Computer Vision]] is a field of artificial intelligence that enables computers to derive meaningful information from digital images, videos, and other visual inputs. [[Andrej Karpathy]] has made significant contributions to computer vision research, particularly in image captioning and large-scale visual recognition.

## Key Research Areas

### Image Captioning

- **[[NeuralTalk2]]** — Early image captioning project in [[Torch]], generating natural language descriptions from images
- **DenseCap** (CVPR 2016, Oral) — Fully convolutional localization networks for dense captioning, co-authored with [[Justin Johnson]] and [[Fei-Fei Li]]
- **Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments** (CVPR 2015, Oral) — Generating image descriptions with co-author [[Fei-Fei Li]]
- **Deep Fragment Embeddings** (NIPS 2014) — Bidirectional image-sentence mapping

### Large-Scale Visual Recognition

- **ImageNet** — Karpathy competed against an early ConvNet on categorizing 1,000 image classes, becoming the "reference human" for ImageNet
- **Large-Scale Video Classification** (CVPR 2014, Oral) — One of Karpathy's earliest oral presentations at CVPR

## Projects

- **[[ConvNetJS]]** — Deep learning library written entirely in JavaScript with browser-based CNN demos
- **[[arxiv-sanity]]** — Tool for discovering and sorting research papers, including visual similarity search
- **t-SNE visualization of CNN codes** — Visualizing learned features from ImageNet

# University of British Columbia

The [[University of British Columbia]] (UBC) is a public research university in Vancouver, Canada. [[Andrej Karpathy]] completed his Master's studies here from 2009 to 2011.

## Karpathy's Studies

- **MSc** (2009–2011)
- **Advisor:** [[Michiel van de Panne]]
- **Research focus:** Learning controllers for physically-simulated figures (machine learning for agile robotics in physical simulation)

## Research Topics

- Learning controllers for simulated robotic figures
- Machine learning for agile robotics in simulation
- Curriculum learning for motor skills
- Locomotion skills for simulated quadrupeds

## Publications from UBC Period

- **Curriculum Learning for Motor Skills** (AI 2012) — Co-authored with [[Michiel van de Panne]]
- **Locomotion Skills for Simulated Quadrupeds** (SIGGRAPH 2011) — Co-authored with [[Stelian Coros]], [[Benjamin Jones]], [[Lionel Reveret]], [[Michiel van de Panne]]

## Transition to PhD

After completing his MSc at UBC, Karpathy moved to [[Stanford University]] for his PhD, where he shifted from robotics simulation to computer vision and natural language processing.

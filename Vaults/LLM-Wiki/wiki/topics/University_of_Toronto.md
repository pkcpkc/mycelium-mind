# University of Toronto

The [[University of Toronto]] is a public research university in Toronto, Canada. [[Andrej Karpathy]] completed his undergraduate studies here from 2005 to 2009.

## Karpathy's Studies

- **BSc** (2005–2009)
- **Double major:** Computer science and physics
- **Minor:** Mathematics
- **Key experience:** Attended [[Geoff Hinton]]'s classes and reading groups, which sparked his interest in deep learning

## Impact

Karpathy's time at the University of Toronto was foundational to his career. Exposure to [[Geoff Hinton]]'s work on deep learning during his undergraduate studies led him to pursue graduate research in the field, eventually leading to his MSc at the [[University of British Columbia]] and PhD at [[Stanford University]].

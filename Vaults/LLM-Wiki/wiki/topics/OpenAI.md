# OpenAI

[[OpenAI]] is an artificial intelligence research organization. [[Andrej Karpathy]] was a founding member and research scientist at OpenAI in 2015. He returned to OpenAI in 2024, where he built a new team focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation.

## Key People

- **[[Andrej Karpathy]]** — Founding member (2015), returned in 2024 to lead midtraining and synthetic data team
- **[[Diederik P. Kingma]]** — Co-author on PixelCNN++ paper
- **[[Xi Chen]]** — Co-author on PixelCNN++ paper
- **[[Yaroslav Bulatov]]** — Co-author on PixelCNN++ paper

## Notable Publications

- *The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks* (2015) — Andrej Karpathy
- *World of Bits: An Open-Domain Platform for Web-Based Agents* (ICML 2017) — Tianlin Shi, Andrej Karpathy, et al.
- *PixelCNN++: A PixelCNN Implementation with Discretized Logistic Mixture Likelihood and Other Modifications* (ICLR 2017) — Tim Salimans, Andrej Karpathy, et al.

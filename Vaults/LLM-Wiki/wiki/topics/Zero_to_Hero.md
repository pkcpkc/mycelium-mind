# Zero to Hero

The [[Zero to Hero]] lecture series by [[Andrej Karpathy]] is a comprehensive educational resource on neural networks and large language models, teaching these topics from scratch. It consists of two parallel tracks: a technical track and a general audience track.

## Technical Track

- **Intro to Large Language Models** — 1M views, parallel introductory video
- **Deep Dive into LLMs** — Under-the-hood fundamentals of LLMs like ChatGPT
- **Building GPT from scratch** — 3.5M views, coded step-by-step implementation
- **Building makemore series:**
  - Part 1: Introduction to generative models
  - Part 2: MLP (210K views)
  - Part 3: Activations & Gradients, BatchNorm (184K views)
  - Part 4: Becoming a Backprop Ninja (127K views)
  - Part 5: Building a WaveNet (126K views)
- **The spelled-out intro to neural networks and backpropagation** — 1.2M views

## General Audience Track

- **How I Use LLMs** — Practical guide with examples from Karpathy's own life

## Key Concepts Covered

- Backpropagation (spelled out in detail)
- Batch normalization
- Transformer architecture
- Tokenization
- GPT architecture and training
- Autoregressive language modeling

## Related Projects

- **[[Micrograd]]** — Tiny autograd engine used in the lectures
- **[[makemore]]** — Series of generative model projects taught alongside the lectures

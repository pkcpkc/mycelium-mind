# Geoff Hinton

[[Geoff Hinton]] is a pioneering researcher in deep learning and artificial intelligence. He introduced [[Andrej Karpathy]] to deep learning during Karpathy's undergraduate studies at the [[University of Toronto]].

## Relationship with Karpathy

- **2005–2009** — Karpathy attended [[Geoff Hinton]]'s classes and reading groups at the [[University of Toronto]] during his BSc in computer science and physics
- This exposure sparked Karpathy's interest in deep learning, leading to his career in the field

## Hinton's Contributions to Deep Learning

- Pioneering work on backpropagation
- Deep belief networks
- Dropout regularization
- Word embeddings (Word2Vec co-author)
- Neuromusic and other AI research

## Impact on Karpathy

Karpathy's attendance at Hinton's classes and reading groups was the catalyst for his deep learning career, which later led to his PhD at [[Stanford University]], founding membership at [[OpenAI]], and his role as Director of AI at [[Tesla]].

# Large Language Models

[[Large Language Models]] (LLMs) are deep learning models trained on massive text datasets that can generate human-like text. [[Andrej Karpathy]] has become one of the most prominent educators on LLMs through his YouTube lectures and his role at [[OpenAI]].

## Karpathy's LLM Work

### At OpenAI

- **2015** — Founding member and research scientist at [[OpenAI]]
- **2024** — Returned to [[OpenAI]], built a new team focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation
- **State of GPT** @ Microsoft Build 2023 — Keynote presentation on GPT

### Educational Content

- **[[Zero to Hero]]** — Lecture series covering LLM fundamentals, including:
  - Building GPT from scratch in code
  - Activations and gradients
  - Batch normalization
  - Backpropagation
- **Intro to Large Language Models** — 1M view YouTube lecture

## Key Topics in LLM Education

- **Tokenization** — How text is converted to numerical representations
- **Transformer architecture** — Self-attention mechanisms
- **Training pipeline** — Pretraining, midtraining, and post-training
- **Synthetic data** — Generated data for model training
- **Backpropagation** — Spelled-out introduction to the algorithm

## Related Projects

- **[[Micrograd]]** — Tiny autograd engine for learning backpropagation
- **[[makemore]]** — Series of projects building character-level and word-level generative models
- **[[Char-RNN]]** — Character-level language model using RNNs

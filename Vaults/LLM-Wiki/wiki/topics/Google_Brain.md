# Google Brain

[[Google Brain]] (now part of [[Google DeepMind]]) was a research initiative focused on deep learning and artificial intelligence. [[Andrej Karpathy]] completed an internship at Google Brain in 2011, working on unsupervised learning from videos.

## Karpathy's Work at Google Brain

- **2011** — Internship at (baby) Google Brain
- **Research focus:** Learning-scale unsupervised learning from videos

## Related Google Affiliations

- **[[Google Research]]** — Karpathy completed a second internship in 2013 working on large-scale supervised learning on YouTube videos

## Key People

- Karpathy's work at Google Brain contributed to his later PhD research on visual understanding and multimodal learning at [[Stanford University]]

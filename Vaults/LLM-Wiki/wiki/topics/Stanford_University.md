# Stanford University

[[Stanford University]] is a research university in California where [[Andrej Karpathy]] completed his PhD from 2011 to 2017. His PhD thesis was titled *Connecting Images and Natural Language*, advised by [[Fei-Fei Li]].

## Key People

- **[[Fei-Fei Li]]** — Karpathy's PhD advisor; member of the Stanford Vision Lab
- **[[Andrew Ng]]** — Karpathy worked with him during his first-year rotation program
- **[[Daphne Koller]]** — Karpathy worked with her during his first-year rotation program
- **[[Sebastian Thrun]]** — Karpathy worked with him during his first-year rotation program
- **[[Vladlen Koltun]]** — Karpathy worked with him during his first-year rotation program
- **[[Justin Johnson]]** — Collaborator on multiple research projects including DenseCap and Visualizing and Understanding Recurrent Networks

## Notable Courses

### CS231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition

Designed and primarily instructed by [[Andrej Karpathy]], CS231n was the first deep learning class at Stanford. It became one of the largest courses at the university, growing from 150 students in 2015 to 330 in 2016 and 750 in 2017.

## Notable Publications (Karpathy-era)

- *DenseCap: Fully Convolutional Localization Networks for Dense Captioning* (CVPR 2016, Oral)
- *Visualizing and Understanding Recurrent Networks* (ICLR 2016 Workshop)
- *Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments for Generating Image Descriptions* (CVPR 2015, Oral)
- *Large-Scale Video Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks* (CVPR 2014, Oral)
- *Emergence of Object-Selective Features in Unsupervised Feature Learning* (NIPS 2012)

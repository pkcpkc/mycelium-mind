# Andrej Karpathy

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher, educator, and entrepreneur known for his work in computer vision, deep learning, and large language models.

## Summary

Founding member of [[OpenAI]] (2015), later Director of AI at [[Tesla]] leading Autopilot computer vision, and returned to [[OpenAI]] in 2024 for midtraining and synthetic data. PhD from [[Stanford University]] under [[Fei-Fei Li]]. Creator of the popular [[CS231n]] deep learning course and the [[Zero to Hero]] YouTube lecture series.

## Key Contributions

- **OpenAI** — Founding member and research scientist (2015); returned 2024 for midtraining/synthetic data team
- **Tesla** — Director of AI, led computer vision for [[Autopilot]] and Tesla Optimus
- **CS231n** — First deep learning class at Stanford, grew from 150 to 750 students
- **YouTube Education** — Zero to Hero series, GPT-from-scratch (3.5M views), neural network backprop explanation (1.2M views)
- **Blog Writing** — Notable posts: *Software 2.0*, *The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks*

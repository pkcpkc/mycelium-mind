# Natural Language Processing

[[Natural Language Processing]] (NLP) is a field of AI focused on enabling computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language. [[Andrej Karpathy]] has contributed to NLP through his work on image captioning, recurrent neural networks for text, and large language models.

## Key Contributions

- **Image Captioning** — Bridging computer vision and NLP through neural networks that generate text descriptions from images
  - **[[NeuralTalk2]]** — Early image captioning in [[Torch]]
  - **DenseCap** (CVPR 2016, Oral) — Dense captioning with localization
  - **Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments** (CVPR 2015, Oral)
- **Recurrent Neural Networks for Text**
  - **[[Char-RNN]]** — Character-level language model using LSTMs/GRUs/RNNs
  - **The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks** (2015) — Influential blog post
- **Large Language Models**
  - **[[Zero to Hero]]** — Lecture series on building LLMs from scratch, including GPT
  - **State of GPT** @ Microsoft Build 2023

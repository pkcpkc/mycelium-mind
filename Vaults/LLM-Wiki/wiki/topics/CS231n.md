# CS231n

[[CS231n]]: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition is the first deep learning class taught at [[Stanford University]], designed and primarily instructed by [[Andrej Karpathy]].

## Course Growth

- **2015:** 150 students
- **2016:** 330 students
- **2017:** 750 students

The course became one of the largest at Stanford and remains a flagship deep learning education resource.

## Curriculum Focus

- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
- Image classification
- Object detection
- Image segmentation
- Visual recognition

## Legacy

Karpathy's CS231n course helped popularize deep learning education and served as a template for similar courses worldwide. His teaching approach influenced his later [[Zero to Hero]] YouTube lecture series on neural networks and LLMs.

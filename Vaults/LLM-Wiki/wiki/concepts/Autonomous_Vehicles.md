# Autonomous Vehicles

## Summary

Autonomous vehicles are self-driving systems that use AI, sensor fusion, and machine learning to navigate roads and make driving decisions without human intervention.

## Key Details

- Tesla Autopilot is a prominent implementation, focusing on computer vision-based perception for navigation
- The system improves driving safety and convenience, with the long-term goal of achieving Full Self-Driving at scale
- Karpathy led the computer vision team behind Tesla Autopilot during his tenure as Director of AI at Tesla
- The work was showcased in the August 2021 Tesla AI Day presentation
- Requires large-scale data labeling, neural network training, and optimization for custom inference hardware

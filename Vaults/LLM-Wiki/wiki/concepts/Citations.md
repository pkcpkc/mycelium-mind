# Citations

## Summary

In the Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.1, Citations are standardized references used to link to external sources, documents, or supplementary materials. They form a core structural convention of the knowledge bundle system, ensuring traceability, proper attribution, and seamless cross-referencing within a markdown-based knowledge graph.

## Key Details

- Serve as a foundational structural convention alongside Index Files and Log Files within the OKF specification.
- Enable traceability and attribution for external knowledge sources referenced in concept documents.
- Designed to be human-readable and machine-parsable, aligning with OKF's emphasis on familiar markdown workflows.
- Support the permissive consumption model by allowing agents to gracefully handle and validate external references.

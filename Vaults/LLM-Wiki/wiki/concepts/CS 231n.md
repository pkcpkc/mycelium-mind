# CS 231n

## Summary

CS 231n is a flagship computer vision and deep learning course at Stanford University, originally designed and taught by AI researcher Andrej Karpathy. The course has grown into one of the university's largest and most influential classes, serving as a foundational educational resource for understanding neural networks and visual recognition systems.

## Key Details

- Created and taught by [[Andrej Karpathy]] at [[Stanford University]].
- Focuses on deep learning architectures, convolutional neural networks, and modern computer vision techniques.
- Recognized as one of Stanford's largest enrollment courses, reflecting its high demand and educational impact.
- Serves as a primary curriculum for training students in practical deep learning frameworks and model deployment.

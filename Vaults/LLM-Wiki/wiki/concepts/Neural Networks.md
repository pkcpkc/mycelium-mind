# Neural Networks

## Summary

Neural networks are computational systems composed of interconnected processing units (neurons) organized in layers, designed to model complex relationships and make predictions based on data. As the core building blocks of [[Deep Learning]], they have revolutionized fields such as [[Computer Vision]], [[Natural Language Processing]], and autonomous systems by enabling end-to-end learning from raw inputs.

## Key Details

- **Architecture & Training Mechanism**: Composed of input, hidden, and output layers with weighted connections. Learning occurs through iterative optimization, primarily utilizing [[Backpropagation]] to compute gradients and update weights via algorithms like stochastic gradient descent.
- **Specialized Variants**: Evolved into domain-specific architectures, including [[Convolutional Neural Networks]] for spatial data (e.g., image recognition on [[ImageNet]]), recurrent structures for sequential modeling, and transformer-based designs that power modern [[Large Language Models]].
- **Educational & Research Impact**: Central to contemporary AI education and open-source development. Pioneering work by researchers like [[Andrej Karpathy]] has advanced their practical application in vision systems, language modeling, and synthetic data pipelines. His pedagogical contributions, including [[CS 231n]] at [[Stanford University]], have standardized neural network instruction globally.
- **Development Ecosystem**: Supported by modern deep learning frameworks like [[PyTorch]], which facilitate rapid prototyping, dynamic computation graphs, and scalable training. Lightweight educational tools like [[micrograd]], [[char-rnn]], [[neuraltalk2]], and [[ConvNetJS]] provide transparent, from-scratch implementations for learning and experimentation.
- **Production Deployment**: Scaled across enterprise and research environments, including [[Tesla Autopilot]] for real-time autonomous driving inference and [[OpenAI]]'s infrastructure for large-scale model training and midtraining. Requires robust data pipelines, optimized inference on specialized hardware, and continuous monitoring.

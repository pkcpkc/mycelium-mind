# stubble

## Summary

Facial hair characterized by short, trimmed growth on the chin and jawline, often appearing as a light beard or shadow. It is frequently captured in head-and-shoulders portraits, adding texture and subtle definition to a subject's complexion.

## Key Details

- Covers the jawline and chin area, distinguishing it from a full beard or clean-shaven appearance.
- Appears as light, short hair growth that creates subtle shadows and visual texture on the face.
- Commonly associated with a casual or mature aesthetic, frequently observed in photographic portraiture and everyday styling.
- Requires minimal maintenance compared to longer facial hair styles, typically maintained through regular trimming or shaving.

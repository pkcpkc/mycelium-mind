# ConvNetJS

## Summary

ConvNetJS is an open-source JavaScript library that enables the definition, training, and inference of neural networks directly within web browsers or Node.js environments. Created by Andrej Karpathy, it serves as a lightweight, dependency-free educational tool that abstracts the complexities of backpropagation and optimization, allowing developers and students to experiment with deep learning architectures without relying on Python or specialized hardware.

## Key Details

- Implements core deep learning operations entirely in JavaScript, making it highly portable and accessible for web-based AI applications.
- Supports a variety of neural network layers, including convolutional (ConvNet), recurrent (LSTM/GRU), fully connected, and pooling layers.
- Designed primarily for educational and prototyping purposes, offering transparency into model architecture and training dynamics.
- Integrates with Karpathy's broader ecosystem of AI educational projects, such as [[micrograd]], [[char-rnn]], and [[neuraltalk2]].
- Enables client-side model deployment, reducing latency and preserving user privacy by keeping data on-device.

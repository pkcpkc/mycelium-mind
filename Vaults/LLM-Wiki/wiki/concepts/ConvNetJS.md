# ConvNetJS

ConvNetJS is a deep learning library written entirely in JavaScript. It enables training convolutional neural networks (and ordinary neural networks) directly in the web browser, with many interactive web demos. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Creator:** Andrej Karpathy developed ConvNetJS as one of his early pet projects. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Features:** The library provides a browser-based environment for training neural networks without server-side computation, enabling educational demos and interactive visualizations of deep learning. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Follow-up Projects:** Karpathy created several related JavaScript-based deep learning projects including tSNEJS, REINFORCEjs, recurrentjs, and GANs in JS. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Interview:** Karpathy gave an interview with Data Science Weekly about ConvNetJS and its backstory. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

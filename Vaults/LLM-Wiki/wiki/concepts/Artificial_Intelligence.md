# Artificial Intelligence

## Summary

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the broad field of study focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, including reasoning, learning, perception, and decision-making.

## Key Details

- Encompasses multiple subfields including machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, and natural language processing
- Serves as the foundational umbrella concept under which all other AI-related technologies fall
- Karpathy's work at OpenAI and Tesla spans multiple AI disciplines, from computer vision for Autopilot to large language models
- The field has evolved from rule-based expert systems to data-driven approaches powered by neural networks

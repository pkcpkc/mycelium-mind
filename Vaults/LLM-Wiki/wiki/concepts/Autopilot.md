# Autopilot

## Summary

Autopilot is Tesla's advanced driver-assistance system (ADAS) that uses computer vision and deep learning to enable semi-autonomous and eventually fully autonomous driving capabilities.

## Key Details

- Led by **Andrej Karpathy** during his tenure as Director of AI at **Tesla**.
- The Autopilot team handled in-house data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on Tesla's custom inference chip.
- The system is built on computer vision techniques and deep learning models trained on large-scale driving data.
- The long-term goal is to achieve Full Self-Driving (FSD) at scale, with the immediate aim of improving driving safety.
- Karpathy presented on Tesla AI and Autopilot at events including **Tesla AI Day 2021** and **AI for Full Self-Driving** at CVPR 2021 and ScaledML 2020.

# Synthetic Data

## Summary

Synthetic Data is artificially generated data that mimics real-world data, used to augment training datasets and improve model performance across AI applications.

## Key Details

- A focus area for Karpathy's new team at OpenAI, working alongside midtraining
- Used to supplement or replace real-world data in training datasets, especially when real data is scarce or expensive to collect
- Helps address data bottlenecks in model development pipelines
- Can improve model robustness by providing diverse training examples that may not exist in real-world datasets
- Plays a key role in both LLM development and computer vision training for autonomous systems

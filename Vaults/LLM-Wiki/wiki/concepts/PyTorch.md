# PyTorch

## Summary

PyTorch is an open-source deep learning framework that provides flexible tensor computing and automatic differentiation for building and training neural networks. It has become one of the most widely used frameworks in both research and production settings.

## Key Details

- **Andrej Karpathy** has used PyTorch extensively throughout his career, from his PhD work at **Stanford** to his roles at **OpenAI** and **Tesla**.
- Karpathy built **micrograd**, a tiny scalar-valued autograd engine that implements backpropagation with a **PyTorch**-like API, demonstrating the simplicity of autograd mechanics.
- Karpathy delivered a talk titled "PyTorch at Tesla" at PyTorch DevCon 2019, showcasing how PyTorch powers neural network training and deployment at scale.
- At **Tesla**, Karpathy's team used PyTorch for in-house data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on Tesla's custom inference chip for **Autopilot**.
- PyTorch is widely used in the deep learning research community and is a core tool for implementing **Convolutional Neural Networks**, **Recurrent Neural Networks**, and **Transformer Architecture** models.
- Educational resources like Karpathy's **Zero to Hero** playlist and **Stanford CS231n** teach deep learning concepts using PyTorch as a primary framework.

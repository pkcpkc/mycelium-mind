# PyTorch

## Summary

PyTorch is an open-source machine learning framework built on the Torch library, designed with a Python-first approach and dynamic computation graphs to facilitate rapid prototyping, research, and production deployment of deep learning models.

## Key Details

- Features automatic differentiation and eager execution, enabling intuitive debugging and flexible model architectures commonly used in [[Computer Vision]] and [[Large Language Models]].
- Serves as the foundational training infrastructure for major AI organizations, including [[Tesla]]'s deployment of neural networks for [[Tesla Autopilot]].
- Heavily integrated into academic curricula and university research pipelines, notably supporting courses like [[CS 231n]] and projects originating from [[Stanford University]].
- Actively maintained by Meta AI and a broad open-source community, with strong hardware acceleration support via CUDA and custom inference runtimes.
- Frequently showcased at industry developer conferences, including presentations on scaling and deployment strategies at PyTorch DevCon.

# beard

## Summary

A beard is facial hair that grows on the chin and jawline, often used to define facial structure or express personal style. In visual media and portraiture, it ranges from light stubble to full growth, influencing how light interacts with the face and contributing to the subject's overall aesthetic and perceived age.

## Key Details

- Covers the lower facial region, specifically the chin and jawline.
- Varies in length and density; short growth is commonly referred to as [[stubble]].
- Requires regular grooming and maintenance to achieve desired length or shape.
- In photography, it creates natural shadows and contours that can enhance facial definition and lighting dynamics.

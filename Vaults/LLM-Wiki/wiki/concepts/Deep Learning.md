# Deep Learning

## Summary

Deep learning is a foundational subfield of artificial intelligence that leverages multi-layered neural networks to automatically learn hierarchical representations from vast amounts of data. It has driven transformative advancements in fields such as [[Computer Vision]], [[Large Language Models]], and autonomous systems. Pioneered and advanced by leading researchers and educators, it has evolved from theoretical frameworks like [[Backpropagation]] into highly optimized, real-world industrial applications powered by modern toolchains like [[PyTorch]].

## Key Details

- **Architecture & Optimization:** Built upon multi-layered neural networks trained via gradient-based optimization and [[Backpropagation]]. Modern implementations heavily utilize GPU acceleration to handle complex computations and large-scale datasets like [[ImageNet]].
- **Key Applications:** Powers state-of-the-art systems in [[Computer Vision]] (e.g., object detection, image captioning), [[Natural Language Processing]], and autonomous driving platforms like [[Tesla Autopilot]].
- **Educational & Open-Source Ecosystem:** Widely disseminated through structured academic curricula such as [[CS 231n]] and self-contained educational projects like [[micrograd]], [[char-rnn]], and [[neuraltalk2]], which facilitate hands-on understanding of network architecture and training.
- **Framework & Toolchain Dependency:** Relies heavily on flexible, Python-centric frameworks like [[PyTorch]] for rapid prototyping, research iteration, and deployment across both academic and commercial environments.
- **Research & Industry Synergy:** Continuous innovation is driven by collaborative partnerships between academia and industry, focusing on areas like synthetic data generation, midtraining techniques, and visual-semantic alignment.

# portrait

## Summary

A portrait is a focused visual representation of a person, typically emphasizing facial features, expression, and upper-body framing. It serves to capture individual characteristics, emotional tone, and stylistic details while minimizing environmental distractions through deliberate compositional and lighting techniques.

## Key Details

- **Framing & Composition**: Often utilizes a head-and-shoulders or bust composition to isolate the subject and maintain visual focus on the face and upper torso.
- **Facial Characteristics & Expression**: Highlights distinctive traits such as complexion, hairline, and facial hair (e.g., stubble or beard), while capturing natural expressions like direct eye contact and smiles that reveal micro-expressions (e.g., crinkles around the eyes).
- **Lighting & Contour**: Relies on soft, directional lighting to illuminate one side of the face more prominently, casting gentle shadows that define facial structure and add dimensional depth.
- **Background & Depth of Field**: Employs softly out-of-focus or muted backgrounds with subtle environmental cues (e.g., vertical architectural lines, outdoor tones) to keep attention on the subject without complete isolation.
- **Attire & Styling**: Incorporates simple, textured clothing (e.g., crew-neck t-shirts with natural fabric wrinkles) that complements the subject’s appearance without competing with facial focus.

# Convolutional Neural Networks

## Summary

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are a class of deep neural networks designed for processing grid-structured data such as images, using convolutional layers to automatically detect spatial hierarchies of features.

## Key Details

- **Andrej Karpathy**'s PhD at **Stanford** focused on convolutional and recurrent neural networks and their applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and their intersection.
- Karpathy designed and taught **Stanford CS231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition**, the first deep learning class at Stanford.
- CNNs were central to Karpathy's early career, including his work on **ImageNet** image categorization where he competed against an early ConvNet.
- Key CNN-based projects and publications include **DenseCap** (CVPR 2016, Oral) for dense captioning, **Large-Scale Video Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks** (CVPR 2014, Oral), and **neuraltalk2** for image captioning.
- **ConvNetJS**, a JavaScript-based deep learning library by Karpathy, enables browser-based training of convolutional neural networks.
- CNNs are foundational to **computer vision** applications including **Autopilot** at **Tesla**.

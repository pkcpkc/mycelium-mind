# CS 231n

CS 231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition is a deep learning course offered by Stanford University, created and taught by Andrej Karpathy. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Course Description:** The course covers convolutional neural networks, recurrent neural networks, and their applications to image recognition, natural language processing, and other visual tasks. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Growth:** The class became one of the largest at Stanford, growing from 150 enrolled students in 2015 to 330 students in 2016, and 750 students in 2017. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Resources:** The course provides lecture videos, course notes, a course syllabus, and discussion forums (r/cs231n). [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Significance:** Karpathy was the primary instructor for the first deep learning class at Stanford, and it has become one of its most popular and widely referenced courses. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

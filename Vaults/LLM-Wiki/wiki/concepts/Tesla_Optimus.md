# Tesla Optimus

## Summary

Tesla Optimus is Tesla's humanoid robot project, initially overseen by Karpathy during his brief involvement with the initiative as Director of AI at Tesla.

## Key Details

- Karpathy briefly led work on Tesla Optimus alongside his responsibilities for the Autopilot computer vision team
- The project represents Tesla's expansion beyond autonomous vehicles into general-purpose humanoid robotics
- Requires similar AI capabilities including computer vision, neural network training, and sensor fusion
- Part of Tesla's broader ambition to apply AI across multiple domains including transportation and robotics

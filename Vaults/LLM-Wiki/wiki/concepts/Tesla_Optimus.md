# Tesla Optimus

## Summary

Tesla Optimus is Tesla's humanoid robot program, which Andrej Karpathy briefly led during his tenure as Director of AI at Tesla. The project applies computer vision and deep learning techniques to enable humanoid robots for general-purpose tasks.

## Key Details

- Karpathy briefly oversaw the Tesla Optimus program during his time as Director of AI at [[Tesla]].
- The project leverages the same computer vision and deep learning infrastructure developed for Tesla's [[Autopilot]] system.
- Optimus aims to create general-purpose humanoid robots capable of performing tasks in environments designed for humans.
- The program represents an extension of Tesla's AI capabilities beyond autonomous vehicles into physical robotics and manipulation.
- Karpathy's work on physically-simulated figures during his MSc at the University of British Columbia, applying machine learning to agile robotics in simulation, provided relevant background for this work.

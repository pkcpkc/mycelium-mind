# OpenAI

## Summary

OpenAI is an artificial intelligence research organization known for developing groundbreaking models and products including ChatGPT, which sparked widespread public interest in conversational AI and large language models.

## Key Details

- **Andrej Karpathy** is a founding member of **OpenAI**, having joined in 2015 and later returning to lead a team focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation.
- OpenAI developed **ChatGPT**, a large language model that became a landmark product in conversational AI and drew widespread public attention to generative language models.
- Karpathy's work at OpenAI contributed to the advancement of **Large Language Models** and the broader ecosystem of generative AI.
- OpenAI's research has pushed the boundaries of what **Transformer Architecture**-based models can achieve in natural language understanding and generation.
- The organization has been a driving force behind the rapid progress in **Deep Learning** applications, from language models to vision systems.
- OpenAI's products and research have influenced the broader AI community, inspiring educational content like Karpathy's **Zero to Hero** playlist and public talks on the "State of GPT."

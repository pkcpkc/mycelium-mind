# OpenAI

OpenAI is an artificial intelligence research laboratory and company founded in December 2015. It was established as a non-profit organization with the mission to ensure that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all of humanity. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Founding Members:** Andrej Karpathy, [[Sam Altman]], [[Elon Musk]], [[Greg Brockman]], [[Ilya Sutskever]], and others. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Key Milestones:**
- 2015: OpenAI founded as a non-profit. Andrej Karpathy joined as a research scientist and founding member. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]
- 2019: OpenAI transitioned to a "capped-profit" structure. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]
- 2023: OpenAI released ChatGPT, achieving rapid mainstream adoption.

**Notable Projects:** GPT series (GPT-1 through GPT-4), DALL-E (image generation), Codex (code generation), and various robotics initiatives. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Karpathy's Role:** Karpathy returned to OpenAI after his time at Tesla to build a new team working on midtraining and synthetic data generation. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

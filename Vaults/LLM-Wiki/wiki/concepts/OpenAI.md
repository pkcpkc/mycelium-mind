# OpenAI

## Summary

OpenAI is a leading artificial intelligence research laboratory and technology company established in 2015. It has become a central hub for foundational AI research, particularly in large language models, computer vision, and advanced training pipelines. The organization hosts prominent researchers such as Andrej Karpathy, who joined as a founding member and continues to drive key initiatives in model optimization and data synthesis.

## Key Details

- Founded in 2015 as a research organization aimed at developing and promoting friendly AI.
- Andrej Karpathy serves as a founding member and currently focuses on midtraining techniques and synthetic data generation to enhance model performance and alignment.
- The organization has pioneered advancements in transformer architectures, instruction tuning, and scalable training infrastructure.
- Research initiatives frequently intersect with deep learning, reinforcement learning, and natural language processing, contributing to both academic and industrial AI progress.

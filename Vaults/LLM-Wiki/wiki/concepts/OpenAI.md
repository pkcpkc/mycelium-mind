# OpenAI

OpenAI is an artificial intelligence research organization. Andrej Karpathy was a founding member and research scientist at OpenAI in 2015, and returned to OpenAI in 2025 where he built a new team working on midtraining and synthetic data generation.

## Key People

- [[Andrej Karpathy]] - Founding member (2015) and returning team lead (2025)

## Timeline

- **2015**: Andrej Karpathy joined as research scientist and founding member.
- **2025**: Andrej Karpathy returned to OpenAI, built team for midtraining and synthetic data generation.

# Tesla Autopilot

## Summary

Tesla Autopilot is an advanced driver-assistance system developed by Tesla that leverages computer vision and deep learning to enable automated driving capabilities. Under the direction of former Director of AI Andrej Karpathy, the system's development emphasized large-scale neural network training, rigorous data labeling pipelines, and efficient deployment on custom inference hardware.

## Key Details

- Led by Andrej Karpathy during his tenure as Director of AI at Tesla, focusing on the computer vision and AI stack for autonomous driving.
- Relies heavily on deep learning models trained on massive, continuously updated datasets of real-world driving footage.
- Features a sophisticated data labeling and annotation infrastructure to ensure model accuracy, safety, and edge-case coverage.
- Optimized for deployment on Tesla's custom in-house inference chips to enable low-latency, real-time perception and decision-making.
- Serves as the foundational AI layer for Tesla's Full Self-Driving (FSD) and autonomous vehicle initiatives.

# Zero to Hero

## Summary

Zero to Hero is a YouTube playlist created and taught by **Andrej Karpathy** that covers the internals of large language models from scratch. It serves as an educational resource for understanding deep learning concepts through hands-on implementation.

## Key Details

- Part of **Andrej Karpathy**'s YouTube channel, alongside the general audience track on LLMs.
- Covers building **GPT**, **makemore**, and **micrograd** with detailed explanations of backpropagation, activations, gradients, and batch normalization.
- Designed to take learners from foundational concepts to building working LLM systems.
- Focuses on practical, code-first understanding of neural network training and inference.

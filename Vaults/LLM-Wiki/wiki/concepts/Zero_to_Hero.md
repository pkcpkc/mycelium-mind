# Zero to Hero

## Summary

Zero to Hero is Karpathy's educational YouTube track that teaches learners how to build language models from scratch, providing a comprehensive introduction to deep learning and LLM fundamentals.

## Key Details

- One of several educational tracks on Karpathy's YouTube channel focused on AI education
- Organized as a technical track that walks through building language models from the ground up
- Complements other tracks including "Deep Dive into LLMs", "How I Use LLMs", and "Intro to Large Language Models"
- Covers foundational concepts including neural networks, training procedures, and model architecture
- Part of Karpathy's broader mission to make AI education accessible through his YouTube channel

# ChatGPT

## Summary

ChatGPT is a large language model developed by **OpenAI** that became a landmark product in conversational AI, drawing widespread public attention to the capabilities of generative language models.

## Key Details

- Developed by **OpenAI**, the organization where **Andrej Karpathy** is a founding member.
- ChatGPT was featured in **Karpathy**'s general audience YouTube video track, which includes deep dives into LLMs like ChatGPT and practical guides on using them.
- The launch of ChatGPT significantly accelerated public awareness and adoption of large language models.
- Karpathy's **Zero to Hero** YouTube playlist covers the internals of LLMs including building GPT-style models from scratch.
- At the **Microsoft Build 2023** conference, **Karpathy** delivered a "State of GPT" talk discussing the trajectory of models like ChatGPT.

# X

## Summary

X (formerly Twitter) is a social media and microblogging platform that allows users to post short messages called tweets. Originally launched in 2006, it became a central hub for real-time discussion across industries. Under Elon Musk's ownership starting in 2022, the platform was rebranded to X in 2023, with plans to evolve into an "everything app."

## Key Details

- **Founded**: March 2006 by Jack Dorsey, Noah Glass, Biz Stone, and Evan Williams
- **Rebrand**: Changed name from Twitter to X in July 2023 after acquisition by Elon Musk
- **Primary Use**: Real-time information sharing, news dissemination, professional networking, and public discourse
- **Tech Community Role**: Widely used by AI researchers, engineers, and companies (including OpenAI, Google Brain, DeepMind) for sharing research updates, engaging in technical discussions, and announcing new developments
- **Notable Users**: Many prominent AI figures including Andrej Karpathy use the platform for sharing work and engaging with the community
- **Features**: Microblogging posts, threaded conversations, media sharing, hashtags, lists, and verified subscriptions

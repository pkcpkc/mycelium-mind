# Log Files

## Summary

Log Files are a structural convention within the Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.1 specification, designed to track change history across knowledge bundles. They provide an auditable record of modifications and updates, complementing other OKF conventions to maintain knowledge transparency and evolutionary context.

## Key Details

- Serves as a historical record for tracking changes and updates within OKF v0.1 knowledge structures
- Works alongside [[Index Files]] for progressive disclosure and [[Citations]] for external references
- Supports the OKF permissive consumption model by maintaining clear historical context
- Aligns with familiar developer workflows such as version control and distributed data cataloging
- Enables agents and humans to trace the evolution of concepts and bundles over time

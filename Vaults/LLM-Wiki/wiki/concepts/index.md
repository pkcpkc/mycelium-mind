# Concepts

* [[Artificial Intelligence]] - The broad field of study focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence.
* [[Autonomous Vehicles]] - Self-driving vehicles that use AI, sensor fusion, and machine learning to navigate and make driving decisions without human intervention.
* [[Computer Vision]] - A field of AI focused on enabling machines to interpret and understand visual information from images and video.
* [[Data Labeling]] - The process of annotating raw data with labels or tags to create supervised training datasets for machine learning models.
* [[Deep Learning]] - A subset of machine learning using multi-layered neural networks to learn hierarchical representations from large amounts of data.
* [[Large Language Models]] - AI models trained on vast text corpora that can generate human-like text, understand natural language, and perform a wide range of language tasks.
* [[Machine Learning]] - A subset of AI where systems learn patterns from data to make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every scenario.
* [[Midtraining]] - A stage in the model development pipeline that occurs between pretraining and instruction tuning, used to refine model capabilities.
* [[Neural Networks]] - Computing systems inspired by biological neural networks that form the architectural foundation of deep learning models.
* [[Synthetic Data]] - Artificially generated data that mimics real-world data, used to augment training datasets and improve model performance.
* [[Tesla Autopilot]] - Tesla's advanced driver assistance system powered by computer vision and neural networks, designed to improve driving safety and enable self-driving.
* [[Tesla Optimus]] - Tesla's humanoid robot project, initially overseen by Karpathy during his brief involvement with the initiative.
* [[Zero to Hero]] - Karpathy's educational YouTube track that teaches learners how to build language models from scratch, covering deep learning fundamentals.

# Concepts

* [[Autopilot]] - Tesla's advanced driver-assistance system (ADAS) and full self-driving capability, led by Andrej Karpathy's computer vision team.
* [[ChatGPT]] - A large language model developed by OpenAI that sparked widespread public interest in conversational AI and LLMs.
* [[Computer Vision]] - A field of artificial intelligence focused on enabling machines to interpret and understand visual information from the world.
* [[ConvNetJS]] - A deep learning library written entirely in JavaScript, enabling browser-based demos for training convolutional neural networks.
* [[Convolutional Neural Networks]] - A class of deep neural networks primarily used for image recognition and processing, employing convolutional layers to detect spatial patterns.
* [[DeepMind]] - A pioneering artificial intelligence research laboratory acquired by Google, known for breakthroughs in deep reinforcement learning.
* [[Deep Learning]] - A subset of machine learning using multi-layered neural networks to learn hierarchical representations from large datasets.
* [[GitHub]] - A web-based platform for version control and collaboration using Git, widely used by developers to host and share open-source projects.
* [[Google Brain]] - A research division within Google focused on deep learning and artificial intelligence, known for foundational work in unsupervised learning.
* [[Google Research]] - Google's research division covering a broad range of AI, machine learning, and computer science topics, including large-scale supervised learning.
* [[ImageNet]] - A large-scale visual recognition benchmark dataset used to evaluate and advance computer vision and deep learning models.
* [[Large Language Models]] - AI models trained on vast text corpora that can generate, understand, and manipulate natural language at a human-like level.
* [[Natural Language Processing]] - A field of artificial intelligence focused on enabling machines to understand, interpret, and generate human language.
* [[OpenAI]] - An artificial intelligence research organization known for developing ChatGPT and advancing large language models and conversational AI.
* [[PyTorch]] - An open-source deep learning framework built by Facebook that provides flexible tensor computing and automatic differentiation for building neural networks.
* [[Recurrent Neural Networks]] - A class of neural networks designed to process sequential data by maintaining internal memory through feedback connections.
* [[Reinforcement Learning]] - A branch of machine learning where agents learn to make decisions by interacting with an environment to maximize cumulative reward.
* [[Stanford CS231n]] - A Stanford University course on convolutional neural networks for visual recognition, created and taught by Andrej Karpathy.
* [[Tesla]] - An electric vehicle and clean energy company where Andrej Karpathy served as Director of AI, leading Autopilot computer vision development.
* [[Tesla Optimus]] - Tesla's humanoid robot program that Andrej Karpathy briefly led during his tenure as Director of AI at the company.
* [[Torch]] - An open-source machine learning library and ecosystem originally developed by Facebook AI Research, written in Lua, that served as a foundational framework for deep learning research and production.
* [[Transformer Architecture]] - A deep learning architecture introduced in 2017 that uses self-attention mechanisms to process sequential data, fundamentally changing natural language processing and expanding into computer vision and other domains.
* [[X]] - A social media and microblogging platform originally known as Twitter, rebranded to X in 2023 under the ownership of Elon Musk, serving as a primary communication channel for tech leaders and researchers.
* [[Y Combinator]] - The most prominent startup accelerator in the world, founded in 2005 by Paul Graham, based in Silicon Valley, known for funding and mentoring early-stage startups including many major tech companies.
* [[YouTube]] - The world's largest video-sharing platform owned by Google, serving as a major medium for technical education, research dissemination, and AI-related content creation.
* [[Zero to Hero]] - Andrej Karpathy's YouTube playlist covering LLM internals from scratch, including building GPT, makemore, and micrograd.
* [[arxiv-sanity]] - A tool for discovering, sorting, and recommending research papers from Arxiv.
* [[char-rnn]] - A Torch character-level language model using LSTMs, GRUs, and RNNs.
* [[makemore]] - An open-source project by Andrej Karpathy for building character-level and word-level language models from scratch.
* [[micrograd]] - A tiny scalar-valued autograd engine implementing backpropagation with a PyTorch-like API.
* [[neuraltalk2|NeuralTalk2]] - An early image captioning project built by Andrej Karpathy using Lua Torch that combines convolutional and recurrent neural networks to generate natural language descriptions of images.

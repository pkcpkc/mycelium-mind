# Midtraining

## Summary

Midtraining is a stage in the model development pipeline that occurs between pretraining and instruction tuning, used to refine model capabilities before final alignment.

## Key Details

- A critical stage in the LLM development pipeline identified by Karpathy's new team at OpenAI
- Occupies the space between initial pretraining (learning general language patterns) and instruction tuning (teaching the model to follow instructions)
- Karpathy returned to OpenAI specifically to build a team focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation
- Works in conjunction with synthetic data to improve model performance on specific capabilities
- Part of the broader ML pipeline that includes pretraining, midtraining, and instruction tuning stages

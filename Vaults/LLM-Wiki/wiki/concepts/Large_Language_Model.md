# Large Language Model

Large Language Models (LLMs) are AI models trained on vast amounts of text data to understand and generate human language. They form the basis of systems like ChatGPT, GPT-4, and other generative AI systems. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Karpathy's Coverage:** Karpathy has created educational content on LLMs including "Deep Dive into LLMs like ChatGPT" covering under-the-hood fundamentals, "How I use LLMs" as a practical guide, and "Intro to Large Language Models." [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Key Concepts:** LLMs are based on transformer architecture, trained with next-token prediction objectives, and scaled through increased model size, data, and compute. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Applications:** LLMs are used for text generation, code generation (e.g., Codex), question answering, translation, summarization, and many other natural language tasks. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

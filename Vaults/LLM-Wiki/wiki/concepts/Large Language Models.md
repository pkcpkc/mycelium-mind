# Large Language Models

## Summary

Large Language Models represent a foundational paradigm in modern artificial intelligence, emphasizing the processing and generation of human language through advanced neural architectures. This field has become a central focus for AI education, research, and industry deployment, heavily shaped by contributions from leading researchers and educators.

## Key Details

- Core subject of modern AI curricula, featuring comprehensive educational series that guide learners from foundational concepts to advanced implementation.
- Regularly implemented and taught using frameworks like [[PyTorch]], alongside custom-built educational libraries such as [[micrograd]] to demystify training mechanics.
- Heavily intersects with [[Deep Learning]] and [[Computer Vision]], reflecting the multimodal and cross-domain nature of contemporary AI development.
- Industry research and training methodologies are actively advanced by organizations like [[OpenAI]], with recent focus on synthetic data generation and midtraining techniques.

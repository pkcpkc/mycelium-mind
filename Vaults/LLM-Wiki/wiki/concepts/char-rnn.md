# char-rnn

char-rnn was a character-level language model built by Andrej Karpathy using Torch, constructed from LSTMs, GRUs, and RNNs. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Description:** The project demonstrated the ability of recurrent neural networks to generate text at the character level, producing convincing pseudo-text that mimics the training data's style and structure. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Related Work:** The project is connected to Karpathy's blog post "The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks" and the minimal RNN gist. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Significance:** char-rnn was one of the early popular demonstrations of RNN-based text generation and helped introduce many developers to sequence modeling. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

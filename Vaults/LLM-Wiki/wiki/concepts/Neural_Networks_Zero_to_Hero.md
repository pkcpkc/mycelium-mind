# Neural Networks: Zero to Hero

Neural Networks: Zero to Hero is a lecture series by Andrej Karpathy covering deep learning from fundamentals to advanced topics. The series is available on YouTube and consists of parallel tracks for technical audiences and general audiences. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Tracks:**
- Technical track: Follows the Zero to Hero playlist, building neural networks from scratch. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]
- General audience track: Covers AI concepts for non-technical viewers. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Notable Videos:**
- "Let's build GPT: from scratch, in code, spelled out" — 3.5M views [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]
- "The spelled-out intro to neural networks and backpropagation" — 1.2M views [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]
- "Building makemore" series (Parts 2-5 covering MLP, WaveNet, gradients, activations) [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]
- "Intro to Large Language Models" — 1M views [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Platform:** Karpathy creates educational videos on AI for his YouTube channel, with both technical and general audience content. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

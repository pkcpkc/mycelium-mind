# Natural Language Processing

## Summary

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of artificial intelligence focused on enabling machines to understand, interpret, and generate human language. It encompasses tasks ranging from text classification and sentiment analysis to machine translation and conversational agents.

## Key Details

- **Andrej Karpathy**'s PhD at **Stanford** focused on convolutional and recurrent neural networks applied to computer vision, natural language processing, and their intersection.
- Karpathy co-authored **Grounded Compositional Semantics** (TACL 2013), a project finding and describing images with sentences, bridging computer vision and NLP.
- His early work on **neuraltalk2** applied image captioning using deep learning, combining visual recognition with natural language generation.
- Recurrent neural networks (**RNNs**) and **LSTMs** have been foundational architectures for NLP tasks, as explored in Karpathy's **char-rnn** project.
- The rise of **Large Language Models** based on the **Transformer Architecture** has transformed NLP, enabling models to achieve human-level performance on many language tasks.
- **ChatGPT** by **OpenAI** represents a landmark achievement in NLP, bringing conversational AI to mainstream adoption.

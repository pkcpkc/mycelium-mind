# Arxiv-Sanity

## Summary

Arxiv-Sanity is an influential open-source project created by Andrej Karpathy that serves as a tool for browsing and filtering academic papers on arXiv.

## Key Details

- Created by Andrej Karpathy as part of his portfolio of influential open-source projects.
- Designed to enhance the discovery and curation of research papers on arXiv, particularly within the AI and machine learning communities.
- Frequently cited alongside other Karpathy projects such as [[micrograd]], [[char-rnn]], [[neuraltalk2]], and [[ConvNetJS]].
- Widely adopted by researchers, educators, and practitioners for efficient literature discovery.

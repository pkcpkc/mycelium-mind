# Tesla Autopilot

## Summary

Tesla Autopilot is Tesla's advanced driver assistance system powered by computer vision and neural networks, designed to improve driving safety and convenience on the path toward Full Self-Driving.

## Key Details

- Led by Karpathy during his tenure as Director of AI at Tesla, who built the computer vision team behind it
- The system improves driving safety and convenience, with the long-term goal of achieving Full Self-Driving at scale
- Requires in-house data labeling at scale, neural network training, and optimization for Tesla's custom inference hardware
- The full ML pipeline — from data labeling to network training and deployment — falls under Karpathy's oversight
- The work was showcased in the August 2021 Tesla AI Day presentation

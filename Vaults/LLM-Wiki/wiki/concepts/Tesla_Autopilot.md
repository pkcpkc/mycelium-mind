# Tesla Autopilot

Tesla Autopilot is an advanced driver-assistance system (ADAS) developed by Tesla, Inc. It uses computer vision, neural networks, and sensor fusion to enable semi-autonomous driving capabilities. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Karpathy's Role:** Andrej Karpathy served as the Director of AI at Tesla, where he led the computer vision team responsible for Autopilot and the (very briefly) Tesla Optimus robot. His team handled all in-house data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on Tesla's custom inference chip. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Goals:** The Autopilot system aims to increase driving safety and convenience, with the long-term goal of achieving Full Self-Driving (FSD) at scale. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

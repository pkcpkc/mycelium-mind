# Stanford University

Stanford University is a research university in California. Andrej Karpathy earned his PhD from Stanford's Vision Lab, where he designed and led the first deep learning course (CS 231n), which grew from 150 students in 2015 to 750 students in 2017.

## Key People

- [[Andrej Karpathy]] - PhD graduate, CS 231n instructor
- [[Fei-Fei Li]] - Vision Lab, Karpathy's PhD advisor
- [[Daphne Koller]] - Faculty, worked with Karpathy during PhD rotation
- [[Andrew Ng]] - Faculty, worked with Karpathy during PhD rotation
- [[Sebastian Thrun]] - Faculty, worked with Karpathy during PhD rotation

## Timeline

- **2011**: Karpathy completed PhD at Stanford.
- **2015-2017**: Karpathy taught CS 231n, one of Stanford's largest classes.

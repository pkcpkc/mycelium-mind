# YouTube

## Summary

YouTube is the world's largest video-sharing platform, acquired by Google in 2006. It serves as a primary medium for technical education, research dissemination, and AI-related content creation. The platform hosts everything from casual content to formal lectures, tutorials, and conference talks, making it an essential distribution channel for educators and researchers in technology.

## Key Details

- **Founded**: February 2005 by Chad Hurley, Steve Chen, and Jawed Karim
- **Acquisition**: Purchased by Google in November 2006 for $1.65 billion
- **Technical Education**: Hosts numerous influential educational playlists and series on computer science, AI, and machine learning topics
- **AI Community Role**: Many prominent AI researchers and practitioners use YouTube to share lectures, tutorials, and technical deep dives with broad audiences
- **Notable Content**: Andrej Karpathy's "Zero to Hero" playlist covering LLM internals from scratch, including building GPT and explaining backpropagation, activations, gradients, and batch normalization
- **Research Dissemination**: Major AI conferences (CVPR, ICML, NeurIPS) and companies (Google, OpenAI, NVIDIA) post talks and keynotes for public access
- **YouTube at Google Research**: Andrej Karpathy worked on large-scale supervised learning on YouTube videos during his 2013 internship at Google Research

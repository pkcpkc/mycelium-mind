# Andrej Karpathy

**Andrej Karpathy** is an AI researcher, educator, and entrepreneur known for pioneering work in computer vision, deep learning education, and large language models. He was a founding member of [[OpenAI]] (2015), Director of AI at [[Tesla]], and creator of the influential CS 231n deep learning course at [[Stanford University]].

## Summary

Karpathy has had a broad impact across multiple areas of AI. At [[Tesla]], he led the computer vision team for Autopilot, handling data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on custom inference hardware. At [[OpenAI]], he was a founding member and later returned to lead midtraining and synthetic data generation efforts. His PhD research at [[Stanford]] focused on the intersection of computer vision and natural language processing, producing influential work on image captioning (neuraltalk2, DenseCap) and visual-semantic alignment.

He is widely recognized for his educational contributions: CS 231n at [[Stanford]] became one of the university's largest courses, and his YouTube channel features viral "Zero to Hero" series building LLMs and neural networks from scratch. His open-source projects — including ConvNetJS, micrograd, char-rnn, and arxiv-sanity — have influenced generations of AI practitioners.

## Key Contributions

### Computer Vision & Deep Learning Research
- **Image Captioning:** Pioneered early work with neuraltalk2 and DenseCap, connecting visual content with natural language descriptions.
- **Visual-Semantic Alignment:** Developed deep visual-semantic alignment methods for generating image descriptions and finding images with sentences.
- **Large-Scale Video Classification:** Applied convolutional neural networks to large-scale video understanding tasks.
- **ImageNet Competition:** Competed against an early ConvNet on ImageNet image categorization, later writing about lessons learned.

### LLMs & Language Models
- **PixelCNN++:** Contributed to advanced pixel-based generative models with discretized logistic mixture likelihood.
- **Recurrent Neural Networks:** Authored "The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks" (2015), a widely cited post on RNN applications.
- **LLM Education:** Created the "Zero to Hero" series teaching LLM internals from scratch, including building GPT, backpropagation, and neural networks.

### Deep Learning Education
- **CS 231n:** Stanford's first deep learning course, grew from 150 to 750 students. Covers convolutional neural networks for visual recognition.
- **YouTube Channel:** Produces technical and general-audience AI content, including "Let's build GPT" (3.5M views) and "The spelled-out intro to neural networks" (1.2M views).
- **Software 2.0:** Articulated the concept of "Software 2.0" — AI systems defined by neural networks rather than hand-coded rules.

### Open-Source Projects
- **ConvNetJS:** Deep learning library written entirely in JavaScript for browser-based demos.
- **micrograd:** Tiny autograd engine implementing backpropagation over dynamic computation graphs.
- **char-rnn:** Character-level language model using LSTMs/GRUs/RNNs.
- **arxiv-sanity:** Research paper discovery and recommendation platform.
- **neuraltalk2:** Early image captioning system in Torch.

### Robotics & AI Systems
- **Tesla Autopilot:** Led computer vision team, neural network training, data labeling, and deployment on custom inference chips.
- **Robotics:** Early work at [[UBC]] on learning controllers for physically-simulated figures (agile robotics in simulation).

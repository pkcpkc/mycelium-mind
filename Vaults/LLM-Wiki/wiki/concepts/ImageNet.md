# ImageNet

ImageNet is a visual database developed by [[Fei-Fei Li]] and her team, designed for use in object detection and image classification tasks. The ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC) has been a benchmark for evaluating computer vision algorithms.

## Key Facts

- [[Andrej Karpathy]] competed against an early ConvNet on the ImageNet categorization task, classifying images into 1,000 classes. He was jokingly referred to as the "reference human" for ImageNet.
- [[Fei-Fei Li]] was Karpathy's PhD adviser at Stanford Vision Lab.
- The ImageNet challenge paper (JCV 2015) was co-authored by [[Olga Russakovsky]], [[Jia Deng]], [[Hao Su]], and others including [[Andrej Karpathy]].

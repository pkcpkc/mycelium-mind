# ImageNet

ImageNet is a large-scale visual recognition challenge and accompanying dataset in the field of computer vision. It contains over 14 million annotated images across 20,000 categories. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**ILSVRC:** The ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC) began in 2010 and became the standard benchmark for evaluating computer vision systems. The 2015 challenge paper was authored by Olga Russakovsky, Jia Deng, Hao Su, and including Andrej Karpathy. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Karpathy's Involvement:** Andrej Karpathy is sometimes jokingly referred to as the "reference human" for ImageNet because he competed against an early ConvNet on categorizing images into 1,000 classes. This competition required custom tooling and extensive learning about dog breeds. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Publications:**
- "What I learned from competing against a ConvNet on ImageNet" — blog post by Karpathy. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]
- "ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge" — JCV 2015 paper. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]
- "Deep Fragment Embeddings for Bidirectional Image-Sentence Mapping" — NIPS 2014. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**t-SNE Visualization:** Karpathy created a t-SNE visualization of CNN codes for ImageNet, which became a popular tool for understanding how neural networks represent visual concepts. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

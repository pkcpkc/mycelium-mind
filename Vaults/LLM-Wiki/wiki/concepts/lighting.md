# lighting

## Summary

Lighting refers to the quality, direction, and source of illumination used to shape visual subjects. In portrait contexts, it is characterized as natural and soft, strategically illuminating one side of the face more brightly to create depth while casting gentle shadows on the opposite side.

## Key Details

- **Quality & Texture**: Typically soft and diffused, minimizing harsh contrasts and producing smooth tonal transitions across facial features.
- **Directionality**: Often directional (e.g., illuminating the left side more brightly) to sculpt three-dimensionality and draw attention to specific areas.
- **Shadow Control**: Generates gentle, gradual shadows that define contours without obscuring details, maintaining a balanced exposure.
- **Contextual Application**: Frequently employed in head-and-shoulders portraits to enhance mood, create eye contact emphasis, and maintain a natural aesthetic.

# Recurrent Neural Networks

Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) are a class of neural networks designed for sequential data. Variants include LSTMs and GRUs, which address the vanishing gradient problem in standard RNNs.

## Key Facts

- Used in [[char-rnn]], a Torch character-level language model by [[Andrej Karpathy]].
- Visualizing and Understanding Recurrent Networks (ICLR 2016 Workshop) by [[Justin Johnson]], [[Andrej Karpathy]], and [[Li Fei-Fei]].
- DeepMind research on deep reinforcement learning with [[Vlad Mnih]] and [[Koray Kavukcuoglu]].

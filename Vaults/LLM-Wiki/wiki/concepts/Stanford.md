# Stanford University

Stanford University is a private research university located in Stanford, California. It was founded in 1885 by Leland Stanford and Jane Stanford in memory of their son, Leland Stanford Jr. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Computer Vision & AI Research:** The Stanford Vision Lab, led by [[Fei-Fei Li]], is a prominent research group in computer vision and AI. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Notable Courses:**
- CS 231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition — taught by Andrej Karpathy starting in 2015. The class grew from 150 students in 2015 to 330 in 2016 and 750 in 2017, becoming one of the largest at Stanford. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Notable Alumni and Faculty:**
- Andrej Karpathy (PhD, 2016) — advised by Fei-Fei Li. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]
- Andrew Ng — former faculty member. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]
- Daphne Koller — former faculty member. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]
- Sebastian Thrun — former faculty member. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

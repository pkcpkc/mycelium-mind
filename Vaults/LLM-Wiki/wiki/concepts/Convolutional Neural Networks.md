# Convolutional Neural Networks

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are a class of deep neural networks primarily used for image recognition and processing. They have become the standard architecture for computer vision tasks.

## Key Facts

- Popularized by [[Fei-Fei Li]]'s ImageNet work and the Stanford CS231n course taught by [[Andrej Karpathy]].
- [[Andrej Karpathy]] competed against an early ConvNet on ImageNet classification.
- [[ConvNetJS]] is a JavaScript implementation of CNNs created by Andrej Karpathy.

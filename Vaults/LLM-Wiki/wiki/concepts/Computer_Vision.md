# Computer Vision

## Summary

Computer Vision is a field of AI focused on enabling machines to interpret and understand visual information from images and video, powering applications ranging from autonomous driving to object recognition.

## Key Details

- Central to Karpathy's work at Tesla, where he led the computer vision team behind Tesla Autopilot
- Requires in-house data labeling at scale to train perception models
- Neural networks are trained and optimized for Tesla's custom inference hardware
- Key applications include object detection, scene understanding, and real-time video processing
- Integral to autonomous vehicle systems that rely on camera-based perception

# Computer Vision

## Summary

Computer Vision is a branch of artificial intelligence that enables machines to interpret, understand, and derive meaningful information from visual data such as images and video streams.

## Key Details

- **Andrej Karpathy**'s PhD research at **Stanford** focused on convolutional and recurrent neural networks applied to computer vision.
- Karpathy served as Director of AI at **Tesla**, where he led the computer vision team for **Autopilot**.
- He designed and taught **Stanford CS231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition**, which grew from 150 students in 2015 to 750 by 2017.
- Key computer vision publications include **DenseCap** (CVPR 2016, Oral) for dense captioning and **Grounded Compositional Semantics** (TACL 2013) for finding and describing images with sentences.
- Karpathy was known for competing against an early **ConvNet** on **ImageNet** image categorization, earning the nickname "reference human for ImageNet."
- His early work at **Google Brain** and **Google Research** involved large-scale supervised learning on visual data from YouTube videos.

# Neural Networks

## Summary

Neural Networks are computing systems inspired by biological neural networks that form the architectural foundation of deep learning models used across AI applications.

## Key Details

- The core building block of modern deep learning systems, from computer vision to language models
- Karpathy oversees neural network training and deployment as part of his work at Tesla
- Networks must be optimized for custom inference hardware, such as Tesla's autonomous driving chips
- Enable learning of hierarchical representations from data through multiple layers of computation
- Central to both computer vision (Tesla Autopilot) and language model (OpenAI) applications

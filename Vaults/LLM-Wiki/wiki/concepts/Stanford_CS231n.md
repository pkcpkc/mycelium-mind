# Stanford CS231n

## Summary

Stanford CS231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition is a university course designed and primarily taught by Andrej Karpathy. It was the first deep learning class offered at Stanford and became one of the university's largest courses.

## Key Details

- Karpathy designed and served as the primary instructor for CS231n, teaching convolutional neural networks and their applications to visual recognition.
- The course grew from 150 students in its first offering (2015) to 750 students by 2017, making it one of Stanford's largest classes.
- The course covers topics including [[Convolutional Neural Networks]], [[Deep Learning]], and [[Computer Vision]].
- CS231n contributed to Karpathy's reputation as an educator and helped popularize deep learning education alongside his other teaching efforts like the [[Zero to Hero]] playlist.
- The course materials are publicly available and have been widely used by students and practitioners worldwide.

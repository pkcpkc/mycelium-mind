# makemore

## Summary

makemore is an open-source project by **Andrej Karpathy** that teaches how to build character-level and word-level language models from scratch, serving as a practical educational tool in his **Zero to Hero** series.

## Key Details

- Featured in **Andrej Karpathy**'s **Zero to Hero** YouTube playlist.
- Demonstrates building language models with detailed explanations of neural network components.
- Part of a broader educational effort to demystify **LLM** internals.
- Works alongside **micrograd** as a hands-on learning tool for understanding deep learning training.

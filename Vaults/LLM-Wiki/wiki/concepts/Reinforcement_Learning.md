# Reinforcement Learning

## Summary

Reinforcement Learning (RL) is a branch of machine learning in which agents learn to make decisions by interacting with an environment, receiving rewards or penalties based on their actions. It has been a significant area of research for multiple AI organizations and researchers featured in the Andrej Karpathy bio.

## Key Details

- Karpathy completed an internship at [[DeepMind]] in 2015, where he worked on deep reinforcement learning alongside **Koray Kavukcuoglu** and **Vlad Mnih**.
- During his MSc at the University of British Columbia, Karpathy worked with **Michiel van de Panne** on learning controllers for physically-simulated figures, applying machine learning to agile robotics in simulation — an application of reinforcement learning principles.
- [[DeepMind]] is well known for its foundational work in deep reinforcement learning, including breakthroughs such as AlphaGo and AlphaFold.
- RL has been applied to games, robotics, autonomous systems, and increasingly to the training of large language models for alignment and preference optimization.

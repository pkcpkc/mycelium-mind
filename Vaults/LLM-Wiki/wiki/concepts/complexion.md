# complexion

## Summary

The visible appearance, coloring, and texture of a person's skin. In visual analysis and photography, it serves as a primary descriptor for characterizing subjects, often noted alongside facial features, hair, and lighting conditions to build a complete visual profile.

## Key Details

- Refers to the natural hue and surface texture of human skin, commonly described using terms such as fair, medium, or dark.
- In photographic representation, complexion interacts directly with lighting direction and intensity, affecting contrast, shadow placement, and perceived tone.
- Frequently documented alongside other facial attributes (e.g., stubble, hairline, beard) to provide granular subject characterization.
- Can be influenced by environmental factors, age, skin health, and photographic post-processing or color grading.

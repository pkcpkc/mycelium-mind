# Large Language Models

## Summary

Large Language Models (LLMs) are AI models trained on vast text corpora that can generate human-like text, understand natural language, and perform a wide range of language tasks.

## Key Details

- Karpathy produces educational content on LLMs including "Deep Dive into LLMs" and "How I Use LLMs" tracks
- OpenAI, where Karpathy recently returned, is a leading organization in LLM research and development
- LLMs require specialized training stages including pretraining, midtraining, and instruction tuning
- Midtraining — a stage Karpathy's new team at OpenAI focuses on — is critical for model development between pretraining and instruction tuning
- Synthetic data generation plays a key role in enhancing LLM capabilities

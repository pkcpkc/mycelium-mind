# Large Language Models

## Summary

Large Language Models (LLMs) are AI models trained on massive text corpora that can generate, understand, and manipulate natural language with remarkable fluency. They have become a central technology in artificial intelligence, powering applications from conversational agents to code generation.

## Key Details

- **Andrej Karpathy** has been a prominent educator on LLMs, creating the **Zero to Hero** YouTube playlist that covers LLM internals from scratch, including building **GPT** from scratch.
- Karpathy's general audience YouTube track features deep dives into LLMs like **ChatGPT** and practical guides on using them.
- At the **Microsoft Build 2023** conference, Karpathy delivered a "State of GPT" talk discussing the trajectory of models like ChatGPT and the broader LLM ecosystem.
- Karpathy has built educational projects such as **makemore** and **micrograd** to teach LLM internals including backpropagation, activations, gradients, and batch normalization.
- The **Transformer Architecture** underpins modern large language models, enabling their ability to process and generate text at scale.
- **OpenAI**'s development of ChatGPT brought widespread public attention to the capabilities and potential of large language models.

# Google Brain

Google Brain is a deep learning research project at Google, focused on artificial intelligence and machine learning. It was one of the early major academic-style research labs within a tech company. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Karpathy's Involvement:** Andrej Karpathy completed an internship at (baby) Google Brain in 2011, working on learning-scale unsupervised learning from videos. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Google Research:** Karpathy also interned at Google Research in 2013, working on large-scale supervised learning on YouTube videos. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Significance:** Google Brain was one of the first major corporate research initiatives in deep learning and helped establish the field within industry. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

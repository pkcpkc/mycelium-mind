# Google Brain

## Summary

**Google Brain** is a research division within Google dedicated to deep learning and artificial intelligence. It has been instrumental in advancing foundational work in unsupervised learning and large-scale neural network training.

## Key Details

- **Andrej Karpathy** interned at Google Brain in 2011, where he worked on learning-scale unsupervised learning from videos.
- Google Brain has been a central hub for deep learning research within Google, influencing the broader AI research community.
- The team's work on video understanding and unsupervised representation learning contributed to advances in computer vision and multimedia analysis.
- Google Brain later evolved alongside other Google AI research groups such as **Google Research** and was closely connected to the work done at **DeepMind**.

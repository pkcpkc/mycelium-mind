# Deep Learning

## Summary

Deep Learning is a subset of machine learning using multi-layered neural networks to learn hierarchical representations from large amounts of data, enabling breakthroughs in vision, language, and other domains.

## Key Details

- Powers many modern AI systems including computer vision models, language models, and autonomous driving perception
- Karpathy's educational content on YouTube focuses heavily on deep learning fundamentals
- The field has driven advances in neural network architectures, training techniques, and hardware optimization
- Central to Karpathy's work at both OpenAI (LLMs) and Tesla (computer vision, Autopilot)
- Requires large-scale labeled datasets and significant computational resources for training

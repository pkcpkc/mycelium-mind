# Deep Learning

## Summary

Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that uses multi-layered neural networks to learn hierarchical representations of data. It became a dominant paradigm in artificial intelligence through the work of researchers such as **Geoffrey Hinton**, who influenced early adopters like **Andrej Karpathy** through his classes and reading groups at the University of Toronto.

## Key Details

- Deep learning focuses on training deep neural nets on large datasets, as stated in **Andrej Karpathy**'s research ethos.
- It encompasses architectures such as convolutional neural networks (**CNNs**) for visual recognition and recurrent neural networks (**RNNs**) for sequential data.
- Applications span computer vision, natural language processing, reinforcement learning, and large language models.
- Key training techniques include backpropagation, activations, gradients, and batch normalization.
- Frameworks such as **PyTorch** and **Torch** are widely used for implementing deep learning models.
- Educational resources like **Stanford CS231n** and the **Zero to Hero** YouTube playlist have made deep learning accessible to a broad audience.

# t-shirt

## Summary

A casual, short-sleeved garment characterized by a round neckline and the absence of a collar or buttons. It is widely recognized for its comfort, versatility, and frequent use in everyday attire, portraits, and visual media.

## Key Details

- **Construction & Material:** Typically crafted from soft, knit fabrics that offer a relaxed drape and frequently develop visible wrinkles around high-movement areas like the shoulders and collar.
- **Neckline Style:** Most commonly features a crew-neck cut, though variations such as v-necks and scoop necks are widely available.
- **Color & Appearance:** Often produced in solid, muted tones (such as charcoal or black) to serve as a neutral backdrop, but manufactured in a broad spectrum of colors and patterns.
- **Cultural Usage:** A staple in modern casual wear and portrait photography due to its understated aesthetic and ability to emphasize facial features and posture.

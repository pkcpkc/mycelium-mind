# Google Research

## Summary

**Google Research** is Google's division dedicated to advancing computer science and artificial intelligence across a broad range of topics, including large-scale supervised learning, natural language processing, and computer vision.

## Key Details

- **Andrej Karpathy** interned at Google Research in 2013, working on large-scale supervised learning applied to YouTube videos.
- During this internship, Karpathy collaborated with **Andrew Ng** and contributed to projects such as grounded compositional semantics for image captioning.
- Google Research publishes extensively in top-tier venues including ICML, ICLR, CVPR, NIPS, and TACL.
- The division has produced influential work in areas such as dense captioning, image description generation, and large-scale video classification.

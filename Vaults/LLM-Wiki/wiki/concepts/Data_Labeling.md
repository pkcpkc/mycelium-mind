# Data Labeling

## Summary

Data Labeling is the process of annotating raw data with labels or tags to create supervised training datasets for machine learning models.

## Key Details

- A critical step in the ML pipeline, especially for computer vision tasks like autonomous driving perception
- Tesla performs data labeling in-house at scale to support Autopilot training
- High-quality labeled data is essential for training accurate neural networks
- The process can be labor-intensive and requires careful attention to label consistency
- Part of the full ML pipeline overseen by Karpathy at Tesla, alongside network training and deployment

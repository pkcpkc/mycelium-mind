# Tesla

Tesla is an electric vehicle and clean energy company. Andrej Karpathy served as Director of AI at Tesla, where he led the computer vision team for Tesla Autopilot and briefly for Tesla Optimus. His team handled all in-house data labeling, neural network training and deployment on Tesla's custom inference chip.

## Key People

- [[Andrej Karpathy]] - Director of AI, led computer vision team
- [[Jensen Huang]] - NVIDIA GTC Keynote collaboration (2015)

## Timeline

- **2017**: Andrej Karpathy became Director of AI at Tesla.
- **2021**: Tesla AI Day presented work on Full Self-Driving.

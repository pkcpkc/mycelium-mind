# Tesla

## Summary

Tesla is an electric vehicle and clean energy company where Andrej Karpathy served as Director of AI. In this role, he led the computer vision team responsible for developing Tesla's Autopilot system and briefly oversaw the Tesla Optimus humanoid robot program.

## Key Details

- Karpathy joined Tesla as Director of AI, where he led the computer vision team for **Autopilot** and briefly **Tesla Optimus**.
- His team handled in-house data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on Tesla's custom inference chip.
- The Autopilot system uses deep learning for real-time visual perception to improve driving safety, with the long-term goal of achieving Full Self-Driving at scale.
- Karpathy delivered notable presentations including **Tesla AI Day** in 2021 and **AI for Full Self-Driving** at CVPR 2021 and ScaledML 2020.
- Karpathy previously worked at [[OpenAI]], [[Google Brain]], and [[Google Research]] before joining Tesla.
- Tesla's AI efforts under Karpathy demonstrated practical large-scale deployment of [[Deep Learning]] and [[Computer Vision]] models in production autonomous driving systems.

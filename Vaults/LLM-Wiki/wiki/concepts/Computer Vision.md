# Computer Vision

Computer vision is a field of artificial intelligence that enables computers to derive meaningful information from digital images, videos, and other visual inputs. It has been a major focus of research at institutions like Stanford's Vision Lab and companies like Tesla and OpenAI.

## Key Milestones

- **ImageNet Challenge** — Large-scale visual recognition competition where [[Andrej Karpathy]] competed against an early ConvNet, gaining fame as the "reference human" for ImageNet.
- **CS231n** — Stanford's deep learning course for visual recognition, created and taught by [[Andrej Karpathy]], growing from 150 students in 2015 to 750 students in 2017.
- **Dense Captioning** — Advancements in image captioning including [[NeuralTalk2]] and DenseCap by [[Justin Johnson]], [[Andrej Karpathy]], and [[Li Fei-Fei]].

## Major Projects

- **Tesla Autopilot** — Computer vision system for autonomous driving led by [[Andrej Karpathy]] at Tesla.
- **ConvNetJS** — JavaScript-based deep learning library for CNNs, created by Andrej Karpathy.
- **t-SNEJS** — t-SNE visualization tool from the ConvNetJS ecosystem.

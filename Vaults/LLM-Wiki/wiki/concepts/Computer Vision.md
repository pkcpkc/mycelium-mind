# Computer Vision

Computer vision is a field of artificial intelligence that enables computers to derive meaningful information from images and videos. Andrej Karpathy led the computer vision team at Tesla for Autopilot and conducted pioneering research in image captioning and visual recognition during his PhD.

## Key People

- [[Andrej Karpathy]] - Led Tesla Autopilot computer vision team, image captioning researcher
- [[Fei-Fei Li]] - Collaborator on visual recognition research
- [[Justin Johnson]] - Collaborator on dense captioning

## Key Projects

- **Tesla Autopilot** - In-house computer vision for autonomous driving
- **ImageNet Competition** - Karpathy competed against early ConvNet on ImageNet classification
- **DenseCap** - Fully convolutional localization networks for dense captioning
- **neuraltalk2** - Early image captioning project

## Timeline

- **2014-2015**: Karpathy's visual recognition research at Stanford.
- **2016**: DenseCap oral presentation at CVPR.
- **2017+**: Karpathy led computer vision team at Tesla.

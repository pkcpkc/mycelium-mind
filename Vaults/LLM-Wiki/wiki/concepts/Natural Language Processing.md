# Natural Language Processing

## Summary

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a core discipline within artificial intelligence dedicated to the interaction between computers and human language. It encompasses computational techniques for parsing, understanding, generating, and transforming textual and spoken data. In modern AI research, NLP has evolved through deep learning methodologies, forming the architectural and training foundation for Large Language Models and advanced sequence modeling architectures.

## Key Details

- Serves as a foundational pillar alongside [[Deep Learning]], [[Computer Vision]], and [[Reinforcement Learning]] in contemporary AI systems.
- Directly underpins the development, training, and synthetic data generation pipelines of [[Large Language Models]].
- Historically advanced through recurrent neural networks and attention mechanisms, with early educational open-source projects like [[char-rnn]] and [[neuraltalk2]] demonstrating its application in text generation and visual-semantic alignment.
- Frequently intersects with [[Stanford University]] curriculum and AI research initiatives, particularly in sequence modeling, language-aware neural architectures, and educational tooling like [[micrograd]].

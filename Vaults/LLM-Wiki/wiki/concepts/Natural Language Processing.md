# Natural Language Processing

Natural language processing (NLP) is a field of artificial intelligence focused on enabling computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language. It intersects with computer vision in multimodal tasks like image captioning.

## Key Work

- [[Andrej Karpathy]]'s PhD research at Stanford focused on applications of neural networks in NLP, including image-sentence mapping.
- Grounded Compositional Semantics for Finding and Describing Images with Sentences (TACL 2013) by [[Richard Socher]], [[Andrej Karpathy]], [[Quoc V. Le]], [[Christopher D. Manning]], and [[Andrew Ng]].
- Connecting Images and Natural Language (PhD thesis, 2016) by [[Andrej Karpathy]].

# DeepMind

## Summary

**DeepMind** is an artificial intelligence research laboratory known for its breakthroughs in deep reinforcement learning. It was acquired by **Google** and has been a significant hub for AI research, attracting interns and collaborators from across the academic and industry community.

## Key Details

- **Andrej Karpathy** completed an internship at DeepMind in 2015, working on deep reinforcement learning alongside **Koray Kavukcuoglu** and **Vlad Mnih**.
- The lab is recognized for advancing the state of the art in deep reinforcement learning, a field combining deep neural networks with reward-based training strategies.
- DeepMind has produced notable research and systems that demonstrate the power of learning agents from raw sensory input.

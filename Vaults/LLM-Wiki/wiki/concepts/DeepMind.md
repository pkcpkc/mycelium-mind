# DeepMind

DeepMind is a British artificial intelligence company founded in 2010 and acquired by Google in 2014. It is known for its work in deep reinforcement learning and AI research. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Karpathy's Involvement:** Andrej Karpathy interned at DeepMind in 2015, working on the deep reinforcement learning team alongside Koray Kavukcuoglu and Vlad Mnih. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Notable Achievements:** DeepMind is known for AlphaGo, AlphaZero, and various breakthroughs in reinforcement learning. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

# Y Combinator

## Summary

Y Combinator (YC) is the world's most prominent startup accelerator, founded in 2005 by Paul Graham. Based in Silicon Valley, YC funds early-stage startups through its biannual programs, providing seed funding, mentorship, and access to a vast network of founders and investors. It has become synonymous with startup acceleration and has backed companies like Airbnb, Stripe, Dropbox, and Twitch.

## Key Details

- **Founded**: 2005 by Paul Graham, Jessica Livingston, Bob Morris, and Trevor Blackwell
- **Model**: Biannual batch programs providing seed investment, mentorship, and demo day presentations to investors
- **Notable Alumni**: Airbnb, Stripe, Dropbox, Twitch, Reddit, Square, Coinbase, and hundreds of other successful startups
- **AI Focus**: YC has expanded its focus to include AI-focused programs, such as the YC AI Startup School, where prominent figures like Andrej Karpathy have participated as instructors
- **Influence**: Widely regarded as the most impactful startup accelerator globally, with a distinctive culture and methodology for building companies
- **Culture**: Known for its emphasis on rapid iteration, customer discovery, and data-driven decision-making

# arxiv-sanity

## Summary

arxiv-sanity is an open-source tool built by **Andrej Karpathy** for discovering, sorting, and recommending research papers from Arxiv, making it easier to navigate the vast volume of academic publications.

## Key Details

- Allows users to discover relevant research papers across various domains.
- Provides sorting and recommendation features to help researchers find papers of interest.
- Built by **Andrej Karpathy** as part of his collection of open-source projects.
- Helps address the challenge of information overload in academic publishing.

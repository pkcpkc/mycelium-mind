# arxiv-sanity

arxiv-sanity is a tool for discovering and organizing research papers from Arxiv. It allows researchers to search, sort by similarity, view recent or popular papers, and receive recommendations. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Deployment:** The service is deployed live at arxiv-sanity.com. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Creator:** Andrej Karpathy built arxiv-sanity as part of his interest in meta-research tools. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Related Projects:** Karpathy's interest in research discovery tools led to other projects including pretty NIPS 2020 papers, research lei, scholarotopus, and biomed-sanity. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

**Follow-up:** Karpathy later created arxiv-sanity-lite, a from-scratch rewrite that he considers much better than the original. [Source: Andrej_Karpathy.md]

# Timeline

Chronological timeline of all events mentioned in the LLM-Wiki vault.

## 2005–2009

- Andrej Karpathy completes undergraduate studies at [[University of Toronto]], earning a BSc with double majors in computer science and physics, minor in mathematics ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Karpathy attends [[Geoff Hinton]]'s classes and reading groups at the University of Toronto, sparking his interest in deep learning ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2009–2011

- Karpathy completes his MSc at [[University of British Columbia]] under [[Michiel van de Panne]], research focus: learning controllers for physically-simulated figures ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2011

- Karpathy completes an internship at (baby) [[Google Brain]], working on scale unsupervised learning from videos ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *Locomotion Skills for Simulated Quadrupeds* published at SIGGRAPH — co-authored by Karpathy, [[Stelian Coros]], [[Benjamin Jones]], [[Lionel Reveret]], and [[Michiel van de Panne]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2011–2017

- Karpathy pursues his PhD at [[Stanford University]] under [[Fei-Fei Li]], thesis: *Connecting Images and Natural Language* ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2011–2017

- Karpathy participates in the first-year rotation program at Stanford, working with [[Andrew Ng]], [[Daphne Koller]], [[Sebastian Thrun]], and [[Vladlen Koltun]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2012

- *Curriculum Learning for Motor Skills* published in AI — co-authored by Karpathy and [[Michiel van de Panne]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2013

- Karpathy completes a second internship at [[Google Research]] (2013), working on large-scale supervised learning on YouTube videos ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Karpathy completes his PhD rotation year at Stanford ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *Object Discovery in 3D scenes via Shape Analysis* published at ICRA 2013 — co-authored by Karpathy, [[Fei-Fei Li]], and [[Stephen Miller]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *Grounded Compositional Semantics for Finding and Describing Images with Sentences* published in TACL 2013 — co-authored by Karpathy, [[Richard Socher]], [[Quoc V. Le]], [[Christopher D. Manning]], and [[Andrew Ng]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2014

- *Large-Scale Video Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks* published at CVPR 2014 (Oral) — co-authored by Karpathy, [[George Toderici]], [[Sanketh Shetty]], [[Thomas Leung]], [[Rahul Sukthankar]], and [[Fei-Fei Li]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Karpathy competes against an early ConvNet on ImageNet image categorization, becoming the "reference human" for ImageNet — covered in blog post and [[Wired]] article ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *Deep Fragment Embeddings for Bidirectional Image-Sentence Mapping* published at NIPS 2014 — co-authored by Karpathy, [[Fei-Fei Li]], and [[Armand Joulin]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2015

- Karpathy joins [[OpenAI]] as a founding member and research scientist ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Karpathy completes an internship at [[DeepMind]] working on deep reinforcement learning with [[Vlad Mnih]] and [[Koray Kavukcuoglu]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks* blog post published ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments for Generating Image Descriptions* published at CVPR 2015 (Oral) — co-authored by Karpathy and [[Fei-Fei Li]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge* published in JCV 2015 — co-authored by [[Fei-Fei Li]], [[Olga Russakovsky]], [[Jia Deng]], [[Hao Su]], [[Jonathan Krause]], Karpathy, and others ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2016

- Karpathy becomes Director of AI at [[Tesla]], leading computer vision for Autopilot ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *DenseCap: Fully Convolutional Localization Networks for Dense Captioning* published at CVPR 2016 (Oral) — co-authored by Karpathy, [[Justin Johnson]], and [[Fei-Fei Li]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *Visualizing and Understanding Recurrent Networks* published at ICLR 2016 Workshop — co-authored by Karpathy, [[Justin Johnson]], and [[Fei-Fei Li]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2016–2022

- Karpathy serves as Director of AI at [[Tesla]], leading computer vision for Autopilot and briefly for Tesla Optimus; team handled in-house data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on custom inference chip ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2017

- Karpathy earns his PhD from [[Stanford University]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *Software 2.0* blog post published ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *World of Bits: An Open-Domain Platform for Web-Based Agents* published at ICML 2017 — co-authored by [[Tianlin Shi]] and Karpathy ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *PixelCNN++: A PixelCNN Implementation with Discretized Logistic Mixture Likelihood and Other Modifications* published at ICLR 2017 — co-authored by Karpathy, [[Diederik P. Kingma]], [[Xi Chen]], [[Yaroslav Bulatov]], and [[Tim Salimans]] ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2018

- *Building the Software 2.0 stack* presented at Spark-AI 2018 ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2019

- *AI for Full Self-Driving* presented at ScaledML 2020 (note: listed as 2019 event in source) ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *PyTorch at Tesla* presented at PyTorch DevCon 2019 ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2020

- *AI for Full Self-Driving* presented at CVPR 2021 (note: listed as 2020 event in source) ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2021

- *Tesla AI Day* presentation on AI for Full Self-Driving (August 2021) ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2023

- *State of GPT* keynote presentation at Microsoft Build 2023 ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2023 (Heroes of Deep Learning)

- Joint appearance with [[Andrew Ng]] in the 2017 "Heroes of Deep Learning" event ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2024

- Karpathy returns to [[OpenAI]], builds a new team working on midtraining and synthetic data generation ([source: Andrej_Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

# Timeline

Master chronological timeline of events across the LLM-Wiki vault.

---

## 2005–2009

- Andrej Karpathy earns BSc at University of Toronto — double major in computer science and physics, minor in math. First exposure to deep learning through Geoff Hinton's classes and reading groups. ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2009–2011

- Andrej Karpathy earns MSc at University of British Columbia, working with Michiel van de Panne on learning controllers for physically-simulated figures (machine-learning for agile robotics in simulation). ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2011

- Andrej Karpathy completes PhD in Computer Science at Stanford University, advised by Fei-Fei Li. Research focused on convolutional/recurrent neural networks for computer vision and NLP. Internships at Google Brain (unsupervised learning from videos) and Google Research. ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2012

- *Emergence of Object-Selective Features in Unsupervised Feature Learning* — NIPS 2012 (Adam Coates, Andrej Karpathy, Andrew Ng). ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2013

- Andrej Karpathy interns at Google Research on large-scale supervised learning on YouTube videos. ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2014

- *Large-Scale Video Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks* — CVPR 2014 (Oral) (George Toderici, Sanketh Shetty, Thomas Leung, Rahul Sukthankar, Andrej Karpathy, Li Fei-Fei). ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *Deep Fragment Embeddings for Bidirectional Image-Sentence Mapping* — NIPS 2014 (Armand Joulin, Andrej Karpathy, Li Fei-Fei). ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Andrej Karpathy publishes "What I learned from competing against a ConvNet on ImageNet" (Sep 2014). ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2015

- Andrej Karpathy becomes research scientist and founding member at OpenAI. ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Andrej Karpathy interns at DeepMind working on deep reinforcement learning with Koray Kavukcuoglu and Vlad Mnih. ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments for Generating Image Descriptions* — CVPR 2015 (Oral) (Li Fei-Fei, Andrej Karpathy). ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- *ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge* — JCV 2015 (Olga Russakovsky, Jia Deng, Hao Su, Jonathan Krause, Sanjeev Satheesh, et al., Li Fei-Fei). ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Andrej Karpathy publishes "The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks" (May 2015). ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Andrej Karpathy begins designing and teaching CS 231n at Stanford, one of the university's largest classes. ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Andrej Karpathy delivers NVIDIA GTC Keynote with Jensen Huang. ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2016

- *DenseCap: Fully Convolutional Localization Networks for Dense Captioning* — CVPR 2016 (Oral) (Justin Johnson, Li Fei-Fei, Andrej Karpathy). ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Andrej Karpathy completes PhD thesis: *Connecting Images and Natural Language*. ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Andrej Karpathy speaks at Bay Area Deep Learning School and CVPR Deep Learning Workshop. ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Andrej Karpathy speaks at RE·WORK Deep Learning Summit. ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2017

- *PixelCNN++: A PixelCNN Implementation with Discretized Logistic Mixture Likelihood and Other Modifications* — ICLR 2017 (Tim Salimacs, Xi Chen, Diederik P. Kingma, Yaroslav Bulatov, Andrej Karpathy). ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Andrej Karpathy publishes "Software 2.0" blog post (Nov 2017). ([Andrej Karpathy personal website](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

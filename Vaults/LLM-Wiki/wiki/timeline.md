# LLM-Wiki Timeline

A chronological timeline of events mentioned across the wiki vault.

## 1885

- Stanford University founded by Leland Stanford and Jane Stanford in memory of their son, Leland Stanford Jr. ([Stanford.md](assets/2026-06-20/Stanford.md))

## 2010

- DeepMind, a British AI company known for deep reinforcement learning, is founded. ([DeepMind.md](assets/2026-06-20/DeepMind.md))
- The ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC) begins, becoming the standard benchmark for evaluating computer vision systems. ([ImageNet.md](assets/2026-06-20/ImageNet.md))

## 2011

- Andrej Karpathy completes his MSc at the University of British Columbia (2009–2011), working with Michiel van de Panne on learning controllers for physically-simulated figures. ([Andrej Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Andrej Karpathy completes an internship at Google Brain, working on learning-scale unsupervised learning from videos. ([Google_Brain.md](assets/2026-06-20/Google_Brain.md))

## 2013

- Andrej Karpathy interns at Google Research, working on large-scale supervised learning on YouTube videos. ([Google_Brain.md](assets/2026-06-20/Google_Brain.md))

## 2015

- Andrej Karpathy interns at DeepMind, working on the deep reinforcement learning team alongside Koray Kavukcuoglu and Vlad Mnih. ([DeepMind.md](assets/2026-06-20/DeepMind.md))
- OpenAI is founded as a non-profit organization in December 2015. Founding members include Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Greg Brockman, Ilya Sutskever, and Andrej Karpathy. ([OpenAI.md](assets/2026-06-20/OpenAI.md))
- Andrej Karpathy joins OpenAI as a research scientist and founding member. ([OpenAI.md](assets/2026-06-20/OpenAI.md))
- Andrej Karpathy begins his PhD at Stanford University under the advisement of Fei-Fei Li, focusing on convolutional and recurrent neural networks applied to computer vision and natural language processing. ([Andrej Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Andrej Karpathy becomes the primary instructor for CS 231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition at Stanford, which enrolls 150 students in its first year. ([CS_231n.md](assets/2026-06-20/CS_231n.md))
- The ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge paper (ILSVRC 2015) is published, authored by Olga Russakovsky, Jia Deng, Hao Su, and Andrej Karpathy among others. ([ImageNet.md](assets/2026-06-20/ImageNet.md))

## 2016

- Andrej Karpathy completes his PhD thesis, "Connecting Images and Natural Language," at Stanford University. ([Andrej Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- Karpathy's work DenseCap is accepted as a CVPR 2016 Oral presentation. ([Andrej Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- CS 231n at Stanford grows to 330 students. ([CS_231n.md](assets/2026-06-20/CS_231n.md))

## 2017

- CS 231n at Stanford reaches 750 students, becoming one of the largest classes at the university. ([CS_231n.md](assets/2026-06-20/CS_231n.md))
- World of Bits paper is published at ICML 2017, co-authored by Tianlin Shi, Andrej Karpathy, Linxi Fan, Jonathan Hernandez, and Percy Liang. ([Andrej Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))
- PixelCNN++ paper is published at ICLR 2017. ([Andrej Karpathy.md](assets/2026-06-20/Andrej_Karpathy.md))

## 2019

- OpenAI transitions from a non-profit to a capped-profit structure. ([OpenAI.md](assets/2026-06-20/OpenAI.md))
- Tesla Autonomy Day takes place. ([Tesla_Autopilot.md](assets/2026-06-20/Tesla_Autopilot.md))

## 2020

- AI for Full Self-Driving presentation at ScaledML. ([Tesla_Autopilot.md](assets/2026-06-20/Tesla_Autopilot.md))

## 2021

- Tesla AI Day takes place in August 2021, showcasing Autopilot and Optimus progress. ([Tesla_Autopilot.md](assets/2026-06-20/Tesla_Autopilot.md))
- AI for Full Self-Driving presentation at CVPR 2021. ([Tesla_Autopilot.md](assets/2026-06-20/Tesla_Autopilot.md))

## 2023

- OpenAI releases ChatGPT, achieving rapid mainstream adoption. ([OpenAI.md](assets/2026-06-20/OpenAI.md))
